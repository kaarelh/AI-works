# One-tape definition of SFI: edit log (2026-10-13)

Agreed change (approved by the author): SFI's prior $M_F$ is the law of **one** random object. A universal machine reads **one** infinite sequence of fair coin flips. Each query is a separate run that reads the sequence from its first bit. Splitting the tape into a "program" and "coins" is only a way of reading that one tape. It gives the mixture formula of `thm:setting:universal` and does not add a second source of randomness.

Binding notes followed: DECISIONS.md and REFRAME.md. The edits make no running-text reference to the notes, change no mathematics, rename no labels, and keep every `\status`/`\src` call. Paper 1's numbering is frozen. A script compared the multiset of `\begin{...}`, `\section`, `\subsection`, `\label{...}`, `\status{`, `\src{` and `\footnote` tokens in every `paper/sections/*.tex` before and after the edits: the two are identical in every file.

## Check of the W passage (change 11) against the cited statements

- **"the tape only selects a deterministic program, its first self-delimiting block".** `def:versions:variants` gives $W(D)=\sum_{p\in\mathrm{dom}V}2^{-|p|}\mathbf 1[\varphi_{V(p)}\supseteq D]$. Since $\mathrm{dom}V$ is prefix-free, this is $\mathrm{Unif}\{\omega:\omega$ begins with some $p\in\mathrm{dom}V$ with $\varphi_{V(p)}\supseteq D\}$ for $D\neq\emptyset$. `rem:versions:Wdelim` says the same: $W$ is the containment functional of "draw $p$ with probability $2^{-|p|}$, output $\varphi_{V(p)}$". At $D=\emptyset$, $W(\emptyset)<1$, which the unchanged next sentence states.
- **"Always $W\le2^{O(1)}M_F$".** `thm:versions:chain` gives $W\le2^{O(1)}M_{\rm prod}\le2^{O(1)}M_F$ for $D\ne\emptyset$, and $W(\emptyset)<1=M_F(\emptyset)$.
- **"$\log(M_F/W)\le\min\{K^{\rm pad}(L_{1/2}(M_F,D)),K(\mathrm{dom}D)\}+O(1)$".**
  - The first term is `prop:versions:tape`(a), last sentence.
  - The second term follows from `thm:versions:chain`: $M_F\le2^{O(1)}M_w\le2^{K(\mathrm{dom}D)+O(1)}2^{-K_F(D)}\le2^{K(\mathrm{dom}D)+O(1)}W(D)$.
  - The same minimum already appears in `tab:versions:compare`, row $M_{\rm prod}$ vs $M_F$, and in `q:versions:open`.
- **The gloss on $L_{1/2}$** paraphrases the definitions of $\nu^{(L)}$ and $L_{1/2}$ in `prop:versions:tape`(a).
- **"unbounded".** `prop:versions:Wgap`: "$M_{\rm prod}/W$ and $M_F/W$ are unbounded".
- **"$\le K^{\rm pad}(2^{d+1})+O(1)$ on two-point binary data over $d$-bit inputs".** By `prop:versions:tape`(b) with $|D|=2$, $W(D)\ge2^{-2-K^{\rm pad}(2^{d+1})-c'}$, and $M_F(D)\le1$. The same bound is stated after `prop:versions:Wgap` and in `rem:versions:Wrate`.
- **"$\le2\log(h(d)+2)+O_h(1)$ for every computable, nondecreasing, unbounded $h$".**
  - `rem:versions:Wrate` states this verbatim. Its proof applies `prop:versions:tape`(c) with $g(N):=h(\max(0,\lfloor\log N\rfloor-1))$.
  - The task's wording ("grows more slowly than $2\log h(d)$") was replaced by this exact statement, which is what the cited results prove.
