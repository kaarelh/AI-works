\section{How the claims were checked}\label{app:verification}

This appendix is an internal record of how the results were established. Its vocabulary (``track'', ``referee'', the hypotheses H1--H10) refers to the research process, whose files are in \texttt{solomonoff-function-induction/research/} of the accompanying repository.

\subsection{Process}\label{app:verification:process}

\paragraph{Roles.} Every role below was played by a separate instance of Claude (Anthropic); no human checked the mathematics. H\"anni's request \citep{hanni2026request}, his notes \citep{hanni2026notes} and the slides \citep{vaintrobhanni2026nnbayes} were the inputs. A preliminary analysis turned them into ten working hypotheses, H1--H10 (\cref{tab:intro:verdicts}), to be proved, corrected or refuted, never assumed.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Five research tracks}, each one instance: \emph{foundations} (H1, H2, H7, invariance, the later request on versions; \cref{sec:setting,sec:versions,sec:learn}), \emph{history} (H3, H4; \cref{sec:hist}), \emph{examples} (H5, H6, positive examples, H\"anni's out-of-distribution examples; \cref{sec:ex,sec:ood:examples}), \emph{context} (H8, H10, literature, extras; \cref{sec:learn:curves,sec:ood,sec:comp,sec:nn,sec:related}) and \emph{steeper} (H9; the companion paper, summarised in \cref{sec:steep}). Each wrote notes with a status on every claim (proved with a full proof, computed with a seeded script and saved output, known with a reference, conjecture, proof sketch, refuted with its counterexample) and its own check scripts.
\item \emph{An adversarial referee} per track, a separate instance that had not written the notes. Each checked every proof marked proved line by line, re-ran the track's scripts on scratch copies, wrote independent code, searched the literature, and reported fatal, major and minor issues. The examples and steeper tracks had two referee runs; the foundations and context referees each built on an earlier pass whose scripts they re-ran.
\item \emph{A revision} by each track, answering every referee item, re-running all scripts and comparing outputs byte for byte, and ending with a verification log and a cross-track consistency section. These final notes (\texttt{notes-final.md}) are the only sources of claims for the paper; they were not refereed a second time.
\item \emph{A planning editor} wrote the notation, the outline and a ledger of every result with its status and source, and resolved 25 cross-track conflicts (\cref{app:verification:conflicts}). \emph{Section writers}, one per group of sections, wrote the sections from the final notes, checking proofs as they adapted them. \emph{A framing editor} wrote the introduction, the discussion and this appendix, and ran the full build.
\end{enumerate}
No referee found a fatal issue in any track. The major issues they found (4 in foundations, 6 in history, 2 and 3 in the two examples runs, 6 in context, 3 and 4 in the two steeper runs) concerned what the notes claimed or missed, not false proofs; all were accepted.

\paragraph{Scripts.} \Cref{tab:verification:scripts} lists the check scripts behind the computed statements. They check finite combinatorial, analytic or information-theoretic steps and finite analogues (mixtures over finite classes); statements about $K$, $M$, $\MF$, $\MH$ and other uncomputable objects are proved by hand. Environment: Python 3.11.15, numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, mpmath 1.3.0.

\subsection{Literature}\label{app:verification:literature}

\item \emph{Length.} The main text (pages 3--52 in the current build) runs well beyond its target of about 38 pages, and the appendices (pages 57--103, including this one) beyond their target of about 35: the writers kept every ledger result with its statement and proof idea, moving secondary items to the appendices (33 items in all). Further cuts would drop proofs or results.
\item \emph{Conversion to arXiv form.} The status tags, source notes, this appendix, process vocabulary in a few sentences of the body (for example references to check scripts by name, ``H\"anni's request'') and the hedges written as ``unverified'' are for the conversion pass, which must also decide how to cite the request \citep{hanni2026request} once H\"anni is the sole author.
\item \emph{Status vocabulary.} Some items carry statuses outside the fixed set, as the ledger gives them: ``remark'', ``remark (cited items)'', ``conditional (on LHG transfer)'', ``proved, given \ldots''.
\item \emph{Open questions as paragraphs.} Their labels sit on paragraphs, so a cross-reference prints the number of the enclosing subsection.
\item \emph{Notation.} NOTATION.md still writes the shared-coin law as $F(x)=\omega_{|x|}$; this paper uses $F(x)=\omega_{|x|+1}$ (\cref{tab:verification:corrections}). The companion paper should use the same indexing.
\item \emph{Shared bibliography.} The two papers' \texttt{bib/core.bib} files are meant to be identical; this paper's copy now names H\"anni as author of both companion papers, and the companion's copy needs the same change.
\end{itemize}
