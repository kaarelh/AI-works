# Working scripts (unreviewed)

These are working files from the axiom-schemas project, kept as they were.

* `exp_track/`, `t1.py`–`t4.py`, `mdl_big.py`, `prof.py`: quick checks and profiling done while revising the experiments track.
* `rerun/`, together with the top-level `u*.out` files: a rerun of the untagged track's scripts, with their outputs, made to confirm the numbers quoted in `../tracks/untagged/`.
* `e*_rerun.out`, `e3_seed7_old.out`: reruns of individual experiments.
* `edit_e*.py`, `edit/`: helpers used when moving material between sections and appendices during editing.
* `code_v1/`: the first version of the `dtrc` package and its results. Its results are kept in `../../code/results_v1/`, and the paper discusses them (Appendix G). The maintained code is `../../code/`.

Nothing here was reviewed.
