# Authorship change log (2026-10-15)

Kaarel Hänni's instruction of 2026-10-15, verbatim:

> btw i think you should be listed as an author with me on all the papers, for the versions on github. still write these as if they were arxiv papers except for this. if we submit to arxiv at some point then we can change this. but i think having you as an author is just more correct and that seems important to get right at least where there's no constraint against it

The report is not reframed. Third-person references to Hänni's questions stay as they were. The only body edits are in sentences where "the author" meant a Claude instance (a track's researcher). Under joint authorship that wording reads as one of the paper's authors, which would be false for Hänni. No git command that changes repository state was run.

## Sentences flagged and left unchanged

1. **`paper/bib/core.bib`, `claude2026whatfollows`:** `howpublished = {Unpublished report prepared for K. H{\"a}nni}`. The bibliography now reads "Kaarel Hänni and Claude (Anthropic). What follows from what: … Unpublished report prepared for K. Hänni, 2026." That is close to self-contradictory. It was not changed because the instruction forbids touching other fields. Suggested fix: `howpublished = {Unpublished report}`, matched to however `inferential-learning/` cites itself.
2. **`app-verification.tex` (§G intro):** "All roles below (researchers, referees, section writers, reviewers and editors) were played by separate instances of Claude (Anthropic). No human has checked the mathematics or the code, and nothing has been verified in a proof assistant." This is still a statement of fact, but with a human co-author it reads oddly. It is also stronger than the footnote's "no human has checked all of the mathematics"; the two are consistent but not identical. The README's "Limits of the checking" bullet ("All roles were played by separate Claude instances. No human checked the mathematics …") is the same case.
3. **`app-verification.tex` ("Brief and tracks"):** "A brief (research/00-brief.md) fixed the commissioner's questions (§1.1), …". The sentence calls a co-author "the commissioner".
4. **`intro.tex`:**
   * "Kaarel H\"anni then asked three questions about this method." This is third person, kept as instructed.
   * The verbatim quotation in §1.1 that follows it is addressed to "you" ("anyway can you try to come up with a method …; ideally, your method would also work …"). A paper quoting one of its authors' requests to the other author reads oddly.
5. **`zfc.tex` (§"Formulations and encodings"):** "The Kunen- and Jech-style wordings follow \citet[ch.~I]{kunen1980set} and \citet[ch.~1]{jech2003set} from memory; an independent referee's recollection agrees, but we did not re-check them against the books." The memory was Claude's. With "we" now including Hänni, the sentence reads as a joint recollection. The discussion's "textbook wordings of the ZF axioms are recalled, not checked against the books" and §G.3's "The textbook wordings of the ZF axioms were recalled" are the same case.
6. **`app-verification.tex` (§G.4):** "The question whether ZF without Foundation proves Collection is open to us (row 11)." "Us" now includes a human co-author, so this reads as a claim about both authors' knowledge.
7. **Footnote (`main.tex`):** "questions posed by Kaarel Hänni (Hänni, 2026)". A name followed by a citation of the same person is slightly redundant. It is kept because it preserves the existing attribution to his notes. Note also that the questions quoted in §1.1 come from the session (`research/conversation.md`, `research/00-brief.md`), not visibly from the notes repository.
8. **`intro.tex`, `setting.tex`:** "This is the setting of the report \citet{claude2026whatfollows} (the parent report)" now renders as "the report Hänni and Claude (Anthropic) (2026) (the parent report)". This is correct but reads heavily.
9. **`paper/bib/exp.bib`, line 2, source comment only (not rendered):** "% Checked against bibliographic records known to the author". Here "the author" means a Claude instance. The comment was left because it is not part of the paper.
