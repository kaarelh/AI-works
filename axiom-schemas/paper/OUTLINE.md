# Outline: "Learning Axioms and Axiom Schemas from Their Instances"

Audience: a mathematically and philosophically literate researcher (logic, learning theory). Standalone: define everything; cite the parent report \citep{claude2026whatfollows} only for background and for the cautious-verifier framework, and restate what is used.

Sources (final, refereed research records — the ONLY sources of claims):
* `research/00-brief.md` (questions, conventions)
* `research/tracks/single/notes-final.md` (+ referee.md): general one-template theory (Thms A–H)
* `research/tracks/cases/notes-final.md` (+ referee.md): Q1 (universal axioms from instances) and Q2 (ZF schemas)
* `research/tracks/untagged/notes-final.md` (+ referee.md): many schemas without labels, DTRC theory, natural examples
* `research/tracks/experiments/notes-final.md` (+ referee.md) and `code/results/*.md`: implementation and experiments
* `research/prior/induction/*-notes.md` and `*-referee.md` (annotated, raw-impossibility, second-order) and `research/prior/pa-untagged/`: PA induction results (first-order impossibility, determinate learner, Sub/IndEq encodings, slot counting for PA)
* Verification records: each track's `referee.md` and the Verification log at the end of each `notes-final.md`.

Page targets (11pt): main text ≈ 40 pages, appendices ≈ 30 pages.
