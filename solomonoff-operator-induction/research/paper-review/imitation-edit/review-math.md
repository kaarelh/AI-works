| M1 | minor | `imit.tex` l. 28, paragraph after `def:imit:setting` | "when the input carries information about the cell, hypotheses that model $\beta$ predict better than the person" is false as stated. What is needed is information about the *coin* $\beta(w)$, i.e. $P^*\ne\nu_H$ on a set of positive $\bar\mu$-mass (`robust` Prop 4.2(b), A-m10).
