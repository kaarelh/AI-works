\emph{This appendix records how the draft was prepared and checked, in the vocabulary of that process. \subsection{Preparation}\label{app:verification:prep}

Every role was played by a separate instance of Claude (Anthropic); no human has checked the mathematics. A research \emph{track} on steeper simplicity started from H\"anni's grokking note, the LessWrong thread and the earlier formalisation T5 (\citealp[section ``Simplicity and normativity from imitation'']{claude2026whatfollows}), re-derived every T5 result it uses rather than copying it, and wrote notes with full proofs and seeded check scripts (\texttt{tracks/steeper/notes.md}, \texttt{checks/c1}--\texttt{c5}). Two adversarial \emph{referees}, each a fresh instance that formed its own view before reading earlier reports, checked every proof line by line and wrote independent code (\texttt{referee\_code/r1}--\texttt{r6} on 2026-10-11, \texttt{q1}--\texttt{q4} on 2026-10-12). After each report the track revised its notes (\texttt{notes-final-run1.md}, then \texttt{notes-final.md}), answered every item in a table, added checks \texttt{c6} and \texttt{c7}, re-ran all scripts, and closed with a verification log. A planning editor then wrote the claims ledger (\texttt{paper-steeper/CLAIMS.md}), the notation shared with the companion paper and the outline; two section writers wrote \cref{sec:setup,sec:absorb,sec:teacher,sec:limits,sec:split,sec:algstat,sec:related} and their appendices from \texttt{notes-final.md} and the ledger only; a framing editor wrote the abstract, the introduction, the discussion and this appendix, and ran the build. The results of the companion paper used here were established in the same way by four other tracks (foundations, history, examples, context) and are cited by name.

\subsection{Status of the claims}\label{app:verification:status}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Proved:} every theorem, proposition, lemma and corollary, with a full proof in the main text or the appendices; none is a proof sketch. Results of the companion paper are cited by name.
\item \emph{Computed:} every numerical statement, from the scripts above. Numbers with machine constant $\kappa=0$ are illustrations, not results about a reference machine; rows with $\kappa=30$ show how the bounds degrade. \Cref{ex:split:noisyparity} is an illustration only.
\item \emph{Known:} the standard facts (F1)--(F5), Sion's minimax theorem, Hoeffding's bound without replacement, Ville's inequality, the martingale behind Kakutani's dichotomy, Solomonoff's bound, Parseval list decoding, the notions of algorithmic statistics, proper scoring rules, and the literature of \cref{sec:related}.
\item \emph{Conjectures:} none. Open problems are collected in \cref{sec:discussion:open}.
\item \emph{Refuted:} rows 1--3 of the table above.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Open issues of this draft}\label{app:verification:open}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Length.} \Cref{sec:intro,sec:setup,sec:absorb,sec:teacher,sec:limits,sec:split,sec:algstat,sec:related,sec:discussion} run about $30$ pages against a target of $18$--$22$: about $25$ for \cref{sec:setup,sec:absorb,sec:teacher,sec:limits,sec:split,sec:algstat,sec:related} (budget $18.5$), $3$ for the introduction with its table (budget $1.5$) and $2$ for the discussion (budget $1$). \Cref{app:setup,app:absorb,app:teacher,app:limits,app:split,app:algstat,app:related} run about $22$ pages (target about $20$). Every body section exceeds its budget; the writers kept every ledger item with its statement. Candidates for the appendices are \cref{ex:split:noisyparity,rem:split:siprograms,prop:absorb:inputs} and the secondary remarks of \cref{sec:limits,sec:related}.
\item \emph{Status vocabulary.} Some \texttt{\textbackslash status} values are outside the fixed vocabulary (``remark'', ``remark; cites proved results'', ``computed; illustration'', ``proved; a natural reading refuted'' and compound forms); the conversion pass must map them, for example remarks to no tag and illustrations to computed.
\item \emph{Rounding.} The scripts print the upper bounds of \cref{tab:teacher:numbers} rounded to nearest, so their last digit is not guaranteed to be on the safe side.
\item \emph{Bibliography.} In \texttt{bib/core.bib}, which is shared with the companion paper, the entry \texttt{hanni2026\allowbreak request} calls H\"anni's note a ``personal communication to the author'' titled ``Request for a paper'' and points to a file of the preparation process, which fits neither sole authorship nor the formal register. The entry \texttt{hanni2026lesswrong} lists the thread's second participant as an author, so every citation of H\"anni's own comments prints both names; since only H\"anni's comments are cited, the entry could name him alone.
\item \emph{Cross-paper numbers.} Results of the companion paper are cited by name; theorem numbers can be added once both papers are final.
\end{itemize}
