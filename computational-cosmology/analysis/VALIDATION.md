## Mathematical review

- Strict finite-time reception boundaries and an explicit distinction between comoving workers and bound systems.

- Separate checks for collapse, turnaround, orbital stability, and depletion; geometric room alone does not certify a long-lived reserve.
- Net incoming/outgoing energy and entropy in the thermal-channel calculation, with an explicit channel convention.
- Finite-duration step costs and memory maintenance combined in one optimization; every execution prefix and the complete controller must be accounted for.
- Exact checkpoint counts for the last partial block, plus the distinction between input-output simulation and preservation of an internal experiential history.
- A closed, initially programmed controller assumption for the quantum target-counting bound; instantaneous storage does not bound lifetime streamed advice.

Exploratory review notes remain on the working branch. The report and its reproduced numerical results state the final conclusions.
