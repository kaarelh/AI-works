# References of the note, with how each was checked

This file lists every work cited in `note.md` (Sections 0 to 13, with Section 12 as in `research/sections/related-work-final.md`), in alphabetical order. The short list at the end of the note has the same entries, one line each. For each entry the file says where the work is cited, how its bibliographic data were checked, and where the statement that the note uses was read.

**How to read an entry.**

* *Cited in* gives the sections of the note. "By name" means that the note names the author there without a year.
* *Data* says how the bibliographic data were checked: against the arXiv record ("arXiv record"), against the page of the Proceedings of Machine Learning Research ("PMLR page"), from the header or the reference list of a paper that was read ("header of …", "reference list of …"), or from web-search results ("search"). A field that could not be checked is named as not verified.
* *Statement* says where the statement used in the note was read, and by whom. The readers are named by their role in this project: the *prover*, the *first verifier* (line-by-line report, `verification/KEY-check.md`), the *second verifier* (adversarial report, `verification/KEY-attack.md`) and the *editor* of one of the eight parts KEY (`proofs/KEY.md`, last section and literature section); the *lead* (`research/literature-checks.md`); the *author of the draft of Section 12* (`research/sections/related-work.md`); a *digest* (one of the background files `research/understand/*.md`, whose authors read many sources and say so entry by entry). These records were taken over as they stand.
* *This session* means: read by the editor of Section 12 on 16 November 2026, in the text extracted with `pdftotext` from the PDF on arxiv.org or proceedings.mlr.press, at the place named. Only those two hosts could be opened. Journal sites, the NeurIPS proceedings, DBLP, Crossref, the Alignment Forum and university pages did not resolve; for works that are only there, the data rest on search results and on reference lists, and the statement on a secondary source or on memory, as the entry says.
* Two marks: **[named]** for a work that the note does not cite with a year but whose author it names for a method or an inequality; **[restatement]** for a work that is cited only here, as the place where a statement of another entry was read.

Inequalities that the note names after their authors and uses as textbook facts (Pinsker, Markov, Hölder, Chernoff) have no entry. The citations were extracted from `note.md` as assembled on 16 November 2026 and compared again with the section sources as they stood later that day; `verification/referee-references.md` describes the check.

No statement in this file is stronger than its source: "not opened" means that nobody in this project read the work.
