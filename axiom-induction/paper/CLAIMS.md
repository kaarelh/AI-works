**C1. # Answers to the question

Revised after the review of the paper. The paper's introduction (`sec:intro`) gives the binding short answer of `research/paper-review/edits/DECISIONS.md` §8.2 and the answers A1–A7 below, in the canonical wordings of DECISIONS §9; the discussion (`sec:disc:good`) states the good version once. The earlier answers A1–A9 of this ledger overstated several results (review issues C-01–C-15) and are replaced.

* **A1. A good version exists, in a precise and limited sense.** Finite sets of DT° templates, a proper prefix-code prior, Dirichlet weights; a derivation grammar with a data-independent normaliser, which gives the size principle that Hänni's scores lack (`def:model:prior`, `lem:model:kraft`, `def:model:lone`, `lem:model:size`, `prop:model:whichsize`). The likelihood is a computable real whose positivity is undecidable; the citation likelihood, its Dirichlet sum and the experiments' two-step chain are computed exactly over finite pools (`prop:model:compute`, `prop:exp:exact`). The recommended inducer adds a model of reported theorems and of mistakes and outputs (Bel, Pl) (`sec:disc:good`).
* **A2. ∀xφ: prediction confirmed, derivability not.** In every comparison analysed (`thm:univ:B`) closed instances never make ∀xφ more probable than φ(z): the odds stay at the prior odds (closure reading, $\Lsel$, scores), fall by a constant factor per datum (derivation likelihoods in $\Cmin$; 0.23 at c = 0.3 under $\Lone$, quantifier-free φ), or drop to zero (strict citation); exceptions are non-logical weight effects under $\Lsel$ with a background (`prop:univ:B2`); with ∧-rules open (`rem:univ:detour`). "All future data are instances" → 1 (`thm:univ:confirm`); with data from a model generator "the axioms prove ∀xφ" tends to the prior share of provers in the generator class (0.03–0.48 for 0+x=x) or to 0 (`thm:univ:omega`, `tab:univ:share`); with unmodelled selection the limit is class-dependent (`prop:univ:noguard`, `prop:univ:sentences`); one parameter instance or one entailing quantified datum licenses ∀xφ (`prop:univ:open`, `prop:univ:quant`).
* **A3. Templates.** They block (the acceptance part of) Hänni's schema (`prop:time:notemplate`) but, over PA, not the collapse for Σₙ-sound assigners on Σₙ sentences (`prop:time:collapse`); open for merely consistent assigners. They bring φ(z) into the class. A schema and its root split tie at matched weights, differ by Occam terms with learned weights, and the split wins linearly when the instantiation grammar misfits usage: the posterior tracks usage, not the logical boundary of a schema (`rem:ident:mdl`).
* **A4. Derivation-length prior.** Generative reading: the size principle. "Statements given without proof should be easily derivable": the data are theorems, and a derivation likelihood favours the theory that makes them cheap to state; under the two-part code deriving each of six library theorems costs 6–24 times as much as adopting it (`prop:pa:memo`, `tab:pa:theorems`), and on a computed stream Q plus the theorems (weaker than PA) beats Q+T_Ind within the candidates scored (`rem:pa:streams`). The generator wins on data from its own process under the full-sum $\Lone$ or on direct schema uses (`prop:pa:gibbs`, `prop:pa:wellspec`).
* **A5. Time penalty.** Membership- and generation-time penalties do not stop the collapse (`prop:time:cheap`); derivation size in written symbols prices it: at least a fixed polynomial root of the assigner's nondeterministic time, infinitely often, for every theory with polynomial-time membership consistent with its labels (`thm:time:ntime`, `cor:time:hard`, `prop:time:sigma`); at most a polynomial of the deterministic time for the collapse constructions (proof sketch, `rem:time:upper`); for plain $\Lone$ only a logarithmic charge is proved (`cor:time:log`), a polynomial one conjectured (`conj:time:poly`). Summary: `rem:time:summary`.
* **A6. His last bullet.** Data from a law of the model, fixed weights: concentration on the generator class, the prior inside it (`thm:ident:doob`, `cor:ident:prior`); the theorem set under $\Lone$ (parameters admissible) and the instance union under $\Lzero$ are identified (`cor:ident:deductive`); with learned weights the exact class has mass 0 and the instance union is identified for a.e. weight vector under $\Lzero$ (`rem:ident:exact`, `thm:ident:limit`); the actual axioms keep their prior share. Human-stated theorems: in finite classes the posterior goes to the best-fitting generators, which can be weaker or unsound (`thm:ident:kl`, `tab:ident:misspec`), and in the full class even this can fail (`rem:ident:fullclass`); for PA the used axioms are picked up, PA-equivalents only on broad direct-use practice within the candidates scored (`sec:pa:answer`).
* **A7. Side results.** The thresholded verifier is guaranteed sound only for well-specified data: fixed weights, probability ≥ 1 − δ/π(C*_d), uniformly in time (`thm:sound:fixed`); learned weights: on average (`thm:sound:avg`), and at each weight vector with a shrinking threshold for likelihoods linear in w (`thm:sound:shrink`); for a derivation-grammar likelihood with learned weights only the averaged version is proved; misspecified, no guarantee (`prop:sound:misspec`). The trichotomy is (Bel, Dis, Indep); keep Bel and Pl (`prop:sound:belief`, `prop:sound:bracket`); renormalising and 50/50 are incoherent in general (`ex:sound:renorm`, `ex:sound:fifty`, `prop:sound:fiftyfifty`).
* **Provenance.** AI referees with independent code checked the first versions of the four research notes; results added in revision were checked only by their tracks and the section writers (complete list: `app:ver:process`); no human has checked the mathematics; nothing is checked in a proof assistant.
