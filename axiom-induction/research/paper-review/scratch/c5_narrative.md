# Consistency review of the draft "Bayesian Axiom Induction from Instances"

Reviewer role: consistency across the whole paper (`paper/sections/*.tex`, built `paper/main.pdf` of 8 October 2026, 16:22, which is newer than every section file). Reference documents: `paper/OUTLINE.md`, `NOTATION.md`, `CLAIMS.md`; the four `research/tracks/*/notes-final.md` with their verification logs and referee reports; `code/results/*.md` and `*.json`; Hänni's notes in `research/prior/`. Nothing in the paper was edited. Scratch scripts and outputs are in `research/paper-review/scratch/` (`c1_e1_table.py`, `c2_status.py`/`.out`, `c3_status2.py`/`.out`, `c4_make_issues.py`). The JSON issue list `issues-consistency.json` is generated by `c4_make_issues.py` from the same records as the numbered list at the end of this file.

Severity scale (as instructed): **fatal** = false or unsupported mathematical claim, or a claim stronger than the sources; **major** = missing hypothesis, wrong number, inconsistency, misleading framing, or a passage a reader cannot follow; **minor** = local wording or formatting.

Result: **42 issues: 3 fatal, 14 major, 25 minor** (4 of the minor ones are duplication). All three fatal issues and most major ones sit in the framing material (abstract, introduction, discussion, verification appendix). The body sections agree with the ledger and the results files much better than the summaries agree with the body.

Two other reviews ran in parallel (`review-math-A.md`, `review-math-B.md`). Where a problem in the body is already reported there, I cite it (MA-xx, MB-xx) and report only the copy in the framing sections or the cross-section inconsistency.

## 1. Claims of the abstract, introduction and discussion against the body

I checked every sentence of `abstract.tex`, the answers A1–A9 and the verdict table of `intro.tex`, and `discussion.tex` against the result it cites, for scope (calculus, likelihood, fixed or Dirichlet weights, parameters, class) and status.

**What is consistent.** Most answers are faithful summaries: A1 (prior, size principle, scores, computability), A3 (prior share 0.03–0.48, class-dependent limits, parameter instances, quantified data), A4 (Doob, prior inside the generator class, Gold, separation of equivalent axiomatisations), A5 (weaker or unsound limits, PA-equivalent mass only within candidate sets and pools), A8 (memorisation 6–24 times cheaper, MAP weaker than PA on theorem data), A9. The discussion's paragraphs on templates, on the two readings of a derivation-length prior and on Hänni's note are accurate and carry their hypotheses. All quotations of Hänni (both notes) and of the question were checked verbatim against `research/prior/` and `00-brief.md`.

**Where the summaries say more than the body.**

* *"Closed instances never favour ∀xφ"* (abstract; A2 heading) — the body proves the direction for the listed calculi and names an exception: under the stream filter with learned weights and a background the Bayes factor tends to h(w*), which exceeds 1 for small w* (`prop:univ:B2`); with ∧-detours it is open. The intro keeps the second exception, the abstract keeps neither. **C-01 (fatal).**
* *H2 verdict, "a.e. weight vector with Dirichlet weights"* — this confirms for Dirichlet weights a concentration statement that the paper refutes (`rem:ident:exact`: the exact class has mass 0). **C-02 (fatal).**
* *"plain L1 is proved to charge only logarithmically"* (A7) — `cor:time:log` is a lower bound; a polynomial charge is conjectured. The discussion and §6 say this correctly. **C-03 (fatal).**
* Missing hypotheses in the abstract: fixed weights and parameter admissibility for identification (**C-04**); finite classes for "the posterior goes to the best-fitting generator", which the intro's own "What is not achieved (ii)" contradicts (**C-05**); "referees checked the results", although results added in revision were not refereed (**C-06**).
* A6 turns one example into a general law ("Misspecified, a waiting prover wins with probability 1"; "sound only for well-specified data"), while the body also shows misspecified runs with no wins and a merely incomplete verifier (**C-08**). A6 and the discussion's "good version" apply the shrinking-threshold theorem to learned weights in general; it needs a likelihood linear in the weights, which the recommended derivation likelihood is not (**C-09**).
* The discussion's "what it can promise" lists theorem-set identification for a "good version" that includes a selection model, under which exactly that fails (`thm:univ:omega(b)`) (**C-10**); and "an inconsistent theory only by refutation" contradicts three body results in which positive data remove inconsistent theories (**C-15**).
* A7 drops "Σₙ-sound assigners, over PA" from "templates do not block the collapse" (**C-11**) and states the symbol-size charge as lying "between the nondeterministic and the deterministic time", where §6 says "a polynomial root of t(m)" (**C-12**). A4 justifies "the MDL finding is unchanged" by ties and Occam terms only, omitting the linear split win under misfit usage, which is the finding (**C-13**). The H7 row drops "consistent" from "no r.e. theory survives Th(ℕ)" (**C-14**).
* Minor: the abstract's ω-gap dichotomy omits the misspecified case (C-18); the verdict table cites the wrong result for H4(a) (C-19); dropped weight hypotheses and a bare `\log` in the summaries (C-20); the list of conjectures in "What is not achieved" omits the universal-track limits (C-21); the discussion cites E4(C1) for a point it does not illustrate (C-33); the open-problem list misses two items the paper states as open (C-37).

## 2. The same result stated the same way

I compared every result that appears in more than one place. The numbers agree everywhere I checked: the prior shares (0.333, 0.200, 0.484, 0.030, 0.042), the ∀E factors per calculus, the E1–E8 numbers repeated in §§3–7 and §8, Example 4.9's ranges, the spare-slot formula and its 19.03/19.82 bits, the SDPC margins (+770 → +762, crossover 2⁷⁸⁰), the narrow-practice crossover 2^26.7, the Euler leads, the DTRC masses, the c4 run (2000/2000, median 17), and the E2 acceptance counts (2 + 3 = 5 of 25; legacy 19 of 25; seeds 1, 4 among 0–4; legacy seeds 1, 2, 4). Hypotheses and names drift in a few places: the collapse charge (C-12), the shrinking threshold (C-09), the Σ₁-completeness of Q, which §6 uses without the <-free restriction that the paper itself records as a correction from the universal track (**C-16**, major), the forall-elimination rate under a learned shared rule law (C-24), and the H2 row (C-02).
