\section{How the results were established}\label{app:ver}

\subsection{Process}\label{app:ver:process}

\paragraph{Roles.} A research brief (\texttt{research/00-brief.md}) quoted the question and set out hypotheses H1--H7 to be confirmed, refined or refuted (\cref{tab:intro:verdicts}). Four research tracks then worked in parallel: \emph{model} (the general theory), \emph{universal} (the case $\forall x\varphi$), \emph{pa} ($\PA$ and $\ZF$) and \emph{experiments} (the package \texttt{code/bai} and the experiments E1--E8). Each track wrote notes (\texttt{notes.md}) with seeded check scripts. An adversarial referee for each track read the notes, re-derived the proofs, wrote independent code (\texttt{referee\_code/}) and filed a report (\texttt{referee.md}). The track revised its notes into \texttt{notes-final.md}, which ends with a verification log mapping every referee issue to its resolution. The referees found no fatal error. They raised, by track: model, 5 major and 16 minor issues and 6 questions; universal, 3 major and 11 minor issues and 7 questions; pa, 5 major and 11 minor issues and 5 questions the track had missed; experiments, 5 major and 14 minor issues. A planning editor then wrote an outline, a notation file and a ledger of every claim with its status and source, and reconciled 43 cross-track conflicts (\cref{app:ver:conflicts}). Section writers wrote each section from the final notes only, re-reading the proofs as they went and recording what they found (\cref{tab:ver:writing}). A framing editor wrote the introduction, the discussion and this appendix, and ran the build.

\paragraph{What this means for the reader.} All roles were separate Claude instances (Anthropic). No human has checked the mathematics. Nothing is checked in a proof assistant. Track pa's natural-deduction derivations are checked line by line by a small checker, \texttt{nd.py}, which rejects ten planted unsound steps and the referee's 173 mutants (\cref{app:pa:checker}); its referee re-checked the derivations with an independent checker. Results added in the revisions were checked by their track and re-read by the section writers, but not by a referee; among them are (numbered as in the tracks' notes, which the grey source notes give) model Thms 4.7, 4.8, Lemma 4.6, Props 5.5(d), 5.6, 6.11, 6.14, 7.6, 8.2, Lemma 6.10, Thm 6.12, Cor 6.13 and Ex 3.6; universal Lemma N0, Props N1--N3, B2, U2n, U2t and U10(e); pa Props 1.4, 2.3, 3.1--3.4, 4.8 and Lemma 4.7; experiments Props X10, X12, X13 and E8. The appendices name these where they give the proofs (e.g.\ \cref{app:ident,app:pa}).

\paragraph{Track against referee.} In five places the final notes disagree with the referee, with reasons, and the paper follows the final notes. (1) Pa, m11: AS Thm~5.16 holds in closure-normal form with parameters; the real gap is that it concerns a single covering template (\cref{prop:pa:isigma}). (2) Pa, M4: the law G1 does not force a formula metavariable; its 157 skeletons are covered by a term-only theory inside $\ISigma2$, and the posterior keeps $\PA$ through the Occam balance (\cref{ex:pa:skel}). (3) Experiments, M2: open guards do reach $\forall x\forall y$ under the single-parameter convention (\cref{prop:exp:comm}(a)). (4) Universal, M2: the referee's ``$\to0$ over $\Q$'' holds only with bounded split depth (\cref{prop:univ:sentences}). (5) Model, m13: the tightness of the soundness constant holds exactly, not only as a supremum (\cref{rem:sound:tight}).

\subsection{Reproduction}\label{app:ver:repro}

Every computed number comes from a seeded script whose output is saved next to it.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Track checks.} \texttt{research/tracks/<track>/checks/} holds each track's scripts and their \texttt{.out} files (model c1--c16; universal c1--c10; pa \texttt{test\_nd}, c1--c10; experiments' check scripts), and \texttt{referee\_code/} the referees' independent scripts and outputs. Universal re-ran all ten of its scripts and the referee's seven, and pa all twelve of its scripts, in a scratch directory, with byte-identical outputs; pa's \texttt{c10\_quotes.py} checks 87 quoted numbers of its notes against the outputs.
\item \emph{Code.} From \texttt{code/}: \texttt{python3 -m pytest -q tests} (21 unit tests, including a comparison with the referee's independent implementation); \texttt{sh run\_all.sh} (tests and E1--E8, about 50 minutes on four shared cores); seeds and per-experiment commands in \cref{app:exp:repro}. Results are in \texttt{code/results/*.md} and \texttt{*.json}, the first version's in \texttt{code/results/v1/}.
\item \emph{Paper.} From \texttt{paper/}: \texttt{./build.sh} merges the bibliographies and runs pdf\LaTeX{} and Bib\TeX; \texttt{./test-section.sh <section> <appendix>} builds one section alone.
\end{itemize}
