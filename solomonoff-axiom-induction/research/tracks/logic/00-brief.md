## Goal

Establish the phenomena that exist for axiom induction because its data are sentences of a logic, and that have no analogue in SOI on strings. Scout N1 found four, with full first-pass proofs:
1. **the lottery**: a consistent stream on which the one-tape inducer has bounded loss while no consistent r.e. theory proves the stream (so the coin-free and Kolmogorov versions have unbounded loss, and the limit posterior lives on non-r.e. random theories), and the exact boundary of the phenomenon (bounded inconsistency arity; $\Pi_1$ data);
2. **$\Pi_1$ derandomisation**: on $\Pi_1$ data over a true arithmetic background the universal random theory's knowledge at confidence $2^{-k}$ is contained in one true theory of complexity $k+O(\log k)$, giving a coding theorem for $\Pi_1$ data and a Chaitin theorem for random theories;
3. **Gödel phenomena**: the arithmetical shape of the trichotomy ("independent" on a $\Pi_1$ question certifies truth), soundness is never learned, $\Con(T)$'s direction is a choice of universal machine, old evidence;
4. **the ω-gap**: its anatomy, and a uniform Gödelian strengthening.
The thread must verify each proof line by line, write paper-quality proofs, verify the recalled literature or work around it, settle novelty against Bienvenu et al. (2014), and attempt the open questions listed.
