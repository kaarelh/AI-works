\section{Compression schemes}\label{sec:methods}

All code lengths are computed by \repo{code/cgb/coding.py}: integers by Elias-$\delta$ (signed integers by the zigzag map), subsets of $[n]$ of size $k$ by $L_\delta(k+1)+\log_2\binom nk$, and integer arrays of known shape by the cheaper of two codes plus one flag bit. The dense code sends the range and then an adaptive Krichevsky--Trofimov (KT) code over it; the sparse code sends the support as a subset and then the nonzero values with a KT code. Step sizes lie on the grid $2^{e/4}$, $e\in\mathbb Z$, and are sent as signed integers. A unit test checks the Kraft inequality for each component (\repo{code/tests/test_bounds_coding.py}).

\paragraph{Boolean tasks: function-space compression.} Inputs are uniform on $\{\pm1\}^n$ and the trained network $f$ is an MLP of widths $(n,512,512,1)$. The codec only queries $f$ on inputs drawn from the uniform distribution, independently of the training set. (i) It estimates the influences $\mathrm{Inf}_j(f)=\Pr[f(x)\ne f(x^{\oplus j})]$ and proposes coordinate sets $J$, the coordinates with influence above $\tau$ for $\tau\in\{0.3,\dots,0.005\}$, plus $J=[n]$. (ii) For each $J$ with $|J|\le14$ it builds the \emph{truth table} $g(x)=T[x_J]$, where $T$ is the majority of $f$ over each fibre (estimated on a pool of $10^5$ uniform inputs) and is sent with a KT code. (iii) For each $J$ and hidden width $h\in\{0,1,2,4,8,16\}$ ($h=0$ is a linear threshold function) it distils a student MLP on $x_J$ from $f$'s labels on the pool. It then quantizes the student at three step sizes with quantization-aware fine-tuning (straight-through estimator) and sends its integers. (iv) It keeps the candidate minimizing the estimated bound: the Occam term plus the disagreement on a separate selection pool of $3\times10^4$ uniform inputs. (v) The certified disagreement is computed afterwards, either exactly on all $2^n$ inputs ($n=20$) or on $2\times10^5$ fresh inputs ($n=50$). The description includes a 2-bit codec-family tag, the subset $J$, the architecture and the steps. None of the codec's choices needs a union bound in the Occam term, because they are all part of the description.

\paragraph{Images: weight-space compression relative to the initialization.} For an MLP $f$ on $28\times28$ images, the compressed $g$ keeps $f$'s architecture and initialization $\theta_0$ (free by \cref{lem:init}; we still charge 32 bits for the seed). It codes the update $\theta-\theta_0$, with the first layer's update expressed in the orthonormal 2-D DCT basis and quantized with one step per frequency band ($u+v\in[0,4),[4,8),[8,14),[14,22),[22,32),[32,\infty)$). The integers are coded per band. Starting from $f$, $g$ is fine-tuned through the quantizer, with either training labels, $f$'s soft labels on the training inputs (distillation), or both. The objective adds a rate penalty $\lambda\sum|c|/s$ that pushes coefficients to zero. Both this fine-tuning and the choice of $(\text{step},\lambda,\text{loss})$ may use the training set freely.

\paragraph{Images and Boolean tasks: dithered compression for training-data-only bounds.} For one-hidden-layer networks, $g$ is the subtractive dither of $f$ (\cref{lem:dither}) with steps $s_{ik}=2^{(e_i+e'_{\mathrm{band}(k)})/4}$. The per-band exponents follow $(\mathrm{rms}\,\hat x_{\mathrm{band}})^{-p_b}$ and the per-unit exponents follow $\|V_{:,i}\|^{-p_u}$, both times a global scale. The steps are part of the description, and the integers are coded per band. The global scale and the exponents $(p_b,p_u)$ are chosen on a subsample of the training set with one dither draw; the bound is then computed with a fresh draw on the full training set. We use $t_1=5.8$ and $t_2=3.9$ throughout, fixed before the final runs and charged as one of four allowed pairs (2 bits); $\eta/\delta'\le2\times10^{-3}$ at $\delta'=0.01$ for $H\le128$, and every reported bound uses the $\eta$ of its own $H$. The description also carries 2 bits for the initialization scheme and the reference (initialization or zero).

\paragraph{Baselines.} For every trained $f$ we report the Occam bound with 32 bits per parameter (\emph{parameter counting}) and the VC bound $\hat e+\sqrt{(d(\ln(2m/d)+1)+\ln(4/\delta))/m}$ with $d$ equal to the number of parameters. The latter is optimistic for the baseline, since the VC dimension of ReLU networks with $W$ weights and $L$ layers is $O(WL\log W)$ and $\Omega(WL\log(W/L))$ \cite{bartlett2019nearly}.

\paragraph{Confidence.} $\delta=0.05$ throughout. In \cref{thm:reduction} it is split equally between the two terms, unless the disagreement is computed exactly. For the dithered bounds, $\delta=0.04$ and $\delta'=0.01$.
