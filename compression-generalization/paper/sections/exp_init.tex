\paragraph{The init-kernel codec.} \Cref{tab:kernel} compares, on the same Boolean networks ($n=30$, one hidden layer of 512 units), the init-kernel codec of \cref{prop:kernel} with function-space distillation. The lazy regime uses initialization scale 2 and plain SGD; the rich regime uses scale 0.1 and Adam. The init-kernel codec describes $\theta_0+P_r\Delta$, where $P_r$ projects onto the top-$r$ eigenvectors of the uniform-input empirical kernel at $\theta_0$, estimated from 4000 uniform inputs; it is free given $\theta_0$. Its candidate values of $r$ are the dimensions of the low-degree Walsh spaces, $1+n=31$, $1+n+\binom n2=466$ and $4526$, together with powers of two.
