With unlabeled inputs from $D_X$, \cref{thm:reduction} applies to image classifiers directly; this is the setting of \citet{bazinet2026bound}, and we include it to place the other results. The surrogate $g$ is the init-relative, DCT-coded, quantized network of \cref{sec:methods}, fine-tuned with a rate penalty using training labels, $f$'s outputs, or both. The disagreement is measured on the 10{,}000 test images without their labels, with a union bound over the $K$ compression configurations tried for each $f$. \Cref{tab:t2} reports the best Occam bound on $g$ itself and the best bound on $f$. Our compressor is simple and uses a data-independent prior, so these bounds are much weaker than those of \citet{bazinet2026bound}, who report 1.8--3.5\% for an MNIST CNN using surrogates trained on part of the training set (data-dependent priors and sample compression). Coding the first-layer update in the DCT basis instead of the pixel basis matters a great deal here as well.
