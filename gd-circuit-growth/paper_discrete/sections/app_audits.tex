\section{Protocols and audits}\label{app:audits}

Every part was reviewed by independent audit agents: separate AI sessions with their own instructions, which reported to the orchestrating session. This appendix lists what each audit found and what was done about it. The Part 8 audit reports are in \repo{study/hybrid/audit/}. The Part 6 and 7 audit scripts and logs are in the session scratch directory, not in the repository; their findings are recorded in the write-ups \repo{study/discrete_learner/DISCRETE-LEARNER.md} and \repo{study/standard_tasks/STANDARD-TASKS.md}.

\subsection{Part 6}

Five independent audits checked the comparison:
\begin{itemize}
\item data and leakage;
\item compute accounting;
\item reproduction;
\item two reviews of the write-up, one of numbers and one of framing.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Found sound.}
\begin{itemize}
\item There was no test-data leakage. The training sets match the networks' row for row, for every $n$ and seed, across suites and $d$, and the networks' saved weights reproduce their recorded training error on these sets.
\item DFG sees only $(X,y)$ and a random generator.
\item An independent ufunc-level FLOP count agrees with DFG's counter to within 0.98--1.00.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Found wrong, and what was done.} Each item was fixed or disclosed in the write-up.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The degree-5/6 search} was added knowing par5 was in \code{dlheld}. The blind row now uses v1 without it.
\item \emph{The development suites.} \code{random} was used during development, and the small-$n$ rules were tuned on the designed suite's test error. Both are labelled as not out of sample.
\item \emph{A compute-budget bug at $d=120$.} The cross-validation folds each had the whole budget, costing up to 2.7 runs of a 20000-step network per fit. v1.1 fixes it.
\item \emph{A tie-break bias toward low-index inputs.} The input-permutation wrapper was added before any run on \code{dlheld2}, and the size of the bias was measured (\cref{sec:tiebreak}).
\item \emph{A seed-lucky $\nstar$ for mux.} The fresh-seed value (about 87 against 65) is reported.
\item \emph{Several overclaims in drafts.} They were corrected after the numbers and framing reviews (commit \texttt{c12c3b5b}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Part 7}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Protocol audit.} No builder touched a held-out dataset. The plan to designate a primary design did not hold: \code{dfg_dirs} froze its second version after the other designs' held-out runs had started. All three are therefore reported on an equal footing.
\item \textbf{MLP-fairness audit.}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Finding.} The baseline MLP was under-regularized at small $n$: its only regularizer was early stopping, chosen on as few as 10 validation points.
  \item \emph{Action.} \code{mlp_l2} was added, and 18 of DFG's 23 cell wins over the baseline became ties or losses against it.
  \item \emph{Calibration.} The audit also checked the baseline's large-$n$ errors against ranges it recalled for 2-layer MLPs. They are within those ranges except MNIST (2.5\% against about 1.6--2\%). The ranges were recalled, not checked against sources.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Compute audit.} The FLOP counters were checked against an instrumented count. True over reported is 0.98--1.00 for \code{dfg_bits}, 1.00--1.05 for \code{dfg_gates} and 1.01--1.05 for \code{dfg_dirs}. \code{dfg_gates}'s final readout refit is not reserved in its budget check.
\item \textbf{Claims check} (commit \texttt{9fd619b8}). It corrected the following: which wins survive, the image error ranges, the pendigits range, the gradient-boosting comparison, the framing of the test stall, and protocol details.
\item \textbf{Incidents.}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{A crash and a post-freeze fallback.} \code{dfg_gates} crashed on MNIST when a Cholesky factorization failed. A jitter fallback was added that runs only when the factorization fails, so fits that never crashed are bit-identical.
  \item \emph{The one fit where the fallback is known to have fired} (MNIST $n=10000$, seed 1) is an outlier: 0.233 against 0.075--0.076 for the other seeds. It raises that learner's image mean $\log_2$ ratio from $+0.36$ to $+0.41$, and its retry FLOPs are not counted.
  \item \emph{Memory kills.} Several image runs were killed by the memory limit and rerun with resume support, with no effect on the results.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Part 8}

Four code-and-process audits ran while the primary's held-out jobs were running: protocol and leakage, compute accounting, discreteness, and fairness of the references. Each audit reports that it read no held-out result of a Part 8 learner.
\begin{itemize}
\item None found test-data use inside a learner, or held-out access by a builder.
\item The freezes hold: the md5 sums match, and a re-run of the frozen primary reproduced its dev errors exactly.
\item HELDOUT2 and \code{dlheld3} were fixed (commit \texttt{04a215b2}, 06:05) before the first builder started (06:22).
\end{itemize}
\Cref{tab:auditactions,tab:auditactions2} list the findings that changed something or needed disclosure. No learner was changed.



\paragraph{Deviations from the Part 8 protocol.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Headline sets.} The protocol listed all held-out sets as plain held-out data. After the audits, the headline was restricted to the fresh \code{dlheld3} and HELDOUT2.
\item \emph{Toy headline.} It uses permuted inputs, which the protocol did not specify.
\item \emph{References.} The protocol named \code{mlp_best} as ``best of five'' variants; in fact 2--3 existed per split at the time. This report adds \code{nets_wd}, \code{nets_cewd}, \code{nets_best}, \code{mlp_strong} and \code{mlp_strong1}, all of which make the references stronger. The calibration references \code{lin} and \code{hgb} enter no verdict.
\item \emph{Secondary designs.} The protocol evaluates all three frozen designs on all held-out sets.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \code{hyb_stage} and \code{hyb_union} were run on the held-out toy suites only on \code{dlheld3}, with inputs permuted.
  \item \code{hyb_union} was not run on the Part 7 tabular set.
  \end{itemize}
\item \emph{Ablations.} They are post hoc.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Scope cuts for compute.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{mlp_strong} was not run at five large Part 7 cells (adult $n=30000$ and 38842, letter 15000, connect\_4 30000 and 57557), or on the dev sets.
\item \code{nets_cewd} was run on \code{dlheld3} only.
\item The real ablations cover $n\le3000$ with seeds 0--1, on HELDOUT2 and the images only.
\item \code{hgb} was not run on the \code{designed} toy suite.
\end{itemize}
