\multicolumn{2}{l}{\emph{DFG's search} (\cref{sec:learner})}\\
character $\chi_S$; degree & $\chi_S(x)=\prod_{i\in S}x_i$; $|S|$\\
residual $r$; $s$ & $r=y-\hat y$, with $\hat y$ the least-squares fit on the current features; $s=\mathrm{rms}(r)$\\
round & a pass that searches every class on $r$, admits what passes its test and refits; at most 8\\
class; union bound $N$ & candidates tested together; their number $N$ enters the threshold as $\ln(4KN/\delta)$; $K=4$, $\delta=0.1$\\
global, extension, local & every character of one degree $\le K$ on all $d$ inputs (degrees 5--6 on a subsample); $S\cup\{j\}$ and $S\cup\{j,l\}$ for admitted $S$; every subset of the $\le12$ inputs in use\\
linear block & all remaining degree-1 characters at once, admitted by a $\chi^2$ test on their coefficients\\
spectral feature & $(u\cdot x)^2-|u|^2$, $u$ an eigenvector of the residual pair matrix whose eigenvalue clears the noise edge\\
sum block; statistic $t$ & signs $s_i$ on a subset $S$, $t=\sum_{i\in S}s_ix_i$; its features indicate groups of values of $t$\\
folded, modulated block & $|t|$ in place of $t$; the block's indicators times an admitted character $\chi_T$\\
product, split block & the joint cells of two disjoint blocks; a mixed-sign block taken as the product of its $+$ and $-$ parts\\
weighted block & $u=t+a\tau$ in place of $t$, with $a\in\{\pm2,\pm3\}$ and $\tau$ a literal, a 2-parity or an AND of two literals\\
block search & how blocks are proposed: from the residual's degree-1 and pair statistics\\
forced variant & at $n\le512$, takes the best candidate if none passes; chosen by a paired cross-validation test\\
kernel background & one Hebbian step on the final residual, $\sum_ig_ix_i$ with $g_i=\frac1n\sum_tr_tx_{t,i}$, added to the output\\
readout & least squares on the admitted features, refined by 15 logistic Newton steps when $n\ge8p$\\

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\emph{Metrics} (\cref{sec:metrics})}\\
$\nstar$ & the training-set size at which the seed-mean test error reaches 10\%\\
$\log_2$ ratio & $\log_2(\nstar_A/\nstar_B)$, negative when $A$ needs less data\\
clearly better, worse & the ratio is below $-0.25$ (above $+0.25$), or only $A$ (only $B$) reaches 10\% error\\
suite mean; pooled & the mean ratio over the targets both learn; per-target ratios pooled over suites\\
cell; better, tie, worse & a (dataset, $n$) pair on tabular data; the verdicts of the cell rule\\
budget & one toy network run in FLOPs ($1.62\times10^{11}$ at $d=30$); for real data, three training runs of \code{mlp}\\

$n_{\rm struct}$ & the first analysed $n$ at which DFG's model holds the target's exact signed blocks (\cref{sec:interp})\\
