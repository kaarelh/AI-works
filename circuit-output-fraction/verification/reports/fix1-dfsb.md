**Paper.** Atoms are now defined: nodes with at most a = ⌊c log₂|C|⌋ support variables.
- Gates with support at most a are replaced by reduced OBDDs (R8), and an atom is never merged into a larger parent.
- New rule (T′) computes each atom exactly. Atoms are leaves for (D), (B) and Conf.
- (F) runs to a fixpoint, and a conflict gives D(h)=0. (B) breaks ties by smallest index. Opaque gates are leaves of the NNF. A box-free envelope with mass 0 gives D(h)=0.

### New findings of the re-review

### 1. [major] code/rareprob/meta.py, meta_estimate, underflow branch (results.append(Estimate(0.0, 0.0, 2.0**-1000, ...)) after exact_ve)

**Problem.** The new branch certifies p < 2^-1000 whenever the floating-point VE value is below 2^-1000. It does not widen by VE's own rounding error, which the other branch bounds by wid >= 1e-9. Inputs exist where the true p is slightly above 2^-1000 but VE returns slightly below it. The output interval then excludes p deterministically, with probability 1, not at most delta. Reproduced (scratch ve_edge.py): C = AND of 1000 literals with q_i = 0.5 + U(-1,1)*1e-13 and true p = 2^-1000(1+7.4e-15). exact_ve returns 2^-1000(1-4.5e-14), and meta_estimate outputs [9.33263550e-302, 9.332636185032189e-302] (Janson intersected with [0,2^-1000]), which misses p. This occurred in 4 of 400 random trials. It also contradicts thm:meta's requirement of 'certified error bounds, rounded outwards'.

**Proposed fix.** In the underflow branch use the same error bound as the exact branch. For example, take hi = 2^-1000*(1+wid) with wid computed at |log p| <= 1000 ln 2 + slack. Alternatively, test p*(1+wid) < 2^-1000 and otherwise use the widened branch. Add a regression test at the boundary.

### 3. [major] paper/main.tex abstract ('products and read-once compositions of dense sub-circuits, backdoor triggers, small cutsets, DNFs and DNNFs, and their locally disguised versions'); paper/sec-intro.tex lines 66-70 ('read-once compositions of arbitrary dense sub-circuits ... through sound local rewriting, disguised versions of all of these'); sec-discussion.tex line 7

**Problem.** (i) The summary still states the version of Cor. cor:dfsb(a) that both referees refuted (negation, and dense lambda_i that (D) splits into rare parts). The corrected corollary needs each lambda_i to satisfy the hypothesis itself, in its polarity. (ii) DNNFs are listed among circuits whose ideal K-explanation tree is polynomial. T*_K excludes (E), and a polynomial-size d-DNNF can have an exponential tree. Example: the counter NNF of [sum x_i >= 3n/4] (DNNF: True, 171 to 489 nodes).
