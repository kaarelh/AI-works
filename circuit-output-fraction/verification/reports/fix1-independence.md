### 2. [minor] paper/p1-indep.tex l.8-14 (definition of unate) versus paper/sec-setting.tex l.9-14 (NNF)

**Problem.** The definition says 'no variable occurs in literals of both polarities in its NNF (sec:setting)' and then gives its own propagation rule. The two disagree. In sec:setting's NNF, threshold gates without polynomially bounded integer weights 'stay opaque', and the others are 'expanded by a counter', which can introduce both literal polarities even for nonnegative weights. **Proposed fix.** Define unateness directly by the polarity propagation ("C is unate if the top-down propagation below reaches no variable with both polarities") and drop 'in literals ... in its NNF (sec:setting)', or say explicitly that the polarity-propagated circuit, not sec:setting's NNF, is meant.
