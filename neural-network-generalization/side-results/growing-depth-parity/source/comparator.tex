\section{A uniform globally separated comparator}\label{sec:comparator}
\begin{lemma}[Exact interpolation by point masses]\label{lem:comparator}
For any consistent training labels and any $a>0$, there is a globally
margin-separated fitting field with
\[
 \|z_{\rm comp}\|_K^2\le
 Ca^2\left[d\beta^{-\log_2(\pi/2)}+n(d\log d)^\beta\right].
\]
If $\beta\log\log d\le B_0$ and $n\gg d^2$, this is at most
$C_{B_0}na^2d^\beta$. The statement is deterministic.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Start from the constant field $-a$ and add $2a\delta_x$ at each distinct
positively labeled training input. The resulting field is exactly
$\pm a$ on the whole cube and fits all labels. The constant has energy
$a^2/\lambda_0$. If $v$ is the minimum matrix eigenvalue, the added
vector has Euclidean squared norm at most $4a^2n$, hence RKHS squared
norm at most $4a^2n/v$. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-spectrum} and
$\|u+v\|_K^2\le2\|u\|_K^2+2\|v\|_K^2$. Strict margins, when needed,
follow by an arbitrarily small common scaling.
\end{proof}
