\section{Uniform parameter choices and the posterior conclusion}
\label{sec:assembly}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
The depth assumptions imply $\beta^{-1}=O(\log\log d)$,
$\beta\log H=O(1)$, and $\beta H\to\infty$ uniformly in the polynomial
sparsity window. For any fixed small $\alpha>0$, an odd
$r\asymp\alpha k$ eventually exceeds $\exp(C_1/\beta)$ and satisfies
$r\le k/64$. The uniform comparator gives
$\|z\|_K^2\le Cna^2d^\beta$ for every minimizer. All spectral, Gram,
head, and transport bounds consequently have the uniform constants
specified in Section~\ref{sec:uniform-kernel}.

Choose fixed failure probabilities whose sum is at most $1/4$.
Suppose a compatible minimizing field has test error below $3/8$.
Its complete degree-$r$ head inherits this error and the global margin
up to vanishing population errors. Use
\[
 \zeta_{\rm stat}=1/400,\quad e=1/40000,\quad
 \epsilon=\rho^e,\quad p_{\rm bin}=\rho^{\beta/2+30e},\quad
 S=\rho^{-(1-\beta)/2}.
\]
The head cap is $a\rho^{-\beta/2-10e}$ and the contact quantile mass
is a fixed sufficiently small $\zeta_0$. All transport errors are at
most $\rho^g$, with $g=5e$, uniformly in depth. Choose an amplitude
cap $R=\rho^{-t_0}$ after fixing these parameters, with
$pt_0<\min(g,1/800)/2$. Then the bounded head-only losses transfer to
the population with errors tending to zero, and $R\ll S$.

Take $u=\lfloor a_1H\rfloor$ with $a_1>0$ small enough for the uniform
bad-set absorption. Set $m_f=\lceil MH\rceil$, with $M$ a fixed
constant. The polynomial lower bound on $k$ ensures $m_f\le k$.
Choose $a_1/M$ smaller than the constant in
Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-linear-calibration}, and then choose
$\alpha=r/k$ so small that $e\alpha m_f/(u+1)$ is a sufficiently small
fixed number. The restriction estimate gives
\[
 \E_{\rm face}\|P-P_{\rm face,\le u}\|_2^2
 \le Ca^2\rho^{-\beta}H
       \left(\frac{e\alpha m_f}{u+1}\right)^{u+1}
 =e^{-cH},
\]
after decreasing $\alpha$: the logarithm of its prefactor is
$O(\beta H+\log H)=o(H)$. The balanced-contact and random-face
selection therefore provide an accurate eligible face. Since
$\beta\log m_f$ is bounded, uniform calibration forces $u\ge cm_f$,
contrary to the choice of $a_1/M$.
Thus every compatible minimizer has error at least $3/8$.

Finally, the width limit is taken at each fixed $d$ and its finite
$L(d)$. Proposition~\ref{prop:rate} applies without needing any uniform
rate in $L$. Every limiting posterior mixture has draw error at least
$3/8$. If $F$ is the fraction of inputs whose correct-label probability
is at most $2/3$, then
\[
 3/8\le R_{\rm draw}\le(1-F)/3+F,
 \qquad F\ge1/16.
\]
Apply this inequality before the lower width limit to handle arbitrary
weights among tied minimizers. The sample events have joint probability
at least $3/4$. Since $r\asymp\alpha k$ and
$H\asymp\log(ed/k)$, the displayed exact sample size satisfies
$\log n_r\asymp k\log(ed/k)$, as claimed.
\end{proof}
