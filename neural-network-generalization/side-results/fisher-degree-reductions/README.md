# Degree and square-root energy on the Boolean cube: equivalent positive estimates and a signed obstruction

**Status:** Proved side result

This paper shows that three positive estimates are equivalent: weak Fisher-energy control, a linear degree bound, and approximation by sums of squares in relative L1 error. It disproves the analogous unrestricted signed statement.

The equivalence does not prove the positive inequality itself or the natural-scale posterior bound; both remain open. The signed counterexamples have no bounded-source-cost guarantee.
