Oriented-majority interpolant:
- Conditional on parity labels, deleting one target-parity coordinate
  leaves all other input columns independent uniform signs. This is
  true for sparse parity too, including coordinates outside its support.
- Exchangeability gives E[epsilon_i sign(sum epsilon)]=E|sum epsilon|/n.
  The stated Holder/fourth-moment calculation yields >=1/sqrt(3n).
- The oriented terms are independent across columns for each fixed
  training row. Hoeffding gives exp(-m/(6n)); row dependence is harmless
  under the union bound.
- Odd m makes the defining integer linear form nonzero everywhere.
  The shifted first-row Gaussian costs dm/2 and has residual variance
  (d+1)/d<=2. The signed feature mean is uniformly >=1/100.
- D=dm and a=1/100 satisfy the mixture condition once dm>=10000.
  This yields J_L(g)<=5000L(10000dm)^(1/L).
