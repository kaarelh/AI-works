The mathematical theorem and proof pass this audit.

1. The deterministic finite prefix gives an exact neuron-space projection, not merely a covariance inequality. Its scalar metric error is at most sqrt((2h+4)eta), and its reference seed has component p in [p_h,2p_h] and constant diagonal in (0,1]. Both p and the comparison diagonal may depend on the integer width; all subsequent estimates are uniform over that dependence.
2. The sharpened prefix estimate gives S0=1+log(1/p)+log(L+2) <= C(1+log K)T, with T=log(d+2)+h log(C+B+h+L+2). Since T dominates h and log L, the condition L-h>=c0T yields M=L-h-1>=L/2 and M>=CS0 after the fixed K and then c0 are chosen.
3. The existing steering-after-prefix lemma allows every positive initial constant diagonal at most one. If that diagonal is below the desired cooling level, it simply skips cooling. No unproved lower bound on the starting diagonal is used.
4. The cooling, holding, growth, purification, and increasing scalar continuation give a program of exactly M layers, total cost at most V*+CS0^2/L (including the prefix's 1/(8L)), and a component floor exp(-CS0). The bounded score construction has the same exponential scale. Residual purification costs and the stable scalar lift preserve the claimed floor.
5. The square-root entropy occupation argument applies to the continuation's stated upper costs, even though its initial feature kernel is not itself generated by a Gaussian row. Data processing gives the scalar transition entropy bound, the readout gives the terminal scalar bound, and suffix Bellman inequalities give the needed prefix budget. Enlarging true marginal entropies to their construction bounds preserves both inequalities. Thus sum sqrt(D_j)<=CS0.
6. The approximate-prefix mass corollary requires initial metric error <=kappa sqrt(v_M)/(64 exp S). Its inverse is exp(O((1+log K)T)). Choosing eta=8 exp(4-KT) satisfies this uniformly because K eventually dominates log K; h and the kappa factors are absorbed in T. Prefix failure probability can be chosen using the uniform lower bound on kappa.
7. Both explicit width conditions have logarithm O(T). In particular the union over the Boolean cube contributes d, whose logarithm is already in T. There is no exponential dependence on the cube size hidden in the sufficient width. The binary-entropy change-of-measure conclusion is for the raw original Gaussian prior.
