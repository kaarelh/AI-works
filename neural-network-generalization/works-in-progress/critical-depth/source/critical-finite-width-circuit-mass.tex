\section{Finite-width target probability for circuit teachers}
\label{sec:critical-finite-width-circuit-mass}

The finite circuit prefix and scalar-channel realization together give a
quantitative width threshold for the original critical prior.  The
dependence on teacher depth is explicit; no stability restriction is
imposed on the learner's support.

\begin{corollary}[Finite-width target mass for a threshold circuit]
\label{cor:critical-finite-circuit-mass}
Suppose $f:\{-1,1\}^d\to\{-1,1\}$ is the final wire of the
depth-$h$ layered threshold circuit in
Lemma~\ref{lem:critical-finite-circuit-prefix}.  Its maximum hidden
width is $C$, its gate weight norms are at most $B\ge1$, and its
gate margins on Boolean inputs are at least one.  Put
\[
 T=\log(d+2)+h\log(C+B+h+L+2).
\]
There are universal constants $c_0,c_1,c_2>0$ such that, if
$L-h\ge c_0T$, then for every integer $N\ge\exp(c_1T)$ the
original critical Gaussian prior satisfies
\[
 \mu_N\{f(x)z_w(x)>1/2\text{ for all }x\in\{-1,1\}^d\}
 \ge
 \exp\!\left[-N\left\{V_*+c_2\frac{T^2}{L}\right\}\right].
\]
The sufficient width is polynomial in $d,C,B,L$ at every fixed
teacher depth $h$.  All statements concern the raw unrestricted prior.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
We keep track of the accuracy needed by the continuation, since it is
not enough merely to plant approximately correct gate signs.  Choose
the prefix parameters
\[
 a=KT,\qquad \eta=8e^{4-KT},
\]
where the universal constant $K$ will be fixed below.  The prefix
construction has entropy at most $N/(8L)$ and, except on an event
of a prescribed small probability, ends at a kernel with an exact
score component whose metric error relative to
\[
 (v_0,r_0)=(p,\bar q_h-p),\qquad p=k_h/N\in[p_h,2p_h],
\]
is at most $\sqrt{(2h+4)\eta}$.  The ideal diagonal is at most
one.  These assertions hold uniformly at all widths satisfying the
prefix bound, even though $p$ and $\bar q_h$ may depend on $N$.

The estimate in Remark~\ref{rem:critical-finite-prefix-accuracy} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-finite-circuit-seed-log}
 S_0:=1+\log(1/p)+\log(L+2)
       \le C(1+\log K)T.
\end{equation}
Apply the scalar cooling, holding, growth, purification, and final
scalar-continuation construction to this ideal seed.  There are
$M=L-h-1$ remaining hidden layers.  Increasing $c_0$ by a universal
amount makes $M\ge L/2$ and $M\ge C S_0$, so that construction
fits into exactly the available number of layers.  With the prefix
cost included, it has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-finite-circuit-program-cost}
 A:=\frac1{8L}+\sum_{j=1}^M D_j+\frac1{8v_M}
       \le V_*+C\frac{S_0^2}{L}.
\end{equation}
All tracked components in this program are at least $e^{-CS_0}$.
Indeed cooling loses only a polynomial factor in its scale; holding
uses a component $\delta e^{-CS_0}$ with $\delta\ge c/L$;
growth and purification retain that lower bound; and the final
increasing scalar path starts at $p_{\rm sc}\ge c/L$.  The
purified residual is $O(\delta L^{-4})$, so its stable lift retains
a fixed fraction of every scalar state.  The bounded-score
construction of Remark~\ref{rem:critical-channel-reduction} therefore
gives a common score bound $B_{\rm sc}\le e^{CS_0}$.

The square-root entropy estimate applies to this continuation alone,
with its stated upper entropy costs $D_j$.  To check the initial
condition, its ideal starting diagonal is at most one; the Bellman
suffix lower bound and total cost
\eqref{eq:critical-finite-circuit-program-cost} give the same prefix
budget used in the occupation proof.  Thus no Gaussian assumption
on the initial seed is needed.  Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-finite-circuit-score-budget}
 S:=\sum_{j=1}^M\sqrt{D_j}
 \le C\log(L+2)+C\sqrt{L(A-V_*)_+}
 \le C S_0.
\end{equation}

Set $\tau=1/L$ and
$\kappa=\min\{1/4,\tau/[32(A+1)]\}$.  By
\eqref{eq:critical-finite-circuit-seed-log}--
\eqref{eq:critical-finite-circuit-score-budget}, all factors in
\[
 \frac{\kappa\sqrt{v_M}}{64e^S}
\]
are bounded below by $\exp[-C(1+\log K)T]$.  Fix $K$ sufficiently
large that
\[
 \sqrt{(2h+4)\eta}
 \le\frac{\kappa\sqrt{v_M}}{64e^S}
\]
holds uniformly.  This is possible because the logarithmic decay of
$\eta$ is linear in $K$, whereas the required exponent grows only
as $1+\log K$.  The gate-margin condition on $\eta$ is absorbed
by the same choice.  Choose the prefix failure probability at most
$\kappa/4$, using a uniform lower bound on $\kappa$ if its ideal
parameters depend on $N$.

After fixing these universal constants, the prefix width condition
and the sufficient width in
Corollary~\ref{cor:critical-channel-approximate-prefix} are both
at most $\exp(c_1T)$.  In the latter bound this follows from
$B_{\rm sc}\le e^{CS_0}$, $S\le CS_0$, $M\le L$,
$A\le V_*+CS_0^2/L$, and $\log|X|=d\le e^T$.
That corollary yields raw target mass at least $e^{-N(A+1/L)}$.
Finally \eqref{eq:critical-finite-circuit-seed-log} absorbs both the
fixed $K$ and the extra $1/L$ into $c_2T^2/L$.
\end{proof}

The gate-margin and gate-norm assumptions are automatically satisfied
with bounded constants for fan-in-two AND and OR gates and unary NOT
gates, after including constant wires.  Any other fixed Boolean gate
basis can first be compiled into this basis with constant overhead.
More general threshold gates are covered
with the displayed dependence on $B$.  Compiling or grouping a circuit
before applying the corollary is allowed, but its actual resulting
depth, width, and gate norms must be substituted in $T$.
