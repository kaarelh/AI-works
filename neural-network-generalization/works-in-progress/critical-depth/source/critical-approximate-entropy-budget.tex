\section{The entropy budget for an approximate covariance path}
\label{sec:critical-approximate-entropy}

The near-baseline entropy estimate is stable under entrywise errors
in the covariance recursion. This statement does not require a
finite-width large-deviation approximation.

\begin{lemma}[Approximate covariance paths]
\label{lem:critical-approximate-entropy-budget}
Let $H\ge1$, let $X$ be a nonempty finite set of inputs, and let
$Q_0,\ldots,Q_H$ be positive semidefinite matrices on $X$.
Suppose $Q_0(x,x)\le2$ and $Q_j(x,x)\le1$ for $j\ge1$.
At layer $j$, let $\rho_j$ be a controlled preactivation law of
finite entropy
\[
 D_j=D(\rho_j\Vert\mathcal N(0,Q_{j-1})),
\]
and write
$\widetilde Q_j=\mathbb E_{\rho_j}[\tanh(G)\tanh(G)^{\mathsf T}]$.
Assume, for some $0\le\Delta\le1$,
\[
 \|\widetilde Q_j-Q_j\|_{\max}\le\Delta
                    \qquad(1\le j\le H).
\]
Let $z$ lie in the range of $Q_H$, suppose
$|z(x_0)|\ge1/2$ at some $x_0\in X$, and put
\[
 r=\tfrac12 z^{\mathsf T}Q_H^\dagger z,\qquad
 A=r+\sum_{j=1}^H D_j.
\]
There is a universal $C$ such that, for every $\epsilon\ge0$ with
$A\le V_*+\epsilon$,
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^H\sqrt{D_j}
 \le C\left\{\log(H+2)+
       \sqrt{H\bigl(\epsilon+CH\sqrt\Delta\bigr)}\right\}.
\]
In particular, $\Delta\le c\tau^2/H^2$ permits the replacement of
$CH\sqrt\Delta$ by $\tau$, after a universal adjustment of $c$.
The hypothesis uses only one nonzero-margin output; no signed pair
or cube-edge condition is needed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Set $q_j=Q_j(x_0,x_0)$ and
$\widetilde q_j=\widetilde Q_j(x_0,x_0)$. The last variance is
positive, and RKHS Cauchy--Schwarz gives $r\ge1/(8q_H)\ge V(q_H)$.
For each layer, data processing to coordinate $x_0$ and the scalar
Bellman inequality give
\[
 V(q_{j-1})\le D_j+V(\widetilde q_j).
\]
When $q_{j-1}=0$, finite entropy forces the corresponding controlled
Gaussian coordinate to be zero, so $\widetilde q_j=0$; the displayed
inequality remains valid with $V(0)=V_*$.

The uniform scalar-value modulus
(Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar-value-modulus}) gives
\[
 V(q_{j-1})\le D_j+V(q_j)+C\sqrt\Delta.
\]
Telescoping from any hidden index $i$ through the readout yields
\[
 r+\sum_{j=i+1}^H D_j
       \ge V(q_i)-C(H-i)\sqrt\Delta.
\]
Consequently, using $V_*-V(q)\le C_0q$ on $[0,1]$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:approx-critical-prefix-budget}
 \sum_{j=1}^iD_j
       \le\epsilon+C_0q_i+CH\sqrt\Delta
                         \qquad(1\le i\le H).
\end{equation}

For $j\ge2$, scalar strict dissipation at the reference variance
$q_{j-1}\le1$ gives
\[
 D_j\ge\widetilde q_j-q_{j-1}+cq_{j-1}^2
       \ge q_j-q_{j-1}+cq_{j-1}^2-\Delta.
\]
Thus the inflated costs $E_j=D_j+\Delta$ satisfy exact dissipation.
With
\[
 e=\epsilon+C_1H\sqrt\Delta
\]
and $C_1$ sufficiently large, \eqref{eq:approx-critical-prefix-budget}
implies
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^iE_j\le e+C_0q_i,\qquad
 \sum_{j=1}^H E_j\le V_*+e.
\]
If $e\ge1/4$, Cauchy--Schwarz immediately bounds
$\sum_j\sqrt{D_j}$ by $C\sqrt{He}$.

Suppose $e<1/4$. Apply the last-exit dyadic-band proof of
Lemma~\ref{lem:critical-sqrt-entropy-budget} to the states
$q_1,\ldots,q_H$ and costs $E_2,\ldots,E_H$, using the preceding
prefix inequalities. Its small-band cutoff is
$\max\{e,(H+1)^{-1}\}$. That argument bounds
\[
 \sum_{j=2}^H\sqrt{E_j}
        \le C\{\log(H+2)+\sqrt{He}\}.
\]
Zero states are allowed here: they occur only in the initial
low-band part, where the argument uses Cauchy--Schwarz, while every
reciprocal telescoping division is performed on a band with a
strictly positive lower endpoint. If the minimum state is positive,
the original proof applies without change. The discarded first
transition contributes at most $\sqrt{D_1}\le\sqrt A<\sqrt3$.
Since $D_j\le E_j$, this proves the lemma. The case $H=1$ follows
from the same bound on the first transition.
\end{proof}
