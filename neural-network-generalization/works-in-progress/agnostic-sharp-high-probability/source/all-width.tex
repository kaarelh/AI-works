\section{One posterior at every width above the teacher width}
\label{sec:l1-all-width}
For this section take $\phi=\tanh$. Let $\mathcal H$ be the class of
single tanh features on the cube and let $\mathcal F$ be their absolute
convex hull: all finite sums $\sum_j a_jh_j$ with $\sum_j|a_j|\le1$.
Every learner width and every teacher under consideration lies in
$\mathcal F$. Monotonicity of tanh turns each subgraph test into a
linear threshold test after applying inverse tanh; hence the
pseudodimension of $\mathcal H$ is at most $D$. The same argument
also works for hardtanh.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform relative losses over the variation class]
\label{lem:variation-uniform}
Under the conditional sub-Gaussian assumptions, with probability at
least $1-\delta$,
\[
 \sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|\widehat A_S(f)-A(f)|
 \le K\sqrt{\frac{s[D\log(en)+\log(4/\delta)]}{n}},
\]
where $A(f)=R(f)-R(f_0)$ and
$\widehat A_S(f)=P_n[(f-Y)^2-(f_0-Y)^2]$.
The bound is independent of the number of terms in $f$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On any fixed input sample, quantizing the evaluation vectors of
$\mathcal H$ in maximum norm at mesh $n^{-2}$ gives a net with log
cardinality at most $KD\log(en)$. Indeed there are $O(n^3)$ input and
threshold pairs, and the subgraph growth bound controls their label
patterns. Use actual function representatives from a countable
pointwise-dense subclass, choosing the first one in each nonempty
quantization cell; the net is measurable and depends only on inputs.

The empirical Rademacher complexity of an absolute convex hull equals
that of its signed generating class. The finite-net bound for bounded
vectors therefore gives, uniformly over the observed inputs,
\[
 \mathbb E_\varepsilon\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}
       \left|n^{-1}\sum_i\varepsilon_i f(X_i)\right|
       \le K\sqrt{D\log(en)/n}.
\]
Symmetrization and the contraction inequality for
$t\mapsto(t-f_0(X_i))^2$ on $[-1,1]$, whose Lipschitz constant is four,
bound the expected supremum of
$|(P_n-P)(f-f_0)^2|$ by the same order. The harmless fixed-function
offset contributes at most $K/\sqrt n$. Since these squared
discrepancies lie in $[0,4]$, bounded differences adds
$K\sqrt{\log(4/\delta)/n}$ with the required confidence.

For the noise term, condition on the inputs and apply sub-Gaussian
concentration to the preceding finite net. It gives
\[
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal H}|P_n\xi h|
       \le K\sigma\sqrt{[D\log(en)+\log(4/\delta)]/n}
\]
after increasing constants and extending from the net. The extension
error is at most $n^{-2}P_n|\xi|$; the inequality
$\mathbb E e^{t|\xi|}\le2e^{\sigma^2t^2/2}$ controls it by the same
displayed order. A separate conditional sub-Gaussian bound controls
$|P_n\xi f_0|$. By $\ell^1$ duality,
$\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|P_n\xi f|=\sup_{h\in\mathcal H}|P_n\xi h|$.
Combine these bounds with
\[
 \widehat A_S(f)=P_n(f-f_0)^2-2P_n\xi(f-f_0)
\]
and allocate the failure probabilities to sum to $\delta$.
Continuity in the ridge parameters, rational coefficients, and bounded
pointwise approximation justify all suprema through a countable
separable subclass; no measurable-selection assumption on the unknown
conditional mean is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A width-uniform slow oracle for the same fixed posterior]
\label{thm:all-width-variation}
Use exactly the tanh learner, conditioned prior, and fixed-temperature
all-data posterior of Theorem~\ref{thm:l1-main}, with $n\ge2$ and
$0<\delta<1$. On one event of
probability at least $1-\delta$, simultaneously for every teacher width
$C\le N$, incoming cap $B\ge1$, and teacher $g$ with these resources,
\begin{align}
 \int R(f)\,dQ_S(f)
 &\le R(g)+K\sqrt{\frac{s[D\log(en)+\log(4/\delta)]}{n}}\notag\\
 &\quad+\frac{Ks(1+CD)}n\log\{eD(B+8)(C+1)n\}.
 \label{eq:all-width-variation}
\end{align}
The same conclusion holds for the posterior mean. The architecture is
fixed before observing its data; neither the posterior nor its
temperature depends on the teacher's resources.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Work on the common event of Lemma~\ref{lem:variation-uniform}, with
right-hand side $U_n$. For any comparator law $\rho$, Gibbs variational
optimality and the nonnegative posterior KL imply
\[
 \mathbb E_Q A(f)\le\mathbb E_Q\widehat A_S(f)+U_n
 \le\mathbb E_\rho\widehat A_S(f)+T\mathrm{KL}(\rho\|\pi_N)/n+U_n
 \le\mathbb E_\rho A(f)+T\mathrm{KL}(\rho\|\pi_N)/n+2U_n.
\]
For each teacher choose the direct comparison law at accuracy $n^{-2}$.
Its population risk is at most $R(g)+4/n^2$, and its KL is bounded by
\eqref{eq:direct-comparison}. Substitute these bounds. The uniform
event and the pathwise variational inequality work for all such
teachers and laws simultaneously; no confidence allocation is required.
\end{proof}

For tanh teachers with unrestricted incoming coefficients, compactify
each feature at tolerance $1/n$ using the Boolean or slab-regular
lemma. In \eqref{eq:all-width-variation}, replace the logarithmic
resource by
\[
 CD\{D\log(2D)+\log(e(C+1)n)\}
\]
on Boolean inputs, or by
\[
 CD\{\log(eD(C+1)n)+\alpha^{-1}\log(e\kappa n)\}
\]
under slab regularity, and absorb the $4/n$ approximation error.
This gives a vanishing coefficient-one approximation oracle at every
prescribed $N\ge C$ whenever the displayed statistical resources
vanish. At larger widths the merged theorem supplies the faster
near-$1/n$ remainder. Both statements concern the same all-data
posterior, with the same fixed likelihood variance.
