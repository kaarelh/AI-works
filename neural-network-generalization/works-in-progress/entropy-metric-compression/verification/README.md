# Review and verification scope

The [paper](../paper.pdf) gives `Õ(C⁴L¹¹)` at all positive mean-field-or-stronger scales, `Õ(C⁴L⁹)` for every finite `γ≥8/7`, and the strong-scale minimum `Õ(min{C⁴L⁷,C^(9/2)L⁶})`. The last bound also has a sufficiently-small-positive-scale version at each fixed width, called the strong-prior limit. The original calibrated internal-noise support and Gaussian tanh learner are unchanged. These are conventional proofs; **none of the new improvements is Lean formalized**.

The [entropy-metric appendix](../source/entropy-metric.tex) proves the global Ornstein–Uhlenbeck entropy-matrix inequality, its finite matrix message, independent-batch regression, the positive variance envelope, metric projection, and sequential quantization. Its finite comparison class uses arithmetic gates and is not asserted to be an ordinary network class. The proof imports the explicitly stated whole-layer active-direction and bounded-prefix estimates from the preceding construction. The [scale appendix](../source/scale-interpolation.tex) supplies the raw-prior branch, scale discretization, and interpolation. The [multilevel appendix](../source/multilevel-code.tex) proves the additional strong-scale code.

The [current review record](../../../verification/entropy-metric-depth-2026-10-29.md) documents independent mathematical checks. Reviewers checked the Gaussian-channel normalization, conditional future-weight bookkeeping, actual rank versus rank cap, positive-definite metric rounding, the regression risk, the zero-residual case, the nonlinear decoder's scalar mesh, the raw-prior cutoff, scale powers, and the multilevel argument. The [build/hash record](../../../verification/entropy-metric-depth-2026-10-29.json) identifies the reviewed revision.

Candidate function laws are independent of the input; comparison couplings may depend on it. Couplings preserve the original-weight marginal and the canonical prefix/future marginal. No intermediate candidate is assumed robust, and no selected-row address is hidden in the code. Density-test and Gram abort paths stay in the canonical law; the per-input variance-envelope failure is discarded only in the comparison estimate.

The main probability statement bounds posterior-average test-margin failure with probability at least `1−δ` over training samples, for any common iid input law and all eligible widths and stated scales. The required pointwise confidence statement uses `D{x: Q(f(x)z_w(x)>1/4)≤2/3}≤3R(Q)`. Applying the risk theorem at accuracy `ε/3` preserves `δ` and gives strict posterior correct-margin probability `>2/3` outside test-input mass at most `ε`. This is a pointwise confidence claim, not a claim about posterior mass on networks having small individual risk. Positive-scale statements are not automatically claims about an independently defined weak posterior limit at a closed margin boundary.

The displayed resource rates fix input dimension, calibration, accuracy, and confidence. Boolean width counts the reserved constant wire and uses `C≥2`. Existing Lean sources and certified statements are unchanged; no efficient posterior inference or numerical sample-size guarantee is asserted.
