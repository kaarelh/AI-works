Independent mathematical audit: fixed-width isotropic strong-prior upper bound
5 November 2026

Reviewer: isotropic_upper agent (co-developer of the column/moment argument, independently checked the orbit agent's descriptor and integration proofs).

Verdict: PASS, subject to the explicit C_2(f)=1 statement clarification sent to the author. The phrase “bounded-weight implementation” must mean every weight has absolute value at most one in the C=1 corollary; arbitrary finite weight bounds would not imply K=O(d).

Checked components:
1. Weak-margin minimum is attained by coercivity and closedness; readout scaling identifies strict and weak infima.
2. The exact original first-layer Gaussian covariance and weighted energy are preserved.
3. Nearby-column swaps give exact Boolean invariance from global margin, not merely approximate output invariance.
4. Random within-group permutations have the claimed moment generating function by Maclaurin's elementary-symmetric inequality and bounded-variable Hoeffding.
5. Taylor remainder constants, including tanh derivative coefficient recurrence, are valid.
6. Sampling-without-replacement moments are determined by group power sums through the truncation order. The partition inclusion-exclusion and falling-factorial coefficients are correct.
7. Descriptor quantization gives a uniform guarantee over every count vector; rounded descriptors need not be physically realizable.
8. Center packing uses total squared norms of actual selected columns and yields exponent n/(n+2).
9. Input signed permutations preserve exact function masses at every scale, including the augmented bias coordinate via oddness.
10. Uniform-block deletion charges actual posterior probabilities without assuming equal weights across energy levels.
11. Gaussian rate tails are uniform over histories since their denominator always contains the target event.
12. Cloning preserves the original width-normalized energy; C_2=1 gives K<=2d+1.
13. Growing q=ceil(log(d+2)) is valid: group coefficient bounds are exp(O(q log q)), not uncontrolled constants; H and mesh logs have the stated growth. Final budget exp(O_n(d^{n/(n+2)} log^4(d+2))) follows.

Scope: one hidden layer for the fixed-width extension; n fixed as d grows; C_2(f)=1 for the subexponential corollary. The general-energy fixed-q statement remains valid separately. No arbitrary-growing-width theorem, polynomial mistake theorem, or Lean verification is claimed.
