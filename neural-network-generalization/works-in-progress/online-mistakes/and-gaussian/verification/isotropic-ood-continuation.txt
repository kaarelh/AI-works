Isotropic strong-prior OOD continuation — 31 October 2026
========================================================
The requested superpolynomial mistake lower bound remains OPEN in this work.
The original isotropic Gaussian prior, global strict margin, exact label
conditioning and bounded-weight teacher convention are retained throughout
that question. A variance perturbation, arbitrary MAP tie-break, growing-depth
weak-prior limit or abstract Occam learner does not answer it.

Proved mistake bound
--------------------
source/majority-ood.tex gives the full conventional proof for odd majority,
C_2(f)=1 and learner width N=1. An explicitly specified input order gives
Omega(d^2) expected posterior-sampling mistakes in the strong-prior limit,
and a finite-small-scale high-probability consequence. It gives only a linear
lower bound for posterior-majority mistakes. The proof includes the exact
Gaussian symmetry across the two minimum-support sizes; equal energy alone
is not used to infer equal posterior weights. This is not Lean formalized.

New exact obstruction to an integer-energy shortcut
---------------------------------------------------
For x in {-1,1}^8 let
  f(x) = sign(13 + 7x1 + 6x2 + 6x3 + 4x4 + 4x5 + 4x6 + 3x7 + 2x8).
The unique minimum of a0^2+...+a8^2 subject to
  f(x)(a0 + sum_i ai xi) >= 1 for all 256 inputs
is attained at
  a* = (13,7,6,6,4,4,4,5/2,5/2),    Q(f)=701/2.

The adjacent script check_fractional_energy.py verifies every margin and
an exact nonnegative dual certificate, using Python Fraction only. In the
lexicographic (-1,+1) ordering of inputs, the active rows have indices
  28, 35, 67, 96, 131, 132, 136, 144
and their multipliers are respectively
  (145,171,171,209,373,111,111,111)/4.
Their conic combination equals a*, and their scalar product with a* sums
to 701/2. For any feasible a,
  ||a||^2 = ||a*||^2 + 2<a*,a-a*> + ||a-a*||^2 >= 701/2,
with equality only at a*. This is a complete rational optimality proof.

The seed function is the weighted voting game [12;7,6,6,4,4,4,3,2]. That
example is discussed by Freixas and Kurz, "On minimum integer representations
of weighted games", https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.0868 . The Euclidean optimum
and certificate above were computed and independently checked here; they
are not attributed to the integer-representation result.

Why this matters, and its limits
--------------------------------
Exact small-cube enumeration through d=5 and the earlier random searches
had only found odd-integer Q values. A proposed general integer-energy
argument could therefore have falsely suggested only polynomially many
energy levels below a bounded teacher's cost. The certificate disproves
that premise. It does not prove a large number of levels, construct an
adverse sequence, or establish any mistake lower bound for this function.

A further random search (60 functions each at d=10,11,12, with rational
feasibility and dual checks on accepted candidates) found no fractional
example. This failure is search evidence only; the explicit certificate
above is decisive. Random search is poorly suited to finding the special
symmetries needed by this route.

Other routes examined in this continuation
------------------------------------------
1. Ordinary online maximum-margin projection bounds do not automatically
   apply: the global all-input absolute margin makes the feasible parameter
   set a union of sign chambers, not the convex training-margin polytope.
2. Duplicating coordinates or conditioning on helper-coordinate examples
   could imitate a nonisotropic metric on a restricted input set, but does
   not yet preserve the full-cube global-margin event or exclude cheaper
   unintended classifiers. No embedding theorem has been proved.
3. Equal-energy candidates can have different Gaussian prefactors. Treating
   their posterior as uniform, or selecting a convenient MAP tie-break,
   would require an additional symmetry/asymptotic proof. The quadratic
   majority construction has that symmetry; a proposed exponential extension
   currently does not.

The next substantive target is an explicit isotropic construction with many
successive distinguishable minimum-energy classes, together with control of
its Gaussian tie weights and a polynomial bounded-weight teacher. The
fractional certificate removes one apparent obstruction; it does not finish
that construction.
