# Verification record

This is a conventional mathematical proof, not a Lean formalization.

The independent review covers the one-dimensional Lévy increment law, absolute continuity and full support, small-jump background conditioning, unbiased separated designated coefficients, the exact latent Poisson relative-entropy calculation, and data processing to scalar weights.

The network audit checks disjoint embedding supports, cancellation of the designated-edge location costs, the all-width formula and shared-network branches, exact unrolled occurrence counts, and depth-independent constants in the path and perturbation recurrences. The comparison laws use fixed coordinate cutoffs; no data-dependent or feature-dependent cutoff is needed.

The statistical review checks the sharp all-data expected-risk oracle for arbitrary priors and conditional sub-Gaussian noise, its leave-one-out/cooling proof, and the separate high-probability noisy fixed-temperature oracle for the ordinary final posterior mean, the formula-size and depth resource, the uniform function-neighborhood theorem, and the online confidence/majority/sampled-prediction conclusions. Sampled queries must be selected before each prediction's fresh randomness.

The raw-likelihood alternative keeps the raw output in the actual Gaussian likelihood and predicts by averaging clipped posterior draws. Its oracle is against bounded raw teachers. It does not claim the same bound for the unbounded raw posterior mean or for clipping that mean.

The stable-padding audit checks both ReLU identity relays and tanh weak relays under one fixed small Lévy intensity. Their moment bounds and precision costs give nearly linear additional learner-depth overhead.

The optional initialization calculation uses p>4 and proves a non-Gaussian limit with positive finite variance. The main learning theorem only requires p>2. The named variance-gamma variant has a linear coefficient-cap cost in place of the power-tail variant's logarithmic cost.

Review records and exact source hashes identify the audited snapshots. The final PDF is built from thirteen TeX modules using three clean pdflatex passes. Build logs and source/PDF checksums are retained.

The explicit signed beta-prime alternative is checked through direct density and conditional-moment estimates. Its shape changes with fan-in, and its finite-width law need not be infinitely divisible. The learning conclusions transfer through the scalar comparison interface.

The finite-second-moment extension proves its general leave-one-out inequality with unchanged labels, then instantiates the formula comparison and proves universal consistency. The depth lower bound uses shrinking prior increments; its raw-likelihood variant checks the prior second moment and Jensen normalizer explicitly, including native biases and normalized Gaussian readouts.
