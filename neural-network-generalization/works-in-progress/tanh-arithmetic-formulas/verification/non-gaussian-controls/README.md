# Non-Gaussian controls companion: verification

The [16-page companion](../../non-gaussian-controls.pdf) combines the previously [audited manuscript](fixed-rank-entropy-companion-independent-audit.md) with the [audited arbitrary-endpoint extension](arbitrary-endpoints-assembled-audit.md) and the [global-polar strengthening](global-polar-companion-assembled-audit.md). The global-polar assembly audit records the exact current [source](../../source/non-gaussian-controls.tex) hash. This is conventional AI-agent mathematical checking, **not Lean verification or human peer review**.

The audits cover the fixed-rank arbitrary-entropy blocks, arbitrary Gaussian controls, bounded-entrywise-norm quadratic tanh tilts, scalar Poisson factories, continuous-coordinate projections, separate norm oracle, physical normalization and polynomial-bit completion. Arbitrary first and last row laws are additionally allowed, with the explicit overhead in Corollary 3. This does not establish a compiler for arbitrary internal conditional posterior row laws or bounds on all backward costates.

Supporting component checks are included alongside these records. Earlier snapshot hashes refer to earlier manuscripts or mathematical notes; the current source and PDF hashes are in the build manifest.

The [build manifest](build-manifest.json) records an isolated clean build, exact source/PDF hashes and page count. The [compiler log](clean-build.log) has no LaTeX warnings or overfull/underfull boxes. The endpoint proof page was rendered and visually inspected.

[Detailed derivations](../../research-notes/non-gaussian-controls/README.md) preserve the supporting proofs and explicitly marked intermediate limitations. The companion PDF is the current presentation.

The [normalized ridge-difference audit](normalized-ridge-difference-tilt-compiler-audit.md) checks the additional representation theorem, weighted component entropy and squared-entropy bounds, common isometry, and physical scaling. The earlier full manuscript audit includes that assembled theorem and proof.

The [density-deletion audit](arbitrary-entropy-endpoint-deletion-independent-audit.md) independently checks the exact final-layer truncation inequality, including the zero-entropy case and the stated dependence on readout norm and margin.

The [global-polar proof audit](global-polar-normalized-ridge-compiler-independent-audit.md) checks the weighted raw component moments, global covariance isometry, scalar realizations of its adjoint and projection, and the actual-entropy budget. It removes the earlier need to sum normalized coefficient masses; a uniformly small per-layer representation bound remains required.
