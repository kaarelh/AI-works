# Independent root audit of the entropy interfaces

25 October 2026. Conventional mathematical check of sections 1, 3, and 5 of `nongaussian-entropy-formula-interfaces.md`.

**Section 1: PASS.** Conditional Gaussian quadratic transport gives the stated coupling with the anchor marginal unchanged. The scalar conditional monotone coupling is measurable. The degenerate r=0 case is forced by absolute continuity. Orthogonal projection onto the common anchor feature space, followed by taking the norm of its orthogonal residual, is a contraction in this joint L2 coupling; hence the square-root anchor distance estimate follows. The one-query scope is correctly distinguished from preserving a joint law for all queries.

**Section 3: PASS.** For small v, cutoff derivatives through order two are O(v^3), giving the H^(3/2) bound by interpolation. For large v, the homogeneous H^(1/2) norm of v sech^2(v a) scales like |v| (its square like v^2), and multiplication by the fixed cutoff is bounded. The other cutoff derivative terms are no larger. The entropy exponential-moment argument is valid because the same W is bounded both by Cz^2 and Cq^2z^6: use the former in the exponential and the latter in the prefactor. A fixed sufficiently small exponential parameter gives log Gaussian MGF O(q^2), then entropy duality gives O(q^2+D). No higher tilted moments are assumed. The endpoint caveat is substantive and correctly stated.

**Section 5: PASS for the stated complex-formula work convention.** The small-scale normalized residual has work-times-second-moment O(q^2 z^6 T). At large scale the ordinary tanh estimator divided by its scale has work-times-second-moment O(T), and subtracting the linear term preserves the O(z^2 T) bound, with independent mixture sampling or the elementary square-root sum bound. Sparsification to a constant expected-work cap preserves this product quantity up to universal constants and the unavoidable one-node zero output. Integration uses exactly the capped entropy quantity from section 3.

For clarity, this work convention permits complex multiplication and real/imaginary projection nodes during the randomized construction. The final finite complex expression family must be converted to real arithmetic formulas by the previously proved polynomial-size balancing procedure after the good seed is fixed. Charging an O(K^2) real expansion independently at every intermediate Fourier factory would require a different frequency integrability estimate. This does not invalidate the stated complex-work result, but it should remain explicit when it is used in a composed compiler. The continuous direct-integral coordinate/basis problem is not solved by these interfaces and is correctly left open.
