# Fresh-reader review of the raw coefficient-free companion

Reviewed 18 November 2026. The exact reader-facing files are identified in the adjacent hash manifest. This review checks the principal statements, algorithm and probability conventions, and standalone exposition. It does not replace the separate full proof audit of the restoring-bit comparison module.

The paper passes. Its abstract and introduction clearly identify arbitrary-coefficient raw ReLU comparison networks, the actual raw likelihood and posterior mean, one prescribed architecture, and a changed hierarchical Student/Gamma/normalized-Gaussian prior. The primary agnostic guarantee is in expectation under marginally sub-Gaussian labels and uses deterministic observation variance proportional to the square root of sample count. It is not represented as an unchanged independent Gaussian prior or as a same-width theorem for the uncompiled teacher.

The model states the effective coordinate dependence and the physical variance `T/2` explicitly. Comparison truncation and auxiliary prior truncation are confined to proofs. The compiled-width thresholds are displayed, and the simple `N≥n` growing-class corollary has the correct smaller teacher-width exponent. The continuous-input depth requirement is explicitly logarithmic in sample count; it is not hidden under a claim of fixed learner depth. The resource `~(C^(2bh)+H)` is stated with input dimension, teacher depth, and regularity fixed.

The fixed-temperature section correctly changes the assumption to a realizable bounded conditional mean, permits genuinely sub-Gaussian rather than necessarily Gaussian noise, retains `T≥max(8 sigma²+1,49 B²)`, and controls posterior-averaged risk before passing to the mean. The population approximation argument retains its pointwise comparison envelope. The noise floor appears once. Its expectation and confidence statements are distinct from the sharp agnostic expectation theorem.

The independent-coordinate appendix explicitly pays `log(N+1)` and limits its simple growing-width consequence to polynomial learner widths. It clearly changes both the scalar prior family and the agnostic temperature schedule, while retaining raw prediction. Its fixed-temperature corollary is again labeled realizable rather than agnostic. The alternative readout-density and known-budget refinements are also identified as changes to prior or calibration, rather than automatic improvements of the primary learner.

The binary quantizer and normalized-cube density compiler received an independent full mathematical audit by this reviewer, recorded separately in the parent verification directory. The slab/sphere compiler was authored by this reviewer and independently checked by the oracle and main proof reviewers; it is not counted as an independent self-review here. The assembled paper's copies of both modules matched their reviewed sources.

The final finite-second-moment addition also passes reader-scope review. It states its empirical-label-square temperature explicitly, keeps the architecture and prior fixed before data, and retains the marginal second-moment tail in the risk bound. Its remainder is not described as a distribution-uniform polynomial total rate under only finite variance. The displayed compiler entropy, depth ceiling, and statistical exponents agree: `C_n=n^(1/(24bh))`, `H≤n^(1/36)`, and entropy `~n^(1/12)` yield the stated `~n^(-1/12)` statistical term. This theorem additionally controls posterior-averaged raw risk. The final paragraph correctly distinguishes a data-calibrated likelihood from a fixed-variance generative model.

The README preserves the main algorithmic and statistical distinctions and does not introduce additional mathematical claims. Minor terminology and bibliography corrections identified during this review have been applied. No unresolved mathematical scope or presentation issue was found in the listed files.
