\section{Using the true Gaussian observation variance}
\label{sec:raw-matched-variance}
If the observation noise is exactly Gaussian with a known variance, even
the variance inflation in \eqref{eq:compiler-fixed-T} can be removed.
This uses a finite-dictionary testing argument and has a larger
complexity polynomial.

\begin{corollary}[Exactly matched Gaussian likelihood]
\label{cor:raw-compiler-matched}
Assume the input and realizable-conditional-mean conditions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:raw-realizable-compiler}, but now
$Y\mid X\sim N(f_0(X),\sigma^2)$ for a fixed known $\sigma>0$.
Use the same prior and the ordinary raw posterior at temperature
$T=2\sigma^2$, so its Gaussian observation variance is exactly
$\sigma^2$.  Let $K=\max\{\mathcal D_n,1+B^2\}$ and define
\[
 \overline K=K+c_\sigma(1+49B^2)\log(16/\delta),\qquad
 q=c\left[\overline K+\log\frac{n+2}{\delta}+1\right],
\]
\[
 e_n=\min\{n^{-2},\sigma/(4\sqrt n)\},\qquad
 L=H+q+1+\log\frac{d+1}{e_n}.
\]
At every eligible compiled width and depth, with probability at least
$1-\delta$, the posterior-averaged raw risk and the risk of its ordinary
mean are at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:raw-compiler-matched-bound}
 \sigma^2+\frac{c(1+q)}n
   \left[\overline K+dHL^2+H^2L^3+\log\frac{n+2}{\delta}\right].
\end{equation}
The constants depend on the fixed scalar prior laws and $\sigma$.
An expected-risk bound is given by the same expression with
$\delta=n^{-8}/16$, plus $c(1+B^2)n^{-3}$.

In particular, at fixed $d,h,B,\sigma$ and input regularity, put
\[
 C_n=\lfloor n^{1/(24bh)}\rfloor,\qquad
 H\le n^{1/12},\qquad N\ge n,
\]
with the same lower learner-depth thresholds as before.  The expected
excess above $\sigma^2$ is $\widetilde O(n^{-1/2})$.  For every fixed
confidence $0<\delta<1/2$ the same rate holds with probability
$1-\delta$; the displayed bound retains the dependence for varying
confidence.  The prior family and likelihood variance are fixed.  The architecture
may follow any prescribed schedule in these ranges; once it is prescribed,
the prior has no additional sample-size dependence.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The comparison law from Theorem~\ref{thm:raw-realizable-compiler} has
entropy at most $K$, pointwise mean squared discrepancy at most $49B^2$,
and population discrepancy at most $n^{-4}$.  These are exactly the
population-comparison hypotheses of the matched-Gaussian-likelihood
theorem in~\cite{rawprior}.  The normalized readout supplies a native
Gaussian supremum tail, and the Student-hidden dictionary has logarithmic
cardinality at most $c(dHL^2+H^2L^3)$.  The cited theorem combines a
comparison lower bound on evidence, Gaussian likelihood-ratio tests on
this finite dictionary, and the exact likelihood-ratio expectation
identity to control the prior-sieve tail.  It gives
\eqref{eq:raw-compiler-matched-bound} for the actual untruncated posterior.
Its expectation version uses conditional Gaussian-process regression at
the readout to bound the failure-event raw risk, as stated there.

For the growing-class substitution,
$K=\widetilde O(C_n^{2bh}+H)=\widetilde O(n^{1/12})$.
At fixed confidence, or at the displayed polynomially small confidence
used for expectation, $q,L=\widetilde O(n^{1/12})$.
Thus $(1+q)H^2L^3=\widetilde O(n^{1/2})$, and the other numerator terms
are smaller.  Division by $n$ proves the rate.  This is a noisy-realizable
result for the regression function and an exactly matched Gaussian noise
variance; it is not an agnostic fixed-variance oracle.
\end{proof}
