\section{An independent-coordinate raw comparison with a logarithmic width cost}
\label{sec:independent-raw-bits}
A related guarantee preserves independence of all weight coordinates.
Use tanh hidden activations. Every hidden coefficient of fan-in $F$
has the time-$1/F$ increment law of the symmetric process
\[
 \nu_{\rm hid}(du)=
 \frac{c\,du}{|u|(1+|u|)^{p_0}},\qquad p_0>2.
\]
First-layer fan-in is $d+1$, and later matrix fan-in is $N$.
Native later biases use time one. The readout coordinates instead use
\[
 V_j=\Gamma(c_{\rm out}/N,1)-\Gamma(c_{\rm out}/N,1),
\]
with independent gamma variables, and the readout bias uses the
same law with $N$ replaced by one. All actual coordinates are
independent. Both likelihood and prediction use the raw affine
readout. In particular
\[
 \sup_x|z_w(x)|\le |b_w|+\sum_j|V_j|
 \ \le_{\rm st}\ \Gamma(4c_{\rm out},1),
\]
so the native raw-tail exponent is one, independently of width
and depth.

Use the analog-prefix/digital-block notation of
Lemma~\ref{lem:raw-bit-comparison}, including its good input set,
but count only nonzero teacher edges in $E_{\rm pre},E_{\rm dig}$.
Let $V_{\rm pre},V_{\rm dig}$ be the selected hidden-vertex counts,
and let $E_1$ count first-layer input coordinates. Bias positions
are included in the corresponding resource counts $Q_{\rm pre}$ and
$Q_{\rm dig}$, which may conservatively be taken to be their full
dense selected counts. The ideal bit readout has variation plus
bias at most $4M$, $M\ge1$.

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-position independent comparison]
\label{lem:independent-raw-bit-comparison}
At every prescribed finite $N\ge m$ there is a sample-independent
law $\rho$ with
\[
 \rho\{\|z_w\|_\infty\le6M\}=1,\qquad
 \E_\rho\|z_w-g_*\|_{L^2(P_X)}^2\le CM^2(\delta^2+\xi).
\]
Its relative entropy is at most
\begin{align}
 C Q_{\rm pre}
 &\left[(J+2)\log\{C(d+m+2)(B_0+2)\}
                      +\log(1/\delta)\right]\notag\\
 &+C(Q_{\rm dig}+m+1)
      \log\frac{C(Q_{\rm pre}+Q_{\rm dig}+m+2)(k+1)(M+1)}{\delta}
 \notag\\
 &+C(E_{\rm pre}+E_{\rm dig}+m+1)\log(N+1)
       +C E_1\log(d+1)+CM.
 \label{eq:independent-raw-bit-KL}
\end{align}
Here $E_{\rm pre}$ in the learner-width term may omit first-layer
input edges. The bound shown uses fixed positions; no cancellation
of vertex-location costs is claimed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use fixed selected neuron positions for the teacher, which is
possible whenever $N\ge m$. For a hidden coordinate of fan-in $F$,
the small-jump construction gives a centered background law
supported on $[-\epsilon,\epsilon]$ with
\[
 \E|W|\le C\epsilon/F,\qquad
 \E W^2\le C\epsilon^2/F,\qquad
 \KL\le (C/F)\log(C/\epsilon).
\]
A designated coordinate of value $a$ has a law supported within
$C\epsilon$ of $a$, with entropy at most
\[
 \log F+C\log\{C(1+|a|)/\epsilon\}.
\]
One direct construction suppresses jumps larger than
$\epsilon/C$, conditions the remaining symmetric sum to have
magnitude at most $\epsilon$, and inserts one jump uniformly near
$a$. For $|a|=O(\epsilon)$ use two separated signed intervals with
probabilities chosen to give mean $a$. The symmetric small-jump
law has the displayed first and second moments. The designated
jump intensity on an interval of width proportional to $\epsilon$
is at least $c\epsilon/[F(1+|a|+\epsilon)^{p_0+1}]$, which gives
the entropy bound. For a variance-gamma coordinate the same
argument adds $C|a|$ instead of the logarithmic magnitude cost.
These are precisely the scalar comparison bounds of the mixed
hidden/readout prior.

In each selected hidden row, put the designated law on its
nonzero teacher coefficients and the background law elsewhere.
The total background absolute magnitude has expectation at most
$C\epsilon$ and second moment at most $C\epsilon^2$.
Since tanh features are bounded by one, these estimates hold for
the uniform functional contribution of that background, regardless
of the input law or of dependence between preceding features.

The prefix discrepancy therefore obeys the same uniform RMS
recurrence as the ordinary fixed-depth comparison:
\[
 e_\ell\le C m(B_0+2)e_{\ell-1}+C(m+1)\epsilon.
\]
At first layer the error is at most $C(d+1)\epsilon$.
Choose its precision so that, with probability at least $0.99$,
all selected final prefix features differ from their teacher
values by at most $\delta/4$.
The logarithm of the required inverse precision is bounded by
the first bracket in \eqref{eq:independent-raw-bit-KL},
up to its already displayed logarithmic dimension factors.

For the digital block choose
$\epsilon\le c/[(V_{\rm dig}+1)(m+1)(k+1)]$.
The selected-coordinate perturbations and the expected uniform
background magnitudes sum to at most $k/1000$ over all its rows.
With probability at least $0.99$, every row perturbation is at
most $k/8$. Integer margin and tanh saturation then restore
bit error at most $\delta$ at every depth, by exactly the
margin induction in Lemma~\ref{lem:raw-bit-comparison}.

At the raw readout use the variance-gamma designated laws around
its nonzero coefficients and bias, at precision
$c\delta/[(m+1)(M+1)]$, and background laws on the remaining
coordinates. The selected absolute coefficient sum plus bias
is at most $4M+C\delta$. The inactive absolute contribution is
at most $\delta/100$ on a further event of probability at least
$0.99$, by its first-moment bound. On the intersection the raw
supremum is at most $6M$, and the integrated squared discrepancy
is at most $CM^2(\delta^2+\xi)$.
Condition on this intersection. As before its constant probability
changes entropy by at most a fixed factor and a numerical constant.

Every nonzero designated internal or readout edge pays at most
$\log N$, whereas a whole selected background row pays only
$C\log(C/\epsilon)$ after summing its $N$ coordinate costs.
The first-layer designated edges pay $\log(d+1)$.
Hidden coefficient magnitudes enter only logarithmically.
The sum of all readout magnitude costs is at most $CM$.
Adding these terms gives \eqref{eq:independent-raw-bit-KL}.
\end{proof}

Under the raw-tail theorem's marginal sub-Gaussian label assumption,
the likelihood schedule has $T_n\asymp n^{2/3}$ and the expected
oracle remainder is $O(n^{-1/3})$ times this comparison entropy,
plus its approximation remainder. In particular the continuous
quantizer and restoring-bit compiler, with only its output bits
padded through extra layers, give
\[
 \widetilde O\!\left(
    [C^{2bh}+H]\log(N+1)\,n^{-1/3}\right)
\]
at fixed input dimension, teacher depth and input regularity.
Here $b$ is the compiler's fixed exponent, the learner width must
exceed its explicit compiled width, and the continuous learner depth
must exceed its logarithmic quantization threshold.
The tilde hides powers of $\log n$ and fixed regularity constants.
For polynomially bounded learner width the additional factor
$\log(N+1)$ is only logarithmic. This is an independent-coordinate
result at one prescribed dense architecture; unlike the
neuron-gated result, its quantitative resource is not uniform
over arbitrarily large learner widths.

For example, for any fixed $q\ge1$, every prescribed $n\le N\le n^q$
and
\[
 C_n=\lfloor n^{1/(12bh)}\rfloor,\qquad H\le n^{1/6},
\]
with the same lower depth requirements, give a coefficient-one
expected-risk comparison with the best width-$C_n$ raw ReLU teacher and
remainder $\widetilde O(n^{-1/6})$.  The first-layer and hidden-bias
conventions above are part of the prior specification.

\paragraph{A fixed likelihood for a realizable noisy target.}
Suppose instead that $Y=f_0(X)+\zeta$, with conditionally sub-Gaussian
noise of proxy $\sigma^2$, and $f_0$ is represented by the comparison
class with $|f_0|\le B_f$ on the input support.
Put $M=\max(1,B_f)$ and use a single fixed temperature
\[
 T\ge\max\{8\sigma^2+1,49M^2\}.
\]
Apply the compiler and this comparison at sufficient polynomially
small precision so that
$\E_X\E_\rho(z_w-f_0)^2\le n^{-4}$.
Its support bound gives
$\E_\rho(z_w(x)-f_0(x))^2\le49M^2$.
The fixed-temperature population-comparison raw theorem therefore
applies with native tail exponent one. If $K_n$ denotes the right
side of \eqref{eq:independent-raw-bit-KL}, increased to at least one,
both the posterior-averaged risk and the raw posterior-mean risk
satisfy
\[
 \E_S R\le R_0+
 \frac{C K_n\{T+[K_n+\log(n+2)+1+B_f]^2\}}n.
\]
Its high-probability version adds the corresponding confidence
logarithms. At fixed noise and output bounds this is
$\widetilde O(K_n^3/n)$, with unchanged independent coordinates,
unchanged labels, and fixed Gaussian likelihood variance $T/2$.
This is a realizable noisy-target conclusion, not a fixed-temperature
sharp agnostic oracle.
