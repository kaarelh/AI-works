\section{Readout tails and prescribed approximation budgets}
\label{sec:raw-extensions}
The Gaussian readout is the primary model.  Two variants explain which
part of the statistical penalty is intrinsic to the proof and which part
can be improved with additional information or a different prior.

\subsection{Power-law exponents in a light-tailed readout}
Fix $p>1$.  Replace the final Gaussian factors, including the output bias,
by independent variables of density proportional to $e^{-|u|^p}$, and
replace $\sqrt{P_j}$ by $P_j^{1-1/p}$.  The hidden Student/gamma layers
are unchanged.  The corresponding raw-tail and comparison lemmas in
\cite{rawprior} give
\[
 \|\sup_x|z_w(x)|\|_{L^q(\mu)}\le c_pq^{1/p},
\]
and the readout comparison cost becomes
$c_p[(\sum_j|v_j|)^p+|b|^p+1]$.
The constants may depend on the fixed $p$; no uniform limiting claim as
$p\to\infty$ is made.  The restoring-bit comparison carries over with
these same changes.

Set $a_p=p/(p+2)$.  With deterministic temperature
$T_n=t_p n^{2/(p+2)}$, the main theorem becomes
\[
 \E R(\widehat f_S)\le\inf_{g\in\mathcal G_{C,h}}R(g)
 +\widetilde O\big((C^{2bh}+H)n^{-a_p}\big),
\]
at fixed input dimension, teacher depth, input regularity, and label
envelope, with the same compiled-width and depth requirements.  The
additional factor $M_n^p$ is only logarithmic in $n$.  The exact resource
statement replaces $M_n^2$ by $M_n^p$ in $\mathcal B_n$ and changes
constants by factors depending on $p$.

For example, one may take
\[
 C_n=\lfloor n^{a_p/(4bh)}\rfloor,\qquad
 H\le n^{a_p/2},\qquad N\ge n,
\]
with the previous lower depth thresholds.  The remainder is then
$\widetilde O(n^{-a_p/2})$.  At $p=4$, this permits
$C_n=n^{1/(6bh)}$, $H\le n^{1/3}$, and remainder
$\widetilde O(n^{-1/3})$.  This is an explicit alternative readout prior,
not a conclusion that changing the variance alone improves the Gaussian
readout's general agnostic rate.

\subsection{When the approximation and complexity budgets are known}
Suppose now $Y=f_0(X)+\xi$, where $|f_0|\le B_0$ and the conditional
noise is centered and $\sigma^2$-sub-Gaussian.  Write
$\mathcal A(g)=\E(g-f_0)^2$ and $R_0=\E\xi^2$, so
$R(g)=R_0+\mathcal A(g)$.  An optimistic raw-posterior theorem in
\cite{rawprior} uses prescribed upper bounds on comparison entropy and
approximation error.  For the normalized Gaussian readout its
statistical form is
\[
 \E R(\widehat f_S)\le R(g)
 +\widetilde O\left(\frac{K^2+K\sigma^2}{n}
                         +K\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal A_0}{n}}\right),
 \qquad \mathcal A(g)\le\mathcal A_0,
\]
with deterministic temperature of order
$K+\log n+(B_0+U+1)^2+\sqrt{n\mathcal A_0}+\sigma^2$,
where $U$ bounds the comparison output and $K$ bounds its relative entropy.
The approximation budget excludes the irreducible label-noise floor.
The prescribed temperature is a different rule from the universal
$T_n=t_0\sqrt n$ in the main theorem.  No adaptation to unknown $K$ or
$\mathcal A_0$ is inferred from this statement.  Applying this refinement
to population-accurate digital comparisons requires its explicitly
supported-comparison version; a uniform-approximation hypothesis cannot
be replaced silently by a population one.
