ROUND 3 THEORY NOTES -- initial consolidated version

Preferred condition
-------------------
The strongest defensible primary theorem uses clipped-score stability with
clip at the TRAINING margin, +/-1/2, score variation <=1/16, and radius
  c/sqrt(1+(L-1) N ||v||^2).
This is strictly weaker than the old clip-at-1 condition, for a principled
reason fixed by the likelihood rather than by empirical outcomes. Training
signed output >1/2 maps to clipped signed score exactly 1/2; approximation
error <=1/12 still puts the candidate at >=5/12, where the ramp is zero.
Test signed output <=1/4 maps to candidate <=1/3, where the ramp is one.
No clean-margin restriction is imposed on unobserved inputs.

All c>0 are allowed
------------------
The old hidden Boolean cylinder is retained. Increase the readout coefficient
from 2 to a=max(2,256c). Its readout energy is >=a^2 C/8, making the calibrated
radius no larger than the old per-layer noise tolerance 1/(64 sqrt(2C)).
Its noisy signed outputs remain >1. The Gaussian target exponent changes
only through the readout term 2a^2 C. Sample complexity is polynomial in
c+c^{-1}, as well as d,L,C,error^{-1},log(delta^{-1}). Thus .03 and .1 are
legitimate stress parameters. No empirical calibration choice is required.

Target-class limitation
-----------------------
C is the width of a layered BOOLEAN circuit with constant wires counted and
logical depth fitting the actual hidden depth. This is not automatically the
width of a small real-valued NN circuit. Digit tasks need not have a useful
Boolean-C bound, and empirical verification of the condition does not by itself
supply one. The statement covers a robust implementation for each Boolean
circuit, not every implementation. The posterior theorem is not a theorem
about the predictor returned by ordinary gradient descent.

Why the radius is interpretable
-------------------------------
For a nonexpansive hidden stack, independent hidden injection sites have total
linear gain bounded by sqrt((L-1)N||v||^2), and scalar output noise adds the 1.
Thus any finite perturbation in this calibrated ball changes raw output by at
most c. In particular, c<=1/16 automatically certifies all nonexpansive stacks.
The radius is normalized by readout energy, rather than arbitrary raw logit
scale, and uses per-layer RMS hidden perturbations, compatible with cloning.
Finite-tube or diagonal quadratic-metric certificates can allow expansion and
contraction, saturation, and output-invisible directions. A clean Jacobian
alone is insufficient unless a whole finite tube is verified.

Optional valid weakenings (not changes to the agreed primary experiment)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Only demand stability for trajectories staying inside [-1,1]^N at every
   hidden layer. The compressor's successful virtual activations are tanh
   outputs, so it automatically produces such trajectories. Target cylinder
   still qualifies. Negative attacks for this variant must check the bounds.
2. Exclude scalar output noise from the prior condition. With compression
   parameter r<=1/30, account for its output injection as an approximation
   error: 1/16 + r/2 + r/8 <=1/12. A cleaner hidden-only radius is
   c/sqrt((L-1)Ev), with the zero-readout case interpreted separately.
   Same target construction and polynomial proof apply. Keep this a corollary
   unless experiments are explicitly revised in advance.
3. Freeze a trusted region using an independent unlabeled pilot, then validate
   its input-distribution mass independently. Gaussian-condition on stability
   throughout that region; error adds its excluded mass. Certifying only the
   pilot/validation points does not certify a larger region.
4. Permit network-specific exceptional sets from a fixed width-independent
   low-VC family. Independent unlabeled validation plus uniform control on the
   training sample gives the defect bound 3rho+epsilon with ordinary sign or
   margin likelihood. This is a real distributional relaxation, but empirical
   evidence must certify failure containment in the region family, not merely
   observe a few failures. Round-two proof was independently audited.

What current evidence would and would not establish
--------------------------------------------------
A global certificate establishes membership for that trained parameter array.
A sound pointwise certificate on heldout inputs only establishes those points;
a binomial bound can estimate that fixed network's population failure rate.
It does not establish the entire Bayesian posterior's defect class or its
training defect. A trained-network average is not a posterior sample average.
Gradient-based attacks give lower bounds on instability; unsuccessful attacks
are not certificates. Float arithmetic with loose margins is persuasive
numerical evidence, but is not an interval-arithmetic proof.

Raw outputs must be assessed before any rescaling. Every finite-cube network
with no zero outputs eventually enters calibrated clipped support after large
positive readout amplification. The cost is quadratic readout energy and can
be enormous, so this does not show ordinary training naturally finds a useful
low-energy member. Raw numerical stability does not share this automatic
amplification effect.

Unpromising automatic replacements
----------------------------------
Ordinary parameter flatness does not imply hidden-state robustness: previous
fragile-chain examples have an open gradient-descent basin and benign local
parameter geometry while failing this condition. Random dropout/noise success
is weaker than adversarial RMS stability. A margin-relative radius that shrinks
to zero at uncertain inputs does not provide the common inverse-polynomial
radius used in compression; controlling those small-radius inputs requires
an additional trusted-region or complexity assumption. General ReLU/residual
architectures require their own prior/compression/target-mass extension.

1. stability-certificates.tex
   Global exact suffix-spectral condition, diagonal-metric refinement,
   and a finite-radius clean-Jacobian certificate with explicit curvature
   and a continuity barrier. Pointwise finite tubes are distinguished
   from global certificates. Conservative certificate membership is not
   silently made a stricter prior support condition.

2. margin-band-variant.tex
   General odd monotone 1-Lipschitz saturated score transform theorem.
   Optional U(z)=sign(z)min(.25,(|z|-.25)_+) uses identical constants and
   is weaker than clipping at training margins. Among such score maps
   preserving the full .25 train-test gap, it is least restrictive in
   the precise support-inclusion sense U=soft_threshold_B composed F.
   This is optional, not a retroactive change to the primary experiment.

3. distributional-only-obstruction.tex
   Explicit abstract prior on sparse patches. Population instability rho
   and two-element compression outside exceptions coexist with empirical
   instability 1 and poor ordinary consistency-posterior risk until a
   width-dependent target prior mass penalty is overcome. This is NOT a
   lower bound for the specified Gaussian neural-network prior.

4. naturalness-gd-counterexample.tex
   Exact full-batch logistic-GD trajectory: two equally likely correlated
   Boolean inputs, d=2,N=1. Every iterate is correct and exactly stable
   in clipped/margin-band score throughout the entire data support, yet
   all iterates violate full-cube stability at sufficient depth. At c=.001,
   H>=16 suffices for clipped-score and H>=20 for margin-band failure.
   Every positive finite learning rate is allowed. Arbitrary unbalanced
   stochastic batches and prevalence under random initialization are NOT
   claimed. The adversarial hidden trajectory stays in the valid cube.

5. analog-target-digital-simulation.tex
   Global margin>=.5, width C, depth L, weights<=R yields a Boolean circuit
   with size/depth poly(d,C,L,log(2+R)). Explicit weight precision and
   rational Taylor evaluation avoid an oracle for real tanh. This explains
   coverage of analog targets after polynomial architecture overhead,
   without preserving original depth or the original minimum-width bound.
