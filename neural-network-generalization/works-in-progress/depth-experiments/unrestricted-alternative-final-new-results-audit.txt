PASS: unrestricted-critical-scalar-expansion.tex
  Gaussian inflation 4q gives variance increment 2q^2+O(q^3) and entropy
  4q^2+O(q^3). Summing and using a final partial inflation gives a bridge
  from a to b costing <=2(b−a)+Cb^2. A near-optimal path of cost <2 must
  cross a small q, and its first crossing b satisfies b<8q by the Gaussian
  second-moment entropy bound. Prefix cost >=2(b−a), combined with the
  q-to-b bridge and the remaining value V(b), cancels the overshoot and
  proves V(q)=V*−2q+O(q^2). The proof does not require monotone paths.
