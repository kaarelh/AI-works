\paragraph{A second smooth-region exclusion check.}
The zero-first-state construction also fails to enter the proposed
smooth-image region for the fully fitted model 301, despite its smaller
first hidden width of 128. In a separately declared postprotocol
diagnostic, we minimized anisotropic pixel total variation subject to
a black outer two-pixel border, mean ink at most $1/5$, and zero first
affine preactivations. The numerical optimum was $287.0476$, well above
the region's threshold of $150$. An exact rational dual certificate,
including a box bound for every stationarity residual, proves
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(p)\ge287.04761391129
   -\frac{\|\lambda\|_1}{2}
             \|W^1\bar x\|_\infty
\]
with the displayed decimal rounded downward from the saved rational
bound, where $p\in[0,1]^{784}$ and $x=2p-1$.
In particular, every image in the specified region has
$\|W^1\bar x\|_\infty\ge0.26750111067$, again rounded downward.
Nearest eight-bit rounding of the numerical minimizer gave total
variation $287.0353$ and maximum first-preactivation residual
$0.00425048$; none of the 4,097 declared rounded candidates entered
the region. The exact exclusion concerns the zero or near-zero
first-state mechanism. It is not a proof of output stability throughout
the smooth-image region and does not exclude other adversarial inputs
there. The numerical search and exact verification are recorded
separately in \texttt{tv-zero-diagnostic-301.json} and
\texttt{tv-exclusion-301-exact.json}.
