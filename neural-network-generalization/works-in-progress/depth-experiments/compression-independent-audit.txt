1. ENTROPY MOMENT AND SCALINGS
For standard Gaussian G_D, KL variational inequality with |Z|^2/4 gives
E|Z|^2<=4 KL+2D log2. Standardized blocks are sqrt(d)W1/t,
sqrt(N)Ws/t, and Nv/t. Thus all physical moment normalizations in the
construction are correct. In particular E[N|v|^2]<=4K+2log2.

2. FUTURE-ENTROPY LEMMA
E_A' KL(Q_Y|A'||P_Y)=KL(Q_Y||P_Y)+I(Y;A'). Data processing through
(A,J,H), H=f(A,J,X), yields the bound by
KL(Q_Y||P_Y)+I(Y;A)+I(Y;X|A)+H(J|A), which is at most B+H(J|A)
by the KL chain rule. No density for the constructed prefix is required.
The canonical fresh source indices and Gaussian coefficients depend on Y
only through the transmitted A,J,H, as required.

3. ACTIVE-DIRECTION RANK
Conditional on A,Y, each greedy query is chosen after averaging over
active-coordinate values, so its identity depends only on A,Y, not on
H's realized values. Each added orthogonal direction contributes its
conditional whole-N-vector KL to the Gaussian chain rule. Rank is bounded
by t^2 R/(N delta). A basis is recovered from A and the finite input list
by fixed Gram-Schmidt. Gaussian Talagrand supplies squared RMS transport
<=2 delta after multiplying by residual feature length <=1. Projection
and disintegration preserve the stated joint marginals.

4. MESSAGE BUDGET
A list of at most q Boolean inputs, length information, and an abort flag
fit the stated [(d+1)q+2]-bit code. N>N0 and N0>=ceil(LD0/K) imply
NK/t^2+sD0<=2NK/t^2 because t<=1. Markov on the conditional rank gives
abort probability <=eta with q>=2K/(e eta).

5. FIRST-LAYER AND ACTIVE-MATRIX CLIPPING
First-layer row zeroing affects expected squared RMS activation by <=e,
using bounded activations and the physical row moment. For active H, use
the success INDICATOR in the entropy moment estimate, not a conditional
success law. This gives E[|H|_F^2/N;success]<=8K+2q=V. Clipping rows at
BH gives <=4V/BH^2=e activation RMS error. Clipping every row before any
future-dependent selection avoids an illicit maximum over width.

6. SOURCE SAMPLING AND COVARIANCE
For u(j)=sqrt(N)U_j, E uu^T=I. Whole-vector clipping c gives E cc^T<=I
and E|u-c|<=q/T. The chosen m dominates the entrywise/vector/kernel
Hoeffding bounds and their finite-cube union factors. Dividing sampled
vectors by sqrt(1+a) guarantees D^TD<=I, hence I-DD^T is PSD.
The reconstructed row covariance is exactly t^2(Khat-psihat^T psihat).
Both psi and psihat have norm <=1, so covariance error <=a+2 zeta.
The scalar Gaussian same-normal coupling bounds variance contribution by
t^2 times that covariance error. Couplings can be extended to full row
coefficients by disintegration and assembled across independent Gaussian
rows. No simultaneous-in-input transport is claimed or needed.

7. GAUSSIAN COORDINATE CLIPPING
Each xi coordinate has variance <=1, even though coordinates in a row
are correlated. Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the output contribution by the sum
of coordinate squared tails; independence across coordinates is not used.
The bound 3m exp(-BG^2/4)<=e is valid. All coordinates are clipped before
later row selection. Every final row is therefore bounded independently
of N. The four transport contributions yield <20e by the four-term
squared triangle bound.

8. READOUT
The original readout marginal remains Q. Ev>K0 has probability <=eta.
On Ev<=K0, truncating |Nv_i|>Bv changes any bounded-feature evaluation by
<=K0/Bv=r/16. Uniform source sampling contributes another <=r/16 except
probability eta, with the stated m and finite-input union bound.

9. FULL ORIGINAL MARGINAL AND CANONICAL PREFIX MARGINAL
The comparison coupling need not preserve the canonical joint law with
all discarded original matrices. It preserves both (a) the complete
original Q marginal and (b) the canonical current-prefix/future marginal.
At each step, extend the new coupling by the old conditional law of past
original matrices given incoming A,X,Y. This preserves the incoming full
history and previous prefixes, while obtaining the required outgoing
A',Y marginal. The next entropy step uses (b); the final original-network
comparison uses (a). These requirements are compatible.

10. NOISE BUDGET
Abort probability <=2L eta=.02 alpha. Expected hidden squared RMS summed
over layers gives the Markov failure probability <=160Le/r^2=.16 alpha.
On success hidden noise squared <=r^2/8 and output injection <=r/8, so
total squared norm <=9r^2/64<r^2/4. The combined exceptional probability
is <alpha/4, stronger than the stated alpha. The virtual computation is
exactly the original network with these postactivation injections.

11. SMALL WIDTH AND GRID
For N<=N0, the global physical-weight norm bound gives an event of
probability >=1-alpha/4 on which the original network is retained;
eta=0 is allowed. Zero-outgoing-weight padding preserves the output even
for an activation with phi(0) !=0. The chosen mesh gives output error
<=r/8 by the elementary layerwise Lipschitz recurrence. Although the
mesh is exponentially fine in depth, log grid cardinality is polynomial.
All widths pad to the same M=N0; all bounds are independent of t. Thus
there is one finite candidate class for all N,t.

12. NUMERICAL FORMULATION
Before rounding the candidate equals the original noisy output. Rounding
adds <=r/8. This proves the raw numerical lemma without using sign
robustness or a clean margin anywhere. It also explains why the successful
virtual hidden states stay in the valid activation range.
