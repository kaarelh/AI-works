1. Counting obstruction. The Boolean target events are disjoint and their
   union is the global-margin event. The finite-width upper bound with
   only 2m prefactor therefore forces every uniform lower-mass overhead
   R to satisfy R >= m log 2 - log(2m) - E_{N,L}. The bound on the number
   of functions with overhead at most r follows by the same summation.
   More generally, if mass(f) >= exp(-N V_* - B_f) for every f, Jensen
   also gives average_f B_f >= m log 2 - log(2m) - E_{N,L}.
   In particular, E=o(m) rules out all-functions lower masses with
   uniform o(m) overhead. This is a genuine incompatibility, not merely
   a limitation of a particular finite-width coupling. It establishes
   neither learning nor a posterior sample lower bound.

2. Short scalar paths. Near-optimality bounds every prefix entropy by
   V_*-V(q_i)+p. Within [u,2u), all but at most one exiting transition
   therefore have total entropy at most 35u; strict dissipation charges
   at least c u^2 per step. Summing dyadic bands yields O(1/p) steps and
   O(log(1/p)) summed variances. The terminal large-variance band costs
   only a universal number of steps. Both pre- and post-state sums are
   correctly treated.

3. Residual spectral control. Conditional Gaussian integration by parts
   gives Cov(h,G | xi)=a(xi)R, and covariance of the linear-regression
   remainder gives Cov(h | xi) >= a(xi)^2 R, including singular R.
   Jensen gives R' >= (1-q')^2 R. Entropy at most three bounds the scalar
   preactivation second moment by 12+2 log 2 <14; the residual raises it
   by at most one. This gives the stated uniform derivative floor and,
   with the dyadic sum and delta<=p, polynomial spectral loss.

4. Exact-target purification. At epsilon=0 the tracked component is at
   most v. At epsilon=16(v+r), the first-Hermite bound puts it above v
   for sufficiently small universal p0. Continuity supplies an inflation
   fixing it exactly, with entropy <=256v^2. Conditional Gaussian
   Poincare gives r' <= r E sech^4(sqrt(u) Z) <= (1-v)r, after reducing
   p0. PSD residual and constant diagonal are preserved by conditioning.

5. Sharp common-rate theorem. With delta=B1 D/L and
   p=B2 D log(2L)/L, cooling/growth, purification, and the scalar suffix
   fit into a fixed specified depth after choosing universal constants.
   The cold holding interval is valid because the growth and purification
   lengths are prescribed independently of holding time. Purification
   leaves residual <=delta(2L)^(-4), so scalar lifting costs O(L^-4).
   All remaining excess costs sum to O((D^2+D log^2(2L))/L).
