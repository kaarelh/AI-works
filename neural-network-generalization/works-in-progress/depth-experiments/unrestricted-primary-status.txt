UNRESTRICTED GAUSSIAN LEARNING: ROUND 3 STATUS
Updated 2026-10-17 23:05 UTC. Main artifact unrestricted-paper.pdf (41 pages).
All source inputs local to this directory. No Lean certification or GitHub publication.

MODEL/SCOPE
Tanh hidden layers, linear readout, augmented-input bias only. Original raw Gaussian conditioned on all-cube output margin >1/2 and noiseless sample signs. All positive original-prior results fix zeta>0 and cover gamma>=1+zeta. They cover every actual width N>=C_L(f), with C_L the bounded-physical-weight minimum width at that learner depth. Statements are statistical, not efficient posterior sampling. Gamma=1,t=1 remains open.

MAIN NEW RESULTS
1. Finite-scale posterior representation ceiling. For L>=3 and any consistently labeled n-sample, at all widths above poly_zeta(d,n,L,log(2/beta)), posterior mass >=1-beta consists of Boolean functions with SAME-DEPTH globally unit-margin representatives of PF^2 <= d^-1 exp(19)(2T)^(3/2), T=poly(d,n). At L>=O(log(d+n)), one added layer also gives energy L+O(log(d+n)). This supplies a genuine target-relative approximation-barrier reduction, but no explicit small deep circuit meeting the barrier is known.
Files: unrestricted-primary-posterior-ceiling.tex; unrestricted-primary-margin-transfer.tex.
Audits: fresh PASS original theorem; alternatives PASS same-depth PF sharpening.

2. Halfspaces at every learner depth >=2, independent original geometric margin or coefficient precision; uses primary-source-verified Goldmann--Karpinski depth-two majority simulation. All fixed-depth arbitrary-real threshold circuits likewise positive, with (d+s)^{O_zeta(h)} poly(L,stats). An NC1 superpolynomial-sample counterexample of the stated growing-depth kind would separate NC1 from TC0.
Files: unrestricted-primary-halfspaces.tex; unrestricted-primary-threshold-circuits.tex.
Audit: fresh PASS including classical theorem source applicability.

3. Growing fan-in-two circuit improvement. Size s, depth h, S=max(16,2+d+s): sample S^{O_zeta(1+h/loglogS)} poly_zeta(L,eps^-1,log(2/delta)), with constructed target depth q<=max(2,h); hence all learner L>=max(2,h) covered. Blocks of loglogS original levels flatten to two threshold levels. Formula balancing gives exp(O_zeta(log^2 S/loglogS)) independently original formula depth, at learner L>=O(logS/loglogS).
Files: unrestricted-primary-boolean-blocking.tex; unrestricted-formula-balancing.tex.
Audits: alternatives PASS blocking and exact-depth refinement; fresh PASS formula balancing.

4. Target PF sufficiency. Any globally unit-margin depth-D0 target of PF<=P, arbitrary original width/individual-weight scales, is learned with sample poly_zeta(d,D0,P,L,stats) at every actual width, if L>=D0+c log(2+d+D0+P), or alternatively L>=2D0. A norm-preserving width compression theorem yields polynomial width while PF increases by at most factor2. Compression also holds uniformly on a bounded continuous input ball.
Files: unrestricted-primary-product-targets.tex; unrestricted-primary-width-compression.tex.
Audits: fresh PASS product-target construction; fresh+alternatives PASS width compression; alternatives PASS bounded-ball extension.

5. Depth-independent PF Boolean normal form. A globally unit-margin Boolean target with PF<=P at ANY finite depth/width depends on at most P^2 coordinates and has depth,width,weights,PF,dyadic description all poly(P), apart from coordinate indices. Thus log|H_P|<=poly(P)+P^2 log(d+1), with one all-depth realizable generalization event. Proof uses GRS-style rank-one bottleneck plus a new-for-this-purpose bias-free odd scalar sign staircase and exact prefix-norm cancellation. The general rank-one/depth-independent complexity idea is credited to Golowich--Rakhlin--Shamir 2018.
Files: unrestricted-primary-depth-saturation.tex; dependency unrestricted-alternative-product-normal-form.tex.
Audits: TWO independent full PASS audits (fresh, alternatives). Fresh audit in unrestricted-alternative-depth-saturation-audit.txt.

6. Intrinsic excess energy. Define P_*(f) as the infimum of unit-margin PF over every finite depth and width. Always J_{L,N}(f)>=L+log d+2log P_*. For L>=poly(P_*)+c log(d+1), N>=poly(d,P_*), J_{L,N}(f)<=L+c log(2dP_*), with polynomial physical weights. Uses a representation within factor2 of the infimum, not attainment. Consequently polynomial statistical learning in d,P_*,L at all actual widths and those learner depths. The all-linear normalization branch is explicitly handled by tanh+readout2 and shallow scalar transmission.
File: unrestricted-primary-functional-energy.tex.
Audit: fresh final PASS after affine-branch and scope clarifications; recorded in unrestricted-alternative-functional-energy-audit.txt.

7. Quantified small mean-field prefactor window. At sigma^2=t^2/N, inverse-polynomial t suffices for the positive witness classes, all actual widths. Original critical t=1 explicitly excluded. All-depth counting removes extra sample-depth factors at widths with energy witness K<=L+B independent-depth B; sufficient t and smaller-width fallback still depend on L.
File: unrestricted-primary-small-prefactor.tex.
Audit: alternatives PASS original theorem and new counting refinement.

ADDITIONAL INTEGRATED RESULTS
Fourier spectral norm A(f) polynomial implies shallow and all-depth polynomial learning (alternatives author, fresh PASS).
A finite sample-window target-corruption lemma patches sample disagreements with a shallow approximant (fresh author, primary PASS).
Exact cloning of a balanced binary tree can retain quasipolynomial PF despite polynomial absolute path sum; explicitly only a representation-preserving method obstruction, not a function or posterior lower bound (fresh PASS).

UNRESOLVED
No polynomial PF bound for every ordinary small deep circuit, no explicit target-relative small-circuit lower bound, and no general original critical gamma=1 learning theorem. Even a polynomial all-depth PF representation would retain specified learner-depth qualifications in the current positive argument. Capacity/energy obstructions have not been promoted to sample lower bounds. The original all-cube output-margin conditioning remains throughout.
