Independent audit: unrestricted-primary-width-compression.tex and its
integration with unrestricted-primary-product-targets.tex.

Verdict: PASS. No substantive gap found.

The proof does not rescale tanh homogeneously. It instead drops saturated
large rows, linearizes genuinely small normalized rows, samples the
middle rows without changing their incoming weights, and implements the
linear branch by replicated small-amplitude tanh neurons.

Detailed checks:

* Prefix incoming norm is at most S R because every preceding activation
  is zero-preserving and one-Lipschitz; no later biases are allowed.
  Suffix Lipschitz bound P_*/(S A) is valid even when V is hidden, since
  its following activation is also one-Lipschitz.

* Large-row count and bounded activation give output loss P_*/B=e.
  Small-row cubic error gives P_* tau^2 R^3/3=e. Middle deletion costs
  at most P_* R A_m/A, so the threshold Delta is correctly normalized.

* For p_i=(b_i/a_i)/Z, sampled incoming/outgoing squared Frobenius norms
  are exactly c Ahat and Ahat/c, where c=m/Z. This equality survives
  repeated indices. Bounded Hilbert-valued differences give a tail with
  no dependence on either adjacent layer dimension. The stated m has
  ample constants for both the scalar norm event and all finite-input
  vector approximation events.

* The small-row linear map has nuclear norm <=sum a_i b_i. Its rank-k
  operator residual is <=A/(k+1). For its square-root SVD factorization,
  K replicated channels give actual tanh error at most
  c nu^2 (S R)^3/(3K), before the remaining suffix. If the middle branch
  is sampled, Z>=A_m S^2/B^2 and A_m>=Delta A give
  c nu S^2<=m B^2/Delta. If deleted, the chosen c makes this quantity
  exactly one. Hence K has no original-width or individual-weight term.

* Concatenating the two branches with the same c gives pair product
  Ahat+nu <=(1+eta)A by Cauchy--Schwarz. The next compression alters the
  next layer width without re-expanding an earlier compressed width.
  Every intermediate full product remains below 2P0. The at-most-six
  local output errors sum below epsilon. The zero-matrix stop is valid.

* Q, m, k and K have universal polynomial degrees in P0,D0,R,epsilon^-1
  and log(2|X|). There is no degree depending on D0 and no original
  dimensional parameter hidden in concentration or SVD estimates.

* On the augmented Boolean cube, compression error 1/4 leaves signed
  margin >=3/4. Doubling the readout leaves unit margin and product <=4P0.
  The product-target theorem then removes individual-weight dependence
  through small-prefix linearization and balanced cloning. Its T and
  admissible base width are polynomial in the compressed parameters.
  Therefore the claimed O(log(d+D0+P0)) extra depth and the alternative
  L>=2D0 condition are both sound, including all actual admissible widths.

Scope: necessity from the posterior ceiling concerns approximating
functions with low product norm; this sufficiency uses an exact Boolean
representation and extra depth. The statement correctly avoids claiming
an equivalence or deriving a polynomial product bound from circuit size.
