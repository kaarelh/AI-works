# Public experiment subset

The [public reproduction guide](../REPRODUCTION.md) describes the files that
are actually included in this repository. The full original research archive is
larger and is not silently represented as available here.

Every protocol and all 70 final outcomes are included. `all-model-record-summaries.json`
also retains every training history and explicitly omits per-example output arrays.
Original weights and full raw records are included for models 105, 500, 504, and 505.

[ORIGINAL-EXPERIMENT-README.md](ORIGINAL-EXPERIMENT-README.md) is the historical
documentation for the complete research archive. Its statements about all stored
weights, raw records, and caches describe that complete archive, not this curated
public subset. Follow the public guide first.
