*Published record of the final adversarial check of the folder's README, its verification index and the two entries in the collection README, made at finalization on the texts of commit a40d92b6. Its issues, numbered R1–R55 (plus X3 from the cross-reference check), are listed with their ids in [`../checks/final-consistency/final-readme-issues.json`](../checks/final-consistency/final-readme-issues.json); [`final-readme-resolutions.md`](final-readme-resolutions.md) records how each was resolved, and [`final-readme-edit-check.md`](final-readme-edit-check.md) the independent check of those edits.*

Checked: `NNG/works-in-progress/agnostic-learning/README.md` sentence by sentence against `research-notes/` (problem-brief, A, B, C, E, small-learner-width, depth-two, red-team, literature); `verification/README.md` against the verdict lines and issue lists of every report; the agnostic-learning row (line 147) and the "Label-noise research" paragraph (line 224) of `NNG/README.md`. No file was edited. Every quote below was checked to occur exactly once in its file.

**Verdict: major_flaw** (4 major for reporting, the rest minor). The numbers, constants, thresholds and result numbers of the README are almost all correct, and every relative link resolves. The major problems are: both NNG/README.md entries attribute noise memorization (and, in the row, the 0.48 excess) to hard tube conditioning, which A says does not fail that way; the README's intro item 7 states that the depth-two excess is only polylogarithmically small, which is unproved at c=1 and false for c <= nu/log n; and §9 "What holds" restricts only the finite-scale half to clipped predictions, implying that unclipped predictors (the posterior mean of z_w) are proved for theta < 1/H in the strong-prior limit.

## Major

### 1. NNG/README.md, agnostic-learning row (line 147): hard tube conditioning does not memorize noise
> At depth at least three, conditioning tightly on each training output, as the clean-label likelihood scaling and hard tube conditioning do, memorizes label noise at large learner width: on the noisy Boolean cube with about $2^d$ samples the test error exceeds the zero network's by about $0.48$.

A §0.1: "TUBE_rho does not fail by memorizing noise." On the fair noisy cube TUBE has no 0.48 excess: with rho <= 1 the tube is empty (under the fallback convention (U2) the output is the Bayes-optimal zero function), and with rho > 1 the strong-prior posterior collapses to the zero network (A Table 6.1). TUBE's failure is on non-centred labels (excess eta_0^2), at every depth L >= 2, every width and tolerance, already at a fixed input dimension (Props 6.6, 6.6a). The second-round scope referee raised the same point (verification/A-scope.md, N1). The agnostic README itself says "for a different reason: its tube is empty or biased". Also "by about 0.48" is a lower bound beta(1)-o(1), shown with d = log2 n.

Fix: "At depth at least three, the clean-label likelihood scaling, and every likelihood variance $c\sigma^2$ with $c$ up to about $n^{1-1/H}$, memorizes label noise at large learner width: on the noisy Boolean cube in dimension $d=\log_2n$ with about $2^d$ samples the test error exceeds the zero network's by at least about $0.48$. Hard tube conditioning also fails, for a different reason: on labels that are not centred its tube is empty or centred at the labels' midrange, at every depth, width and tolerance, already in a fixed input dimension."

### 2. NNG/README.md, "Label-noise research" paragraph (line 224): same misattribution
> At depth at least three, the clean-label likelihood scaling and hard tube conditioning fit the noise at large learner width and fail every polynomial sample bound that is uniform over input dimensions.

TUBE does not "fit the noise"; its failure is not tied to depth >= 3, large width or growing dimension (A §0.1, Props 6.6–6.6a).

Fix: "At depth at least three, the clean-label likelihood scaling fits the noise at large learner width and fails every polynomial sample bound that is uniform over input dimensions. Hard tube conditioning fails for a different reason, at every depth, width and tolerance and already in a fixed input dimension: on labels that are not centred, its tube is empty or centred at the labels' midrange."

### 3. README intro item 7 (line 21): the depth-two excess is not proved to be small
> At depth two, where memorization is expensive, close conditioning still fails the polynomial sample bound, but with an excess that is only polylogarithmically small (§8).

depth-two.md Cor 5.2 proves a lower bound gamma(lambda)(18dc+1)^{-2}(1-o(1)) for 0 < c <= poly(d); it is a constant for c <= nu/log2 n and beta(lambda)-o(1) when c log n -> 0. So for the closest conditioning the proved excess is constant. At c = 1 only the lower bound is polylogarithmically small; whether the excess itself tends to 0 is open (depth-two §0; README §8: "the lower bound tends to 0, and the known upper bound does not"). The sentence states an unproved upper bound and contradicts the constant regime.

Fix: "At depth two, where memorization is expensive, close conditioning still fails the polynomial sample bound for every $c\le\mathrm{polylog}(n)$: the proved excess is at least of order $(dc+1)^{-2}$, a constant when $c\le\nu/\log_2n$ but only polylogarithmically small at $c=1$, where whether the excess itself tends to $0$ is open (§8)."

### 4. README §9 "What holds" (line 168): the strong-prior claim lacks "clipped"
> The tempered learner of §4 competes with every depth-$L$ network of width $m$ and weight bound $B$, at every learner width, with a sample size polynomial in $d$, $m$, $B$, $1/\varepsilon$ and $\log(1/\delta)$, and, for clipped predictions, the same holds at the repository's finite prior scale

B Thm 6.3 (theta < 1/H) covers only clipped predictions; the posterior mean of z_w (the brief's BMA) and its clip are proved only for theta < 1/L (B Thm 6.4) and are open on 1/L, 1/H) (B §0 "Which posterior mean"; README §11 item 5). Because only the finite-scale half says "for clipped predictions" and the bullet ends "provided ... theta < 1/H", the reader infers that every predictor works for theta < 1/H in the strong-prior limit. "Every learner width" should be N >= m.

Fix: "For clipped predictions, the tempered learner of §4 competes with every depth-$L$ network of width $m$ and weight bound $B$, at every learner width $N\ge m$, with a sample size polynomial in ..., and the same holds at the repository's finite prior scale for $\theta<\theta_{L,\gamma}$ ... (§7). Unclipped predictions, including the posterior mean of $z_w$, are proved only for $\theta<1/L$, in the strong-prior limit." End the bullet with "... with $\theta<1/H$ (clipped predictions)."
