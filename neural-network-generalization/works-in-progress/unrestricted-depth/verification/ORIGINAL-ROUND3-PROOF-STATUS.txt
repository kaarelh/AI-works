# Proof-status ledger

All statuses below refer to the current research drafts and independent mathematical/code audits. They are not Lean-certified. The three assembled papers are [robustness-paper.tex](robustness-paper.tex), [unrestricted-paper.tex](unrestricted-paper.tex), and [critical-paper.tex](critical-paper.tex).

**Open boundaries.** Neither a joint polynomial unrestricted theorem for all small deep circuits nor a counterexample to such a polynomial bound is proved. Polynomial ordinary circuit size is not shown to imply polynomial intrinsic product norm; the sufficient results also retain their learner-depth thresholds. Approximate representation necessity is not an exact-representation equivalence. Critical flattening does not imply a uniform conditioned prior: small function-specific rate gaps survive the width-first limit, and a global-mass counting obstruction rules out the proposed all-functions lower masses with o(2^d) logarithmic overhead. Empirical attacks refute particular global supports; empirical positives certify their exact domains and radii. Neither alone establishes a distribution-wide learning claim or a training-prevalence claim. Output rescaling can force finite-domain support at potentially large energy cost and is not a polynomial prior-mass argument.
