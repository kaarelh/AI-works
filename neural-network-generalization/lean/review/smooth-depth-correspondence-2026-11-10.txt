Actual distribution and smoothness
---------------------------------
JitterGeometry defines radius 1/(100*20^r), the compact jitter subtype,
and the actual sphereArc(scalarCoding+u) map. JitterKernel constructs a
normalized C-infinity compact bump probability, including its normalization
and the identification between real and subtype laws. SmoothDensity proves
that the scalar law has the convolution density, integral one, and compact
support in [-.65,.65]. ArcLength constructs both angular charts, proves
unit speed, circle coverage off the two endpoints, disjointness, total
arclength 2*pi and the exact change of variables to graph coordinates.
CircleDensity defines a globally smooth, nonnegative function on R^2;
smoothInputLaw_arclength_density identifies its restriction as the actual
Radon--Nikodym density of smoothInputLaw r. Thus smoothness is not merely
assumed, and scalar Lebesgue measure is not renamed arclength.

Quantitative geometric facts
---------------------------
JitterGeometry and JitterLower prove sharper coding bounds .51<=|t|<=.64.
Together with the global 20-Lipschitz expansion, these keep the same real
teacher labels within [.5,.65] in absolute value after jitter. They also
keep their distance from .6 times their sign at most .1. These sharpenings
allow reuse of the previously verified exact and mean-field witnesses.
The teacher remains width three, depth r+1 and bounded by 30.
JitterSeparation proves the unchanged interval Frostman estimate and the
same 49/50 separated-sample probability with the literal Delta_n.
Pairing uses the same jitter on both members. Distance remains at most
(13/5)*3^(-r), while target difference is at least .98. Prediction of both
labels within 1/4 therefore requires an output difference of at least .48.
JitterTail pays the exact changed moment factor (625/576) using the slack
in the previous squared-distance bound, preserving the tail exponents.

Main conclusions
----------------
* smooth_strong_prior_depth_lower_bound: the same exp(r/100) sample range,
  literal M_n=interpolationWidth n, N>=max(30,M_n), fixed-width small-scale
  limit, 49/50 sample probability, and one-half confidence-error mass.
* BatchTwoContinuousDepthPower.smooth_finite_power_depth_lower_bound and
  smooth_power_prior_depth_lower_bound: the same all-scale power family
  sigma^2<=N^(-(1+zeta)), polynomial sufficient width, and common measurable
  sample event. The width resource here is the already proved sufficient
  polynomial threshold, not an identification with the paper's displayed
  finite-ceiling threshold.
* smooth_exact_minimizer_all_width_event: exact minimizers exist and every
  probability mixture has regression risk >=1/2 and confidence-error mass
  >=1/2 even at confidence threshold 1/2, simultaneously at all widths.
* smooth_iterated_limit_all_width_failure: actual weak limits exist in the
  specified order (scale to zero first, then tolerance to zero), every such
  limit is supported on exact energy minimizers, and obeys the same two
  failure conclusions on one measurable 49/50 event for every width.
* BatchTwoContinuousDepthCritical.smooth_critical_manuscript_depth_lower_bound:
  the actual gamma=1 prior, width sent to infinity at fixed depth and sample,
  exponential sample range, and simultaneous one-half limiting regression
  and strict-confidence failure. smooth_critical_common_sample_exponent
  also permits a positive exponent <=1/100. No finite width rate is claimed.

Important coverage distinction
------------------------------
The existing alternative proof uses an explicit inverse scalar channel,
linear-in-depth energy and direct Gaussian pair moments. It supplies the
main lower-bound claims above, but does not establish every auxiliary
statement in the manuscript. In particular it does not yet prove the
exact Lambda_n=exp(17/2)/sqrt(2)*T_n^(3/4) Lipschitz ceiling asserted for
all exact minimizers in cor:exact, or the general bounded-label memorizer
with K=2+(L+14)*T^(1/(L-1)), global bound one, and its product estimate.
The finite-scale low-energy representative lemma with its literal displayed
threshold is also not supplied by the alternative direct-pair argument.
These are genuine remaining quantitative coverage gaps for both D and D_r;
they are recorded rather than silently replacing the paper's statements.
The smooth-density construction itself and the lower-bound conclusions
are proved; full cor:smooth coverage awaits these quantitative statements.
