Conclusion on semantics
-----------------------
The reviewed final construction has the intended unconditional theorem shape.
No mathematical defect or hidden compressor/realizability assumption was found
in this review. Publication is additionally contingent on the exact closed
main declaration passing the Lean type/axiom gate and the independent build.

1. Exact model and support condition
-----------------------------------
The weights have the literal finite independent Gaussian law smallPrior,
with variances N*sigma^2/d, sigma^2, and sigma^2/N for first-layer, internal,
and readout entries. The first layer has a constant input coordinate; internal
biases must be implemented as ordinary counted wires. There are r+1 hidden
tanh layers and a linear readout (total matrix depth L=r+2).

The support event quantifies over every Boolean input and every simultaneous
finite postactivation disturbance, including a scalar readout disturbance.
Its squared budget is sum_s ||eta_s||_2^2/N + eta_out^2. Clipped output at
+/-1/2 may move by at most 1/16 inside the actual network-dependent radius
c/sqrt(1+(r+1)*N*||v||_2^2). This is an adversarial finite-disturbance condition,
not a Jacobian condition, a Gaussian-average condition, or an on-distribution
condition. Disturbed hidden states are not artificially confined to [-1,1].

The formal posterior is the actual Gaussian conditioned on the intersection
of that support event and the observed strict signed-margin constraints
f(x_i)*z_w(x_i)>1/2. It does not impose a global clean-output margin. A proved
two-stage identity identifies it with conditioning the robust prior on the
training event. Positivity is an explicit conclusion, backed by the actual
target-cylinder probability bound; it is not assumed.

2. Quantifiers, comparator, and prediction convention
----------------------------------------------------
RobustPolynomialDepthGeneralization places one positive universal coefficient
A and one natural exponent m before all d,C,r,c,epsilon,delta,D,n. In particular,
neither exponent nor coefficient may depend on depth, width, target, scale, or
input distribution. Its sample envelope is
 A*(2+d+C+r+c+c^-1+epsilon^-1+log(1/delta))^m.
The base is positive on the stated domain, so the sample condition and the
high-probability event are nonvacuous.

The comparator is an actual LayeredBooleanCircuit with C slots at each of
r+1 stages, using NAND, copy, and negation, with an explicitly counted constant
wire. Its existence implies C>=1. It is not an arbitrary analog network of
neural-circuit complexity C. Padding a smaller logical circuit must count
retained wires and any required constant wire. An arbitrary distribution D
on the Boolean cube is allowed; uniform inputs are not required.

One measurable sample event, of probability at least 1-delta, works
simultaneously for every integer learner width N>=C and every sigma>0 with
N*sigma^2<=1. This is stronger than separately choosing a good event for each
width or scale. In t=sqrt(N)*sigma coordinates it is exactly 0<t<=1, hence
includes the finite gamma=1 scale and all gamma>=1 exponent scalings at
positive integer widths. No width-first limit is needed.

The conclusion concerns the full posterior mixture: Gibbs probability of
signed output <=1/4 is at most epsilon/3, and the D-mass of inputs whose
posterior probability of signed output >1/4 is <=2/3 is at most epsilon.
The confidence implication is proved by integrating 1-p(x), so it does not
assume concentration on a single predictor. Sign correctness is a weaker
requirement; the margin guarantee controls it as well. Equalities at both
thresholds are explicitly counted as failures in the exact statement.

3. No hidden final compression assumption
----------------------------------------
RobustMainReduction alone is correctly labeled a conditional reduction:
its premise LargeWidthRawCompression is not the final learning theorem.
RobustCompressionLargeWidthProof.largeWidthRawCompression supplies that
premise from an arbitrary original probability law Q and its literal KL
bound relative to smallPrior. Its remaining parameters are the scalar
compression resources and the large-width cutoff. It does not retain a
score approximation, target-mass, robustness, or selected-law hypothesis.
RobustMainProofs.robustPolynomialDepth applies the reduction directly to this
proved raw theorem; its declared type is exactly the transparent proposition
RobustMainStatements.RobustPolynomialDepthGeneralization. The arbitrary
measurable-space parameters used in some intermediate finite-message lemmas
are instantiated with the discrete finite measurable space, not retained as
a premise of the raw or final learning theorem.

The complete history construction calls exists_resource_selectedRun on the
original Q. At each stage the selection is from the actual conditional law
produced by the preceding canonical transition. The terminal law is selected
before the per-input histories. Therefore the candidate marginal is common
to all test inputs; only the coupling and virtual path vary with x.

The per-layer ProbabilityCoupling is instantiated from selectedLayerJoint.
Its left marginal is the actual old state law, including full original
current rows and unconsumed future. Its right marginal is the actual
selectedNext canonical state law, via the proved full-field observation and
state decoder identities. There is no replacement of a Gaussian coefficient
law by an unproved equal law on a bad-bank event: both constructions use the
same totalized state on abort. Pointwise future-tail alignment and the
implication that new-alive implies old-alive are supplied to the history
induction explicitly.

The normalized local live cost is the genuine hidden discrepancy with
physical matrix scale t/sqrt(N)=sigma; its bound is 10/E. The raw full-field
squared-error estimate is divided by N, matching the RMS disturbance budget.
Cost is charged only on successful paths; failures are counted separately.
The initial genuine first-layer clipping history costs at most 1/E. The
induction therefore accumulates (1+10*r)/E, without an exponential-depth
recurrence. It preserves the full original weights in every glued history.

The resource invariant pays for finite selected messages in conditional
future entropy, keeps actual coefficient bounds, and accumulates abort
probability by 2/H per hidden step. In particular, conditioning on the future
is not silently replaced by a posterior independence assumption. Terminal
entropy is allowed to double the original energy envelope; the terminal
readout-energy failure is correctly budgeted as 2/H rather than H^-1.
The final sampled-readout law and the actual terminal histories yield
RawPathCompression with exact original and compact marginals.

4. All widths and polynomial bookkeeping
----------------------------------------
The raw compressor is only needed above its explicit polynomial width
cutoff. At smaller widths, direct Gaussian moment bounds and deterministic
padding/rounding supply the same finite candidate class. The final all-width
split includes the equality case in the small-width branch and has no gap
between C and the cutoff.

The finite code, its size bound, all target-cylinder costs, readout
truncation, and reciprocal robustness radius are substituted into the
proved scalar polynomial estimates. mainCompressionSampleBudget_polynomial
has universal A,m outside all parameters. The finite-class sample event is
chosen from this common code before the later width/scale/posterior choices.
Thus neither a union over an uncountable scale set nor a hidden depth-dependent
exponent is being used.

5. Relation to the manuscript and publication limits
----------------------------------------------------
The exact formal statement matches the qualitative principal theorem of
main-results/robust-polynomial-depth/source/robustness-theory.tex, after the
harmless rescaling of epsilon used to state an epsilon confidence guarantee.
The formal proof uses a looser Pinsker-and-energy compression route and a
looser concentration/sample envelope. It does not certify the manuscript's
sharper transport constants or every quantitative parameter formula.

The manuscript also asserts a version with additional all-input clean
absolute-margin conditioning. The target-mass lemma for that extra event
is formally proved as robust_boolean_target_mass_with_global_margin.
At the mathematical level the full learning extension is sound: the same
target cylinder remains inside the new event, the raw conditioning-mass
lower bound is unchanged, and the entropy compressor applies to the modified
posterior. Nevertheless this changes the posterior and is not the literal
RobustPolynomialDepthGeneralization theorem checked by the main gate.

The margin-band, localized-exception, output-rescaling, unlabeled-pool,
input-neighborhood, alternative-activation, and empirical extensions in the
paper are not all covered by that gate. Nor does it establish that ordinary
gradient descent samples this posterior or commonly produces the global
support condition. The empirical certificates have their own computational
checks, not an implication from this Lean theorem.

The manuscript introduction at robustness-paper.tex lines 97-99 currently
states that its arguments are not Lean formalizations. After the exact gate
passes this should be replaced by a narrow statement about the qualitative
main theorem and the above explicit scope limits.

7. Label-noise FAQ review
-------------------------
The FAQ's added label-noise discussion correctly separates finite-class noisy
Occam learning from the actual hard-conditioning posterior. Contradictory
labels at one input can make that posterior undefined. For independent
symmetric flips eta<1/2 the identity noisy risk = eta+(1-2*eta)*clean risk
is correct, as is the stated loose finite-class Hoeffding sample bound.
With independent input-dependent flip probabilities <=eta<1/2, the noisy
excess risk relative to the clean target is >=(1-2*eta)*clean error, giving
the analogous argument. The fixed-n flip-likelihood ratio bound
((1-eta)/eta)^n cannot overcome a positive prior-rate gap Delta kappa divided
by 2*sigma^2 as sigma tends to zero. Thus the warning about taking the
strong-prior limit first is mathematically appropriate. None of these FAQ
observations is being included in the exact robust main theorem.
