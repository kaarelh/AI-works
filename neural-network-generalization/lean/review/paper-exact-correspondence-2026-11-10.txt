1. source/memorization.tex, exact bounded-label memorization lemma.
   exact_bounded_label_memorization proves the literal width
     M = ceil(max(4,n,3*d*n*R^2)), R=max(2,delta^-1*log(16*n)),
   for every n>=1, d>=1, L>=3 and N>=M. Labels have absolute value
   at most 7/8, and a unit projection places the sample in [-1,1]
   with pairwise separation at least delta. The theorem permits the
   slightly weaker assumption sum u_i^2<=1. The constructed actual
   dense weights interpolate exactly, have global output bound one,
   and energy at most K=2+(L+14)*T^(1/(L-1)), T=4*M*n.

   StepPartition proves a nonnegative partition of unity for ordered
   tanh steps. StepInterpolation proves the sample map is within 1/8
   of the identity and constructs its inverse with norm at most 8/7.
   StepNetwork builds the actual shallow weights and padding. ChannelAnalysis
   proves the needed artanh derivative, concavity and squared bound.
   NormalizedChannel proves the actual finite composition inverse preserves
   [-1,1]. VariableChannel and ChannelSemantics identify dense-network
   output and energy with that composition. TentSchedule, TentRadii and
   PaperSchedule prove the explicit depth budget. PaperMemorizer handles
   arbitrary sample order and nonmultiple cloning widths. No inverse,
   network simulation, energy estimate or global bound is an assumption.

   The proof uses a tent-ratio estimate k+8 rather than the proof's k+7;
   a sharper initial energy bound pays for this. The final stated K, M,
   T and depth range are unchanged.
