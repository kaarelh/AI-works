Compression formalization audit (2026-10-18)

The main formal proof does not assume an entropy-compression oracle. The actual
construction consists of measurable finite query selection from the actual
conditional current-block law, an actual finite query message, iid source-bank
sampling, and an explicit Gaussian residual kernel. All canonical reconstruction
laws are independent of the test input. The comparison law may depend on that
input, but its candidate marginal is exactly the common canonical law.

Important formalization details:

1. The current-block law is conditioned on both the canonical prefix and the
   entire remaining original future. It is not assumed independent of either.
   Future entropy grows by at most the logarithm of the finite message count.

2. The Gaussian transport estimate is implemented through an elementary two-scale
   argument. In the small-variance regime it uses the actual entropy second-moment
   bound; in the large-variance regime it uses the constructed sequential scalar
   maximal coupling and Pinsker bound. The selected residual KL budget is
   N*delta^3/t^2, and the actual activation discrepancy is at most 14*N*delta.
   This changes the polynomial exponent, not the stated polynomial conclusion.

3. Zero residual directions have an independent dummy Gaussian. This avoids the
   false claim that a deterministic zero residual has finite KL against a
   nondegenerate standard Gaussian, while preserving the full original field.

4. Gaussian covariance is totalized on bad source banks only inside the comparison
   construction. The literal canonical coefficient law remains unchanged. Its
   decoded state agrees exactly with the comparison law because both branches
   enter the same absorbing abort state off the good event.

5. The path error is charged only on successful states. Arbitrary aborted paths
   are not claimed to have small unconditional squared disturbance. The final
   failure bound separately includes the abort probability and the successful
   disturbance Markov bound.

6. The actual full original row-and-dummy comparison cost is bounded in
   RobustCompressionSelectedRawCostResources.actual_selected_raw_cost_resources.
   RobustCompressionSelectedLocalCost.selectedLayerJoint_liveLocalCost_le pushes
   this exact law through the actual old/new state decoder and divides by N,
   obtaining integral liveLocalCost <= ofReal(10/inverseLayerError).

7. The readout discrepancy is included in the full reconstruction cost. The final
   allocation reserves hidden cost radius^2/8 and readout error radius/8, rather
   than incorrectly spending the complete radius^2/4 on the hidden path alone.

The local-cost theorem was checked with Lean 4.32.0 and built through Lake.
The theorem is unconditional apart from the actual current-law entropy bound and
its actual PrefixSelection, both supplied by the separately constructed recursive
program. The final main theorem must instantiate this recursive program and must
not retain LargeWidthRawCompression as a hypothesis.
