The latest extension changes Lean proofs, formalization documentation, and
verification records only.
All papers and their statements are unchanged.
Scope: the sixteen papers with main formalizations (the original five,
the second batch of ten, and conditional nonpolynomial learning). No new
formalization project was started for the other papers. The accompanying
OOD research proof is conventional mathematics, not a new Lean coverage claim.

The review compares the model definitions, hypotheses, quantifier order,
prediction rules, literal budgets, and main conclusions with paper statements
and abstract claims. A successful Lean build and semantic correspondence are
separate evidence. This record does not certify every auxiliary display or
informal proof in the collection.

Model checks
------------
The Boolean input encoding, augmented constant input, depth L=r+2, actual
Gaussian variances, all-input strict output margin, exact label conditioning,
positive posterior denominators, actual bounded-weight teacher complexity,
and strict-confidence threshold were checked. Posterior sampling, majority
prediction, and the sign of a posterior mean are different rules. Width-first
and scale-first limits were not interchanged. IID, uniform IID, permutation,
and adversarial-order protocols were kept separate. Continuous-domain results
are regression statements on the actual stated input laws, not Boolean-cube
classification statements.

Main correspondence across the sixteen papers
---------------------------------------------
1. Strong-prior generalization: main statement and abstract fixed-depth
   polynomial learning claim match. The scale threshold may depend on width
   and precedes the input law, sample count, and failure probability.
   NEW: norm/teacher-complexity polynomial comparisons and norm-sublevel
   counting in NormComplexityComparison.lean.
   NEW (second extension): ParameterSensitivityMain and NormBallPacking prove
   the literal sensitivity Lambda^2=((d+1)/d)R^{2(L-1)}+sum_{j=0}^{L-2}R^{2j}
   and packing count (1+2R Lambda)^P, P=N(d+1)+(L-2)N^2+N. Compactness supplies
   weak-margin representatives at the strict-margin energy infimum; no false
   strict-infimum attainment premise is used. General nonpolynomial-activation
   circuit simulation and the growing-depth norm-ball appendix remain open.

2. Gamma above one: main all-width finite-scale statement matches, for each
   fixed gamma>1. The polynomial may depend on gamma and depth. The inverse
   raw-prior-mass-to-complexity comparison is now proved, in both directions,
   in PriorMassComplexity.lean. The inverse polynomial is uniform over every
   learner width N>=1, with no teacher-width restriction. Positive raw mass
   is explicit because Lean defines log(0). The forward direction uses the
   actual cylinder polynomial for all gamma>=1 and N>=C.

3. Mean-field all-width: main statement matches, including one sample event
   for risk <=epsilon/3 and strict >2/3 confidence outside epsilon mass.
   It is tanh-specific. No extra minimum width beyond teacher complexity.

4. Subcritical exponential: main statement matches, including constants
   chosen before gamma<1 and width-first limits. The parity teacher already
   has a closed polynomial-complexity realization. Sharper separate negative-
   gamma constants are now covered: SubcriticalNegativeSharp proves a 3/8
   confidence-failure fraction with sample probability at least 3/4, including
   zero samples and the finite-width liminf conclusion for every gamma<0.
   NEW (third extension): SquaredLipschitzProcess proves the full row-dependent
   squared-process inequality over the entire unit ball, with the actual maximum
   row norm and Euclidean operator norm. No homogeneity of the response functions
   is assumed. ArbitrarySquaredSampling proves the printed C*h_n*(1+C*h_n)
   expectation and n>=A*D*log(2D) probability bound for arbitrary probability
   spaces, measurable features with an almost-everywhere norm bound, and
   isotropy. A is chosen before the space, dimension, law, and response.
   OneSidedSquaredSampling permits arbitrary dependence between the sign label
   and the feature. ParitySquaredSamplingFull proves the uniform coercivity
   event for every degree-r Walsh polynomial at any requested failure level.
   These standalone squared-sampling coverage gaps are now closed.

5. Robust polynomial depth: the circuit-width mismatch is now removed.
   The circuit definition requires a true bias wire only when a NAND gate
   consumes it; padding copies the designated circuit slot instead of forcing
   an extra constant output. Both robust and improved target-cylinder proofs
   have been rebuilt with the same counted width and resources. Width-one
   literal/copy/negation examples are checked in RobustCircuitWidthOne.
   NEW: RobustSpectralCertificate proves the actual suffix-product finite-noise
   bound, exact support ratio, bounded-suffix and contractive cases.
   RobustDiagonalCertificate proves the diagonal quadratic-form certificate
   with literal Q_M=N*sum(max diagonal entry). RobustOutputRescaling proves
   eventual calibrated clipped stability by positive readout rescaling for
   every finite-cube network with nonzero clean outputs. RobustIntervalCertificate
   proves the clipped endpoint test for input-dependent raw intervals.
   NEW: RobustSparsePatchObstruction proves the sparse-exception prior's exact
   consistency-posterior error lower bound for every sample, including duplicates,
   together with its two-constant compressor and exponential target-mass form.
   This is an abstract-prior obstruction, not a Gaussian NNBayes lower bound.
   NEW: UnlabeledStabilityPool proves one pool event for every supported finite-KL
   posterior, including posteriors selected using subsequent labels.
   RobustUnlabeledMain closes the actual input-adapted Gaussian learning theorem:
   universal polynomial labeled complexity independent of width and scale, and
   the printed unlabeled budget 8*(D*/sigma^2+log(2/delta))/epsilon. Target mass,
   entropy, readout truncation, the common candidate law, pointwise stability
   charges, and confidence consequences are all proved. Both global clean-margin
   conventions are covered. RobustSemisupervisedPool proves the shared-pool
   protocol without an independence assumption between the two good events.
   The localized-union theorem and other variants listed below remain open.

6. Unbounded/Lipschitz activations: the main strong-prior and uniform-band
   statements match their stated existential polynomial budgets.
   NEW: GELU bounded-complexity consequence, exact power-prior/band identity,
   all-gamma>=1 band membership, and simultaneous confidence consequences at
   the manuscript budgets in BatchTwoActivationCorollaries.lean.

7. ReLU/GELU polynomial depth: main claims match with the manuscript's explicit
   counted constant-one wire. Existing sharp scales and width-growth results
   are retained. This is not a theorem for arbitrary unbounded activations.

8. Improved original-support all-scale learning: the exponents C^6 log^13 and
   the degree-46 polynomial conclusion are covered. The constant-wire issue
   is now fixed through the target-cylinder construction, including literal
   small widths. The leading finite-concentration factor was improved from
   200 to the printed 16,
   through all scale and width-growth corollaries. This is 16 times the FORMAL
   entropy resource: the paper's literal J and compression-parameter allocation
   are still not proved. The numerical-budget gap is therefore only narrowed.

9. Shallow sharp bounds: the main three explicit budgets match.
   NEW: readout L1 bound from weighted energy, norm-only sample budget, and
   quadratic-width resource bound for the sharper budget, plus an actual
   all-width/all-scale norm endpoint in BatchTwoShallowNormMain.lean. These
   are explicit inequalities, not a separately formalized Big-O statement.

10. Online upper bounds: the three actual-prior regimes and the permutation
    and infinite uniform-IID protocols match the main claims.
    NEW: arbitrary confidence thresholds, including 2/3 and worst-tie 1/2,
    and infinite-majority-count conversion, at the existing sharp neural
    budgets in BatchTwoOnlinePredictionMain.lean. No arbitrary-order upper
    bound is inferred.
    NEW (second extension): BatchTwoOnlineNonuniformMain proves the actual
    infinite-IID expected total bound 3+(h+1)(1+log T)+m beta, with
    T=ceil(p_min^{-1}log(2/p_min)), for arbitrary full-support finite input laws.
    BatchTwoOnlineAdverseOrder gives the explicit point-exception prior,
    per-round error >1-1/R, the actual posterior-draw expectation, and majority
    and confidence failures. It is not a Gaussian NNBayes OOD counterexample.
    NEW (third extension): BatchTwoOnlineBiasedRace/Order/Adverse prove the exact
    IID first-appearance race probabilities, high-probability adverse ordering
    for the explicit geometric input law, and actual posterior error and
    confidence failures on each first visit. Duplicate observations are handled.
    The general merging extension, some sampled-tail refinements, and optional
    ReLU formulas remain open.

11. Circuit converse: principal finite/permutation and IID statements match,
    with the explicit resource constants and the actual circuit syntax.
    Prefix coding has the proved Kraft/counting interpretation; an executable
    bitstream serializer/parser is not supplied by that theorem.

12. Neural polynomial-depth converse: main claims match the actual bounded
    gain setting and the stated order of limits. The optional operator-norm
    extension remains outside the proved endpoint.

13. Non-Gaussian priors: all three original main endpoints match the stated
    smooth, even, strongly log-concave coordinate laws.
    NEW: the broader two-sided quadratic-envelope strong-prior proposition
    is now proved in BatchTwoQuadraticEnvelope{Model,Rate,Main}.lean, for
    actual independent coordinate laws, not an assumed rate oracle. Densities
    may differ by coordinate, need not be even or smooth or log-concave, and
    envelope prefactors may depend on width. The energy exponents have a
    common bounded ratio. This proves fixed-width sufficiently-small-scale
    learning, not a uniform-in-width threshold.
    NEW (31 October): QuadraticTailEnvelopes and QuadraticTailRate derive
    arbitrarily tight global envelopes from positive continuous densities
    and their actual two-sided quadratic log-tail limits. The exact limit
    sigma^2 log mass -> -a*kappa and strict energy ordering now follow.
    No rate theorem or global envelope is assumed in that endpoint.

14. Continuous domains: both principal regression endpoints match.
    NEW: the existing low-energy-interpolant theorem is integrated; minimum-
    energy exact interpolants now exist by compactness and have the witness
    energy bound. One sample event controls all eligible widths, every
    minimizer and every probability mixture of minimizers, in
    BatchTwoContinuousMinimizers.lean.
    NEW (second extension): actual normalized Gaussian posteriors are tight
    as scale tends to zero. Every positive scale sequence has a weakly
    convergent subsequence. Every iterated scale-then-tolerance weak limit is
    supported on minimum-energy exact interpolants; both stages exist without
    uniqueness or regular-value assumptions. BatchTwoContinuousIteratedMain
    transfers the same simultaneous all-width learning event to every such
    limit. Joint paths and reversed limits are not asserted.
    BatchTwoContinuousSuccessMeasurability proves measurability of the literal
    posterior risk and confidence success events, including zero denominators.
    BatchTwoContinuousClassificationFullSupport closes the separate binary-
    margin-likelihood corollary: all N>=C, strong prior and every fixed gamma>=1,
    polynomial in B and inverse teacher margin, and the margin need hold only
    almost everywhere. Null-input labels need not be measurable. The learner
    conditions only on the original binary training margins. Its ordinary
    measurable-label success event is proved measurable in ClassificationMeasurability.
    BatchTwoContinuousScoreSigns proves the general score-to-sign confidence
    transfer with the additive near-zero target mass. The separate spherical
    anti-concentration constant is still not a checked endpoint.

15. Continuous depth lower bound: all three main regimes match the fixed
    nonatomic unit-circle input law and bounded width-three teacher.
    NEW: exact minimizers and every supported mixture have regression error
    at least 1/2 and confidence-failure mass at least 1/2 even at correct-
    probability threshold 1/2. A measurable event of probability >=49/50
    simultaneously controls every eligible learner width and every mixture,
    via the memorizer, compact energy sublevels and pairing barrier, in
    BatchTwoContinuousDepthMinimizers.
    NEW (second extension): BatchTwoContinuousIteratedMain proves existence
    and the same failure bounds for every actual iterated Gaussian weak limit.
    The varying smooth-density variant remains open. No uniform-sphere or
    Boolean-cube lower bound follows.

16. Conditional nonpolynomial learning: the main theorem matches the sole
    explicit complexity-class premise PPoly != TC1 and the actual positive-
    scale Gaussian model. No posterior-ceiling premise remains in the closed
    theorem. The statement is not an exponential-depth or zero-scale theorem.
    NEW: FixedHardFamily.lean chooses a polynomial-teacher hard family before
    the sample budget. Infinitely many dimensions have one input law common
    to every sample length from ZERO up to that budget, all eligible widths,
    and every scale schedule. GaussianOutputSymmetry proves exact one-half
    label confidence at zero samples and supplies the formerly missing case.
    NEW (second extension): ArbitraryPosteriorCeiling proves the auxiliary
    ceiling for every 0<beta<1, with the paper's logarithmic beta width slack,
    uniform over all labels, all m<=n including zero, and all eligible scales.
    It uses the actual raw Gaussian bad-mass and denominator estimates and
    the proved circuit-construction bound; no ceiling oracle is assumed.
