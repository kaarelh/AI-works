Literal distribution and teacher
--------------------------------
uniformSphere d is the law of G/||G|| for d independent standard real
Gaussian coordinates. Zero is assigned direction zero; it has zero mass
when d>0. uniformSphere_on_sphere proves unit-sphere support, and
uniformSphere_euclidean identifies this law with normalized stdGaussian.
uniformSphere_orthogonal_invariant proves invariance under every Euclidean
linear isometry. This is the normalized-isotropic-Gaussian construction of
uniform spherical probability; no finite discretization or restricted band
is substituted. No equality to mathlib's unnormalized Haar-to-sphere measure
is asserted or needed by the learning statements.

scoreTeacher has one hidden tanh unit, first input weight one, zero bias,
and readout one. scoreTeacher_bounded and scoreTeacher_output prove the
literal B=C=1, L=2 representation on every real input. inputBall is a
nonempty compact unit ball in the manuscript's coordinate type, and the
whole spherical probability law is supported there.

Anti-concentration
------------------
spherical_tanh_anticoncentration and its real-measure endpoint prove
D{|tanh(x_1)|<=tau} <= 3 sqrt(d) tau. Dimensions use d=n+1; the theorem
holds for all d>=1 and all tau>0, hence includes the paper's d>=2 and
0<tau<=1/2 without extra hypotheses. The proof differs from the printed
density calculation: tanh bounds reduce the event to a Gaussian cone;
conditioning on the other coordinates, a scalar Gaussian small-ball bound
and the actual Gaussian radial moment give the same literal constant 3.
For tau>=1/3 the probability-one bound suffices. Neither a spherical
anti-concentration premise nor an unproved Gaussian-density assertion is
used.

Actual learner and budget
-------------------------
spherical_score_learning_finite_scale covers every fixed scalar coordinate
law and gamma>=1, including gamma=1. spherical_score_learning_strong_prior
chooses its positive scale threshold after dimension, width and epsilon,
but before delta and sample size. Both use the literal independent-weight
prior and BatchTwoContinuous.posterior, conditioning solely on observed
real scores tanh(x_1), with rho=epsilon/(12 sqrt(d)). They prove that with
sample probability >=1-delta, the sphere measure of inputs at which the
posterior's correct-sign probability is <=2/3 is <=epsilon. All widths
N>=1 are allowed. There is no binary-label likelihood replacement or
additional global-margin conditioning.

The regression theorem is used at accuracy epsilon/2 and output tolerance
tau=epsilon/(6 sqrt(d)). The proved score-to-sign inclusion adds at most
epsilon/2 by the spherical estimate. The explicit common sample budget is
  (12/epsilon) [ A 30^m (1+d+1/epsilon)^(2m)
                 + 13(1+d+1/epsilon) + log(1/delta) ].
A>0 and m are chosen before d,N,epsilon,delta; they may depend on the fixed
coordinate law and (for finite scale) gamma. This is literally polynomial
in dimension and inverse accuracy and logarithmic in confidence, with no
N dependence. sphere_finite_budget and sphere_strong_budget dominate the
respective regression budgets. The main theorem's full posterior is used;
its finite-measure property follows from actual conditioning.
