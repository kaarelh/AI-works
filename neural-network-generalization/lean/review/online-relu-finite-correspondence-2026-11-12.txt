---------------------------

Exact center and perturbation
-----------------------------
The readout is doubled, the teacher is zero-padded to any N>=C, and its
layers are positively rescaled without changing its function. Each
weighted layer energy is K_R/L and the total is exactly
  K_R = L (4 d P_*^2)^(1/L).
Whitening proves each normalized layer norm equals sqrt(K_R/L).
The output of this concrete center has signed margin >1 everywhere.
The perturbation proof propagates each layer's individual Frobenius
error, then applies Cauchy–Schwarz to their sum. It gives exactly
  sqrt(2L) rho (sqrt(K_R/L)+1)^(L-1)
for combined Euclidean energy-coordinate displacement <=rho<=1.
Thus the literal r_R makes output error <=1/4 and preserves strict margin
>1/2. All these statements concern actual network evaluations.

Gaussian mass and tail
----------------------
An explicit coordinate box of halfwidth r_R/sqrt(parameter count) lies
inside the perturbation ball. Integrating the actual product Gaussian
density gives target raw mass >=exp(-B_R/sigma^2), with exactly
  B_R = (sqrt(K_R)+r_R)^2/2
        + S_R log(3 max(1,sqrt(S_R)/r_R)), S_R=d+L.
The proof uses parameter_count*sigma^2<=S_R, with no dimensional factor
lost in the exponent. The Gaussian square MGF yields
  Pr[energy>=T] <= exp(parameter_count log(2)/2-T/(4 sigma^2)).
At the literal T_R=4(B_R+(S_R/2)log2+log m), m=2^d, and sigma^2<=1,
the energy tail is <=target_mass/m. The actual disjoint margin events
transfer this to excluded function mass for the all-width energy class.
The exact capacity count v_R was proved in the preceding product batch.

The parameter count is N(d+2)+r N^2. Its variance product is <=d+L
for all N>=1 when gamma>=2; for r=0, gamma>=1 suffices. These inequalities
are formalized, rather than retained as hypotheses of the final endpoints.
