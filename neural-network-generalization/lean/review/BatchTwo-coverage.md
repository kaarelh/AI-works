# Second batch: ten formalized papers

> **30 October 2026 extension:** [The current coverage record](coverage-extension-2026-10-30.txt) documents new checked activation/confidence, online-majority, shallow-norm, quadratic-envelope, and exact-minimizer results. The rows below retain the earlier main-endpoint map; exclusions now closed by those additions are identified in the new record. The constant-wire and numerical-allocation gaps remain open.

The ten papers were selected for extending the activation class, improving the complexity bounds, controlling total mistakes and their converses, extending the priors and input domains, and establishing a depth obstruction. Their main learning statements are proved for the actual models specified in the manuscripts. The original five results retain their separate entry points and presentation.

The entry point is [BatchTwoMain.lean](../NeuralGeneralization/BatchTwoMain.lean). The [axiom audit](../NeuralGeneralization/BatchTwoAudit.lean) covers the main endpoints and imports the more detailed shallow, online and non-Gaussian audits. A passing kernel check and a comparison with the manuscript are separate checks: this table records the latter. The collection verification record records the fresh source build.

The [statement audit of 23 October](statement-audit-2026-10-23/README.md) independently reread the main statements and their definitions. It identified two circuit-width qualifications: the original-support theorem's formal width reserves a constant wire at every stage, so the paper's nonconstant width-one cases require formal width two; the ReLU/GELU manuscript has been corrected to state its required constant-one wire at every stage explicitly. These qualifications concern the exact learner-width threshold, not the polynomial exponents. The Lean sources are unchanged.

## Main proof differences

The formal proofs need not follow the manuscripts line by line. In particular:

- Activation counting uses an actual scalar increment cover, a dimension-free input moment bound, product Frobenius normalization, and arithmetic–geometric mean. The strong-prior teacher budget is the printed one. The uniform theorem enlarges the unspecified endpoint energy polynomial so that the displayed sample formula dominates a looser formal capacity bound; the reconstruction-energy witness is a separate result, not part of that chain.
- The improved original-support proof uses a larger finite-matrix concentration constant. This preserves its two stated polynomial exponents, while leaving the separate printed numerical allocation outside certification.
- The continuous depth lower bound avoids formalizing the manuscript's finite-domain large-deviation principle. Direct high Gaussian moments bound the numerator. For the mean-field denominator, explicit first-layer seed controls, free cooling layers, a logarithmic amplification schedule and an independent readout are combined under the actual finite-network product law. All local analytic and probabilistic premises are discharged in the final theorem.

Two endpoints bound the probability of a sample event without a Lean measurability proof for that event: the continuous-domain regression statements and the critical (`γ=1`) depth lower bound. The events are measurable (the training event is jointly open, and the risk and confidence maps are measurable by Tonelli), so the statements are not weakened; the 24 October review records this. No endpoint is certified by assuming its main analytic conclusion. A confidence bound for full posterior mixtures is not replaced by a statement about a single minimizer or the posterior mean. The only permitted axiom dependencies are `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

These are formal proofs of the listed mathematical statements, not human peer review or a guarantee that every paragraph of each manuscript is correct. The detailed records preserve exclusions rather than extending verification labels to an entire PDF indiscriminately.
