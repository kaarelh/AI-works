1. Finite compression before invoking stability

Paper: works-in-progress/improved-polynomials/original-support-all-scales/
source/compression-proof.tex, thm:robust-entropy. Its stated scope is every
scalar 1-Lipschitz activation bounded in absolute value by one, every
positive width, and every scale 0<t<=1. The previous Lean endpoint used
tanh, and its all-width score comparison assumed robustness. Those extra
restrictions are removed from the new pre-stability compression endpoint.

For d>=1, L>=2, K>=1, radius rho>0, and failure alpha>0, the actual finite
class PaperCompressionCode d L K rho alpha is fixed before the activation,
learner width N, Gaussian scale t, input x, or posterior Q is chosen. Every
code decodes to an actual depth-L network with a common bounded-weight
budget. The class is independent of N and t. Its log cardinality is at most
  A*(2+d+L+K+1/rho+1/alpha)^45
for one universal positive A. This proves the theorem's existential
polynomial bound, not the appendix's separate literal numerical allocation.

The actual smallPrior uses sigma=t/sqrt(N), so its first, internal and
readout variances match the paper. The only assumption on Q, besides being
a probability measure, is KL(Q || smallPrior) <= NK/t^2. Absolute continuity
and finiteness needed by the construction follow from this finite KL bound.
No stability, support condition, coupling oracle, or compression assumption
is added. The activation may have a nonzero value at zero.

The result supplies one probability law Gamma on the finite class. At every
Boolean input x it constructs a genuine joint probability measure on
(original weights, finite code, additive disturbance). Both required
marginals are exact, and Gamma is common to all inputs. On an event of
probability at least 1-alpha,
  sqrt(noiseBudgetSq eta) < rho/2,
  |decoded output(x) - noisyNetworkOutput phi w x eta| <= rho/8.
The disturbance is the architecture's actual postactivation/readout
perturbation. Its measurability and the measurable success/failure events
are proved. Thus strictness of the printed noise radius is also retained.
The paper's additional rho<=1 and alpha<=1/8 restrictions are unnecessary
for this endpoint, so all of its stated cases are included.

The seven *Activation modules extend the actual selected-bank estimate,

2. Positive mass implies finite network complexity

Paper: main-results/gamma-above-one/source/source/mass-complexity.tex,
cor:mass-complexity. PriorMassComplexity previously proved the polynomial
inverse bound assuming isComplexity phi f C. The paper only assumes finite
unconditioned mass complexity, meaning positive raw function mass.

Positive raw mass supplies an actual strict-margin parameter draw. Its
finite energy can be bounded by an integer. The proved norm compressor
then constructs a bounded-weight finite-width realization, and natural-
number well-ordering gives the least such complexity. This works for any
admissible activation and any weight measure; no nonpolynomial activation
assumption is needed.

The combined paper corollary fixes L>=2 and gamma>1, selects universal
polynomial constants before d,N,phi,f, and obtains both finite minimum
complexity and the width-independent inverse bound from positive raw mass
alone. At every N'>=C and every gamma'>=1 it additionally proves positive
raw mass and the forward mass-complexity bound from the existing cylinder
polynomial, uniformly in gamma'. Positivity is explicit because Lean's
ordinary real logarithm is total at zero; it is not used to silently treat
zero mass as finite mass complexity.
