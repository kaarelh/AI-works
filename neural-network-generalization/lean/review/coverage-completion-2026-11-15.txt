Sixteen-paper disposition
------------------------
1. Strong-prior generalization: main/abstract polynomial statement, general
   continuous nonpolynomial circuit simulation, norm comparison and packing,
   and growing-depth appendix covered.
2. Gamma above one: all-width finite-scale theorem and two-sided prior-mass
   versus teacher-complexity comparison covered.
3. Mean-field all-width: actual tanh posterior, common sample event, width
   threshold, risk and strict-confidence convention covered.
4. Subcritical exponential: parity lower bound and width-first limits,
   sharpened negative-gamma case, and general squared-sampling lemmas covered.
5. Robust polynomial depth: counted circuit width, finite-noise main theorem,
   general margins/transforms, transport/certificates, localization/pilots,
   unlabeled/shared/countable pools, geometric counting, input neighborhoods,
   and hidden-only/valid-hidden-state variants covered.
6. Unbounded/Lipschitz activations: actual strong-prior and uniform-band
   results, GELU consequence, prior/scale identity and confidence covered.
7. ReLU/GELU polynomial depth: counted constant-wire model, actual merging
   support, sharp scales and width-growth conclusions covered.
8. Improved original-support all-scales: complete literal D_*/J learning,
   arbitrary bounded-Lipschitz raw compression, and both literal sample-growth
   conclusions covered, including the optional global-margin prior.
9. Shallow sharp bounds: three numerical budgets and the L1/norm/quadratic
   resource consequences covered at all eligible widths/scales.
10. Online upper bounds: actual-prior permutation and infinite-IID processes,
    posterior draws, confidence/majority conversions, general finite-loss
    comparison, nonuniform IID ordering, ReLU/GELU population supports,
    signed/balanced tanh supports and both exact ReLU resource regimes covered.
11. Circuit converse: actual circuit syntax and resource constants, both
    protocols, Kraft/counting, executable prefix coding and prior law covered.
12. Neural polynomial-depth converse: bounded-gain main result and distinct
    strict-operator-norm-support learner, Gaussian limits and prediction rules
    covered without interchanging limits.
13. Non-Gaussian priors: all three original endpoints, actual independent
    two-sided-envelope laws, quadratic log-tail limits and rate covered.
14. Continuous domains: regression on the stated laws, minimizers/mixtures,
    actual iterated Gaussian limits, measurable success, binary margin
    corollaries and spherical score-to-sign/learning consequences covered.
15. Continuous depth lower bound: fixed-law and varying smooth-density
    constructions, actual minimizers/mixtures/iterated limits, literal
    width-independent ceiling and finite-scale/memorizer bounds covered.
16. Conditional nonpolynomial learning: the sole stated PPoly != TC1 premise,
    actual positive-scale Gaussian learner, fixed hard family including zero
    samples, and arbitrary-confidence posterior ceiling covered.

The exact definitions, hypotheses and quantifier cautions remain in the main
inventory. The strong-prior, small-positive-scale, finite-scale, width-first,
scale-first, IID, permutation and adversarial-order conclusions are distinct.
This is an important-theorem coverage claim, not a claim that every informal
auxiliary calculation has a separately named theorem. Empirical interval
arithmetic is not a Lean certificate. Papers outside these original sixteen,
including later independent online lower-bound and agnostic work, are outside
this task's scope.
