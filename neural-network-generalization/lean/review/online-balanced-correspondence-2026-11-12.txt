Literal support and compiler
----------------------------
BalancedPartitions requires every hidden layer to use the same population
count q, with each group of size floor(N/q) or ceil(N/q). The support requires
q<=1+E_v and all-input clipped raw/averaged error <=1/16. The actual averaged
weights are P_1 W_1, P_s W_s P_(s-1), and P_H v. The theorem
mem_balancedMergingSupport_iff_manuscript proves exact equivalence to the
paper's quantified definition, including 1<=q<=N. The support is closed and
Borel by a finite union over actual partitions; witnesses may depend on w.

For a genuine layered NAND/copy/negation Boolean circuit of width C, with
constant wires counted, every N>=C is partitioned into C nonempty clone
groups with floor/ceiling sizes. There is no unused or extra padding group.
Each source coefficient is divided by its own group's multiplicity, and
the output averages the output-wire group. Exact preactivation and readout
identities, energy bounds, RMS invariants, and finite-noise margins are
proved. Averaging fixes the compiler and all its hidden states; a common
subspace of dimension <=C contains every internal row, ideal state and
actual hidden prefix. The Gaussian cylinder constrains an adapted basis
of this subspace and bounds the free internal operators.

The cylinder has raw signed margin >1 and optional finite-noise robustness.
The raw and averaged networks each differ from the center by <=1/4, so
their clipped outputs agree. The perturbed readout energy is at least
(a^2 C)/8, with a>=4, proving the literal count budget. Its actual Gaussian
mass is at least exp(-balancedTargetCost/sigma^2), where balancedTargetCost
is tanhMergeTargetCost at radius parameter 2c. All N>=C and every positive
sigma with N sigma^2<=1 are quantified, using the actual product Gaussian
first/internal/readout variances N sigma^2/d, sigma^2, sigma^2/N. Equivalently,
sigma=t/sqrt(N) gives the manuscript variances t^2/d,t^2/N,t^2/N^2.
No target-mass, comparison-class or compression witness is an assumption
of the final posterior theorems.

Posterior and comparison class
------------------------------
The posterior conditions this Gaussian on the exact balanced support and
strict training margin >1/2. Noise robustness and global clean separation
are independently optional. Exact two-stage conditioning and positive
normalizers are proved. Truncation uses the already-proved union over all
signed partitions; the unsigned balanced witnesses form a subfamily. This
looser exceptional event is legitimate and is counted before selection.
Its finite grid approximates clipped outputs within 3/32. A single class
is fixed before the input sample, uniformly over all N and sigma. Outside
posterior mass <=epsilon/2, every posterior draw lies in this cover.

Budgets and endpoints
---------------------
The entropy bound is log card <=10^28 C^2 S^9, with
  S=10^10+4100(2c+1)+4 log C+d+r-log(epsilon/4).
At epsilon=2/2^d use
  S_online=10^10+4100(2c+1)+4 log C+2d+r+2.
These are quadratic in teacher width up to logarithms and jointly polynomial
in dimension, depth, the radius parameter and log(1/epsilon), as claimed
by the online import. They are deliberately loose explicit constants.

Actual uniform-permutation posterior tested-margin risk has expectation
<=2+2(capacity+1)(1+d), with logarithmic-confidence bounds. Fresh posterior
parameter draws obey the corresponding sampled-mistake bounds. Both
results extend to the entire infinite uniform-IID input stream, where
repeated inputs have zero risk under training-margin conditioning. Testing
uses margin <=1/4, and therefore bounds ordinary sign mistakes. The
finite-sample endpoint has one measurable input event simultaneously for
all N>=C, permitted sigma, and both optional conditioning choices.
