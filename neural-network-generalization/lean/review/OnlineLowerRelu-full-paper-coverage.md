## Important unnumbered claims

The proof transports the actual product Gaussian measure and proves exact equality of every function mass and posterior after replacing the original scale by `sigma*(d*alpha*beta)^(1/4)`. See the [independent extension review](OnlineLowerCoverage-independent-audit.md).
- One unnumbered formula remains outside literal coverage: the displayed numerical identity `|z|=(2/pi) integral_0^infinity (1-cos(tz))/t^2 dt` and the derived numerical `1/pi` coefficient prefactor. The checked proof uses the corresponding positive integral normalizer and proves the same identity relative to it, without separately evaluating that normalizer as pi (on the full line). All comparisons cancel it, and the sharp parity constant is independently identified combinatorially. This is not an unproved premise of any numbered theorem. Do not describe every displayed identity in the paper as separately formalized.
- The balanced-ray set and actual variation infimum are now explicit definitions, and the corresponding equality/minimizer assertions have been assembled without changing the paper’s mathematical statements.
