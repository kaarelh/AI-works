Actual model
------------
HiddenSigns assigns one Boolean sign to every hidden neuron. Its coordinate
transformation conjugates each internal weight matrix and transforms the
first/readout weights as required. The actual tanh network output, weighted
energy, readout energy and entire product Gaussian law are invariant.
The literal signed projections R_s have entries sigma_i P_ij sigma_j.
signedMergedWeights_first/internal/readout prove that the implemented merge
is R_1 W_1, R_s W_s R_(s-1), and R_H v. The support is a finite union over
actual partitions and signs, hence closed and Borel. The theorem
mem_signedPartitionSupport_iff_manuscript proves exact equivalence to
  (max_s q_s-1) (max_x |z_w(x)|)^2 <= 4 E_v,
  max_x |clip(z_w(x))-clip(z_merged(x))| <= 1/16.
No partition, low-energy witness or target mass is assumed in the posterior.
The optional robustness condition is the existing literal finite-disturbance
support: all hidden and output disturbances of squared normalized norm at
most c^2/(1+(r+1)E_v), with clipped error <=1/16. It may be included or omitted;
the all-input strict margin >1/2 may also independently be included or omitted.
Training always uses signed margin >1/2, and testing uses margin <=1/4.

All-width target mass
---------------------
For every actual layered NAND/copy/negation Boolean circuit B of width C>0,
input dimension d>0 and r+1 hidden stages, and every N>=C, the proof constructs
a saturated clone with k=floor(N/C) active copies and an extra constant-wire
group. A single subspace of dimension <=C+1 contains all center rows and
all hidden states. Population averaging fixes this center and every hidden
state. An explicit rotated Gaussian cylinder constrains that subspace and
bounds the independent remaining Gaussian operators. Its radius is the
minimum of the proved saturation radius and the generic merging radius.
The raw signed margin is >1, while raw and merged outputs are each within
1/4 of the center; hence their clipped outputs agree. Coordinate readout
bounds prove the literal population budget, including the extra group.
The same cylinder satisfies optional robustness. Its Gaussian mass is
at least exp(-D/sigma^2), for the explicitly defined tanhMergeTargetCost D.
No positive-mass assumption remains. Constant wires count in circuit width.

Gaussian truncations and fixed finite class
-------------------------------------------
smallPrior has first/internal/readout variances N*sigma^2/d, sigma^2,
and sigma^2/N. Thus sigma=t/sqrt(N) is exactly the paper's raw variances
(t^2/d,t^2/N,t^2/N^2), with 0<t<=1 equivalent to N*sigma^2<=1.
The final results quantify every positive sigma satisfying this inequality.
The sign union costs 2^((r+1)N). The proven signed exceptional tail includes
that factor, together with the complete finite partition union, before any
witness is selected. After dividing by the target mass and using
N*sigma^2<=1, both posterior tail thresholds are independent of N and sigma.
The actual unsigned population representation uses a*tanh(u/a), with
population scales a=sqrt(k/N). Input/scale Lipschitz estimates, including
the zero-scale extension, prove a fixed finite grid with clipped error
<=3/32. Sign conjugation transfers the same cover to signed populations.
At every nonempty history, the actual posterior is covered except for
mass <=epsilon/2, with one class fixed before inputs are sampled.

Polynomial and online conclusions
---------------------------------
tanh_grid_entropy_polynomial proves log cardinality <=10^28 C^2 S^9, with
  S = 10^10 +4100(c+1)+4 log C+d+r-log(epsilon/4).
This is polynomial jointly in dimension, depth, C, c+c^-1 and log(1/epsilon),
and quadratic in C up to logarithms at fixed remaining parameters.
For epsilon=2/2^d, the online capacity uses
  S_online = 10^10+4100(c+1)+4 log C+2d+r+2.
The constants are deliberately loose; the online paragraph specifies growth
and prior support, rather than a literal numerical constant for this import.

The expected total tested-margin error under the actual posterior is at most
  2+2(capacity+1)(1+d).
Logarithmic-confidence bounds hold for the actual uniform permutation and
for fresh posterior parameter draws, and for the entire infinite uniform-IID
query stream with fresh draws at every query. Repeated inputs have zero risk
by the actual training-margin conditioning. The sampled endpoints use the
query-then-draw probability experiment, not a surrogate process. The separate
finite-sample learning theorem has one input event simultaneous over all
N>=C, all permitted sigma, and both optional conditioning choices.
