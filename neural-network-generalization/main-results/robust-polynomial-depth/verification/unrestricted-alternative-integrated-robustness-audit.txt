Integrated mathematical scope
-----------------------------
The target is a layered NAND/copy/negation Boolean circuit whose required
internal constant wires count toward C and whose logical depth fits the
H hidden stages. It is not an arbitrary same-width analog target. The
posterior is exactly the raw Gaussian conditioned on support stability
and all observed signed margins >1/2. There is no hidden global clean
margin assumption in the statistical proof. The guarantee is Gibbs
test-margin error at threshold1/4, hence implies sign error and the stated
posterior confidence criteria. Neither efficient posterior computation
nor sampling by gradient descent is claimed.

Target cylinder and raw probability
-----------------------------------
Balanced clone groups work at every integer N>=C, including N=C.
The group-constant projection isolates only N[d+2+(L−2)C] constrained
independent Gaussian coordinates. The unconstrained internal bulk is
independent and has simultaneous operator-norm control with probability
at least1/2. Gate fan-in, clone-group averaging, and the global secant
bound preserve the RMS invariant without an exponential depth factor.
The first-layer initialization and the readout perturbation obey the
same invariant. The center-energy bounds cover coincident input wires.

The cylinder has noisy signed output >1 on the whole cube at radius
tau0. Its readout energy is >=a^2 C/8; a=max(2,256c) therefore places
the calibrated radius below tau0 for every c>0. The one-coordinate
Gaussian interval bound is uniform for all0<t<=1, with no omitted log t
factor. Multiplying only the constrained coordinates gives the stated
raw mass exp(−N D*/t^2), with D* polynomial in d,L,C,c and independent
of N,t. This is a subset of every labeled posterior event, so the two
conditioning normalizers cancel exactly in the raw-KL calculation.

Compression-to-statistics interface
----------------------------------
Readout truncation produces a common radius and common raw-KL bound.
The compressor's class is fixed independently of N,t and the sample;
its pointwise couplings may vary with input, while both marginals remain
fixed. This is sufficient when averaging the ramp loss, and no global
coupling or robustness of compressed candidates is assumed. The
training score1/2, successful discrepancy<=1/12, and test threshold1/4
give the correct ramp endpoints5/12 and1/3, including equality cases.
The relative-Chernoff event is finite-class and shared by every width
and Gaussian scale. Dependence on c+c^{-1} remains polynomial, so an
explicit inverse-polynomial-in-depth calibration preserves the theorem.

The appendix's entropy accounting tracks only unprocessed original
weights. The active-list message cost, conditional transport, preserved
original-network marginal, and canonical new-prefix/future marginal are
compatible. All virtual states are actual bounded activation outputs;
later row selection cannot invalidate bounds because all rows were
clipped first. Final rounding costs polynomially many bits despite an
exponentially fine grid. The valid-state and hidden-only variants use
these properties correctly.

Localized and transformed support
--------------------------------
The exact union-conditioned posterior has one raw KL bound. Partitioning
by first witnessing exception set gives average component KL equal to
the whole-posterior KL plus the partition entropy. Markov truncation of
components and readout tails is properly weighted; no component is
incorrectly assumed to contain the target cylinder. Masked loss gives
rho+epsilon, with the original training likelihood maintained on all
points. Pilot independence, the fixed-family same-input corollary,
finite-cube VC reduction, and width/scale simultaneity are consistent.

General margins and monotone score transforms preserve the target

lower bound or a prevalence estimate.

Empirical scope
---------------
The paper distinguishes float64 sufficient tests from exact/interval
certificates, pointwise from global claims, finite pixel grids from the
continuous box, and likelihood eligibility from support stability.
The exact Boolean bit decoder preserves hidden trajectories and noise
budgets but does not preserve the original independent Gaussian prior;
that limitation is stated. Training accuracy/margins are explicitly
empirical measurements, not interval-certified eligibility. No remaining
scope mismatch was found in the integrated inputs read for this audit.
