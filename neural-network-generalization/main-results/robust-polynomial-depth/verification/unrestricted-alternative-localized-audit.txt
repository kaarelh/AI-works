Pointwise coupling with fixed marginals is sufficient: average empirical masked loss under the component's compressed law, use a uniform finite-class event on H times the fixed exception family, and compare population loss. The component law may depend arbitrarily on the sample without requiring a union bound over such laws.

Additional same-sample coverage corollary: VALID for a family fixed independently of S (or a full fixed envelope family whose complexity is controlled). Use the input locations in S to enforce empirical exception mass; uniform coverage and uniform masked-loss bounds can hold on the same sample by a union bound. Independence between those two events is unnecessary. The target cylinder with empty exception remains admissible for every S. This is explicitly a sample-input-dependent prior, which is handled directly by the raw-Gaussian conditioning proof, not an ordinary data-independent PAC-Bayes shortcut. Arbitrarily learning a family from S without controlling the full family is not justified.
