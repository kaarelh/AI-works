1. CUTOFF AND ALL-n QUANTIFIERS
For n>=exp(log^3(d+1)), take the largest odd r with
 A D_r log(2D_r)<=n.
Then n<A D_(r+2)log(2D_(r+2)). The upper entropy estimate on D_(r+2)
combined with this strict next-threshold bound proves r>=c log^2 d.
(The current cutoff inequality alone would not prove the lower bound;
maximality is essential.) Choosing c_L<h(alpha)/2 makes r<=alpha d
uniformly for n<=exp(c_L d). This is a separate fixed-n sample event
for each n, with constants uniform in n; no simultaneous-prefix event
or Bayesian risk monotonicity is asserted or required.

2. SQUARED COERCIVITY AND THE COARSE GRAM FACTOR
The Walsh evaluation vector has exact squared norm D_r and covariance I.
Multiplying it by parity leaves covariance unchanged. Thus the new
sampling theorem applies directly, with deterministic labels f(X_i).
Its population balance gives empirical negative-square coercivity at
n>=A D_r log(2D_r), with a numerical constant such as 1/4.

At the next-shell upper endpoint, paired normalization is exact:
 D_(r+2) is comparable to binom(d+1,r+2).
Consequently
 n ell_r/tau_r <= C A log(e(d+1)/r),
 n d s2_r/tau_r^2 <= C A ((d+1)/r) log(e(d+1)/r).
The resulting coarse factor
 b_r <= C[1+sqrt(A rho^(-1)log(e/rho))+A log(e/rho)]
is correct. It need not be bounded uniformly as rho goes to zero.
Tropp's logarithm is d, not log n. The deterministic far-pair mask,
Bernoulli-to-fixed-size transfer, and iid collision step were checked
previously; the residual terms vanish uniformly here because
r>=c log^2 d and 2c_L<log 2-h(1/4).

3. EVERY MINIMIZER IS CONTROLLED ON THE SAME SAMPLE EVENT
The comparator event bounds B=||z||_K^2<=C_(L,a)n d^beta for every
minimizer, since the exponential lower threshold absorbs its additive d.
For the low projection P, training margins imply (fP)_-<=|z-P| on the
sample. The RKHS evaluation inequality and squared coercivity therefore
give ||P||_2^2<=4 B b_r tau_r/n. This bound is simultaneous for all
minimizers, with no posterior selection or finite-net argument.

For u=r+2ceil(10log(d+1)), the middle band b satisfies
 ||b||_2^2<=B ell_r<=C A rho^(-beta)log(e/rho).
It is legitimate that this norm need not vanish. The final tail t has
 ||t||_2^2<=B lambda_(u+1)=o(1)
uniformly in n. In detail, the two-sided sharp bounds are used at the
two endpoints only, so there is only one C_L factor:
 lambda_(u+1)/ell_r
 <=C_L (r/u)^(1+beta) binom(d+1,r+2)/binom(d+1,u+2)
 <=C_L 4^(-(u-r)/2).
The last factor is at most (d+1)^(-10log4), beating the polynomial
upper bound on B ell_r. This avoids incorrectly accumulating C_L at
each of O(log d) successive shell ratios. Also u/r=1+o(1) uniformly.

4. THE EXACT CLIPPING IDENTITY IS STRONG ENOUGH
For every cube function h, the Jensen identity gives
 |E f clip(h/a)|<=||Delta h||_1/(ad).
For degree at most u this is at most u||h||_2/(ad), since Delta has
Fourier eigenvalues at most u. This is the crucial u/d factor.
The weaker single-edge gradient estimate would lose a square root and
would not by itself establish the current all-n conclusion.

Because P and b are orthogonal, multiplying their squared norms by
(u/d)^2 gives exactly
 C[ rho^(2-beta)
   +sqrt(A)rho^(3/2-beta)sqrt(log(e/rho))
   +A rho^(2-beta)log(e/rho)] +o(1).
All powers are positive for beta in (0,1/2], so a sufficiently small
fixed alpha works, after fixing A and all depth-dependent constants.
The original global margin on z yields clip(z/a)=sign(z), and the
Lipschitz error from t is ||t||_2/a=o(1). Hence every minimizer has
absolute parity correlation at most 1/4 and error at least 3/8.

5. SMALL n
For n<=exp(log^3(d+1)), an odd cutoff s asymptotic to d^(1/3) gives
B lambda_(s+1)=o(1) uniformly, since the eigenvalue's inverse logarithm
is of order d^(1/3)log d. The elementary polynomial-threshold lemma
then gives error 1/2-o(1). The n=0 comparator is just the constant
field and requires no random-sample event.

6. POSTERIOR AND CONFIDENCE TRANSFER
At fixed d,S, there are finitely many sign orthants. The actual-network
large-deviation proof is valid at speed N^gamma for every fixed
0<gamma<1: hidden empirical covariances concentrate exponentially at
speed N; readout is conditionally Gaussian; exponential equivalence
with covariance K transfers the quadratic rate. Strict and closed
margin orthants have the same infimum by radial dilation. A finite
energy gap then forces posterior mass 1-o(1) on minimizing signs.
There is no need for convergence, symmetry, or Gaussian approximation
of tie weights, including gamma>=1/2.

Every supported sign has risk>=3/8; hence any limiting or nonlimiting
mixture has R_N>=3/8-o(1). At each finite N,
 R_N<=1/3+(2/3)F_N,
where F_N=P_x{correct-label posterior probability<=2/3}. Therefore
 liminf_N F_N>=1/16.
This does not require continuity of the threshold functional at ties.
The strict >2/3 correctness convention is handled exactly.

The fixed-n sample events have total failure at most 2/20+o(1), so the
claimed probability 3/4 has slack. Constants may depend on fixed depth
and margin; width is taken to infinity before dimension, as in the model.
