1. Adaptive/positive: the coarse Gram loss rho^{-1/2} is retained.
The total head-plus-band L2 norm is sufficient, because the clipped-parity
inequality has factor u/d. The correlation square is controlled by
rho^(2-beta)+sqrt(A)rho^(3/2-beta)sqrt(log(e/rho))
+A rho^(2-beta)log(e/rho), which vanishes for every beta<=1/2.
The final tail estimate is uniform down to r~log²d. Comparison constants
are applied at spectral endpoints, not multiplied through each shell.
Every minimizer is covered, so arbitrary asymptotic tie weights are safe.

2. Conditioning: the localized bump has correct strict margins both at
selected centers and all other points; pair separation excludes repeated
incompatible labels. Damped Gaussian density comparison is in the correct
direction. Cross energies are controlled uniformly over all selected
subsets before labels are chosen. The global margin event is retained
throughout the auxiliary-coordinate argument. Its Gaussian split may
depend on the auxiliary subset, but its full-G marginal is always the
same prior and posterior. The relative entropy bound on the independent
noise marginal is valid by data processing. The auxiliary tail Gram
estimate contains q*rho_r, not sqrt(m)*rho_r, so the chosen exponential
auxiliary sample sizes are compatible with the far-pair decay.

3. Zero endpoint: quadratic entropy control applies to linear projections
and restrictions under the exact posterior; no independence after
conditioning is assumed. The normalized conditioning cost is
D/n<=C d rho^{-beta}. The head-plus-band normalized correlation square is
rho^(2-2beta)[1+rho^(-1/2)sqrt(log(e/rho))+log(e/rho)].
Its worst power, rho^(3/2-2beta), is still positive at beta=1/2.
The posterior tail trace divided by the amplitude squared is bounded by
C/(d rho^beta), vanishing uniformly down to rho~log²d/d. The entropy tail
term has additional d^-10 and also vanishes uniformly. Small samples use
the fixed global margin a and the PTF annihilation bound directly.
The risk-to-confidence conversion avoids the equality discontinuity at
posterior confidence 2/3 when passing to finite width.

4. Width limits: hidden covariances concentrate at speed N and converge
to the saturated kernel for each gamma<1. At 0<gamma<1 the exact Gaussian
readout is exponentially equivalent, at speed N^gamma, to the fixed
kernel Gaussian; speed separation is sufficient and no relative-error
rare-event CLT is asserted. At gamma<=0, finite-dimensional weak
convergence and Gaussian-null boundaries give exact Boolean-posterior
convergence. Width precedes dimension everywhere.
