INDEPENDENT APPLICATION AUDIT: NEGATIVE GAMMA
15 October 2026. allwidth_bridge.

STATUS: PASS. Reviewed paper/gamma-negative.tex in the round-four paper.
The proof covers EACH n<=exp(c_L d), including all paired shell gaps,
and gives buffered Gaussian confidence failure at least 3/8 with
training-sample probability at least 3/4.

Whitening the s distinct training values makes the sign event a
homogeneous cone. Standard Gaussian radius is independent of direction,
so its conditional squared radius remains s. Jensen and regression give
||m||_K^2<=s, regardless of repeated sample indices. Signed training
means are strictly positive because the conditioning orthant is open.

The univariate conditional law is log-concave by marginalization of a
Gaussian restricted to a convex orthant. Its CDF H is log-concave and
continuous, with H(Y) uniform. Hence log H(EY)>=E log H(Y)=-1.
This proves P(Y>EY)<=1-e^(-1). The scalar law is also a mixture of
Gaussians with common residual variance sigma_x^2, so its density is
at most 1/(sqrt(2pi)sigma_x). Consequently confidence above 13/20
forces a mean margin exceeding
 sqrt(2pi)(1/e-7/20)sigma_x,
a strictly positive constant. This is the appropriate mean-confidence
implication; no unsupported equivalence between mean sign and majority
posterior sign is used.

The residual-variance estimate has correct normalization. Conditioning
on all low modes leaves tail covariance Q, and regression subtracts a
PSD matrix of rank at most s bounded above by Q. Its normalized trace
is at most s ell. Therefore fewer than 2n ell/tau of the points can
lose more than tau/2 residual variance. Observed points, where the
residual variance vanishes, are included in this bound automatically.

At the maximal admissible odd cutoff r, squared coercivity controls
||m_<=r||_2^2<=4b_r tau_r. The coarse Gram factor
b_r=O(1+sqrt(log(e/rho)/rho)+log(e/rho)) is retained correctly.
The O(log d) band can have norm squared n ell_r, and need not vanish.
The final tail norm and exceptional residual-variance fraction do vanish
relative to tau_r. One may justify the geometric eigenvalue decay by
applying sharp spectral estimates at the two endpoints and comparing
binomial coefficients; this avoids multiplying comparability constants
at every pair. The draft's resulting decay claim is correct.

After removing the two o(1) exceptional sets, confidence implies the
low-plus-band polynomial has signed margin a_r proportional to sqrt(tau_r).
Thus its clipped parity coefficient is at least 1-2F_theta-o(1).
Jensen–Laplacian clipping bounds its absolute value by
 C rho sqrt(1+sqrt(log(e/rho)/rho)+log(e/rho))+o(1),
which goes to zero uniformly as the allowed degree fraction shrinks.
There is ample numerical slack to obtain F_theta>=3/8. The smaller-n
PTF argument works for every sample, including polynomial zeros with
any fixed tie convention. n=0 is immediate by sign symmetry.

Finally, theta=13/20 is strictly below 2/3. At fixed d,S there are only
finitely many input probabilities, all converging under the conditional
width limit. Every point whose limiting probability is <=theta is
therefore counted as a finite-width strict-2/3 failure for all large
widths. This gives the stated liminf without a boundary-continuity gap.
No risk monotonicity or simultaneous-prefix probability is assumed.
