VERDICT ON THE ARGUMENT: PASS.

1. Exact posterior and entropy.
The posterior is the original finite Gaussian vector conditioned on the
entire global-margin and training-label event. Its entropy from the raw
Gaussian prior is precisely D=-log P(A). No convexity, independence of
posterior Fourier components, or posterior-mean surrogate is used.

2. Quadratic entropy inequality.
For a centered Gaussian linear image Z with covariance eigenvalues
lambda_i<=ell, trace t, at theta=1/(4ell),
  log E exp(theta ||Z||²)
    =-.5 sum log(1-lambda_i/(2ell)) <=t/(2ell).
The variational entropy inequality therefore gives
  E_post||Z||² <=2t+4ell D.
The population Fourier tail has t=tau and ell=lambda_next. The empirical
tail has covariance Q_SS/n, trace exactly tau (including repetitions),
and largest eigenvalue ||Q_SS||/n. All normalizations are correct.

3. Adaptive denominator.
Damping only degrees <=r by tau_r gives a density ratio bounded below
pointwise by tau_r^(D_r/2), in the stated direction. Its diagonal is
<=2tau_r. The global shift amplitude squared is O(d tau_r), its constant
energy O(d/tau_r), and its bump energy O(n d^beta). Hence its translation
cost is O(d²+n d^(1+beta)tau_r). The determinant adds O(D_r log(r+1)).
On the adaptive shell n>=A D_r log(2D_r), in the large-n range, this gives
D/n<=C d rho^(-beta), rho=r/d, with constants independent of rho.

The product-bump proof was checked including: the beta-integral bound;
the high-degree exponent c_*>.0062; the damped low-degree leakage;
the absolute cross-energy Markov bound; uniformity over arbitrary labels;
and pairwise separation of different sample INDICES, which excludes
repetitions. The undamped proof provides the stated rough cost.

4. Actual-output amplitude.
The auxiliary subset is independent of the actual posterior. For each
fixed good subset its Gaussian augmentation has exactly the original full
G marginal, so conditioning always gives the same G|A. Event inclusion
suffices to bound all selected margins by a product conditional on H_T;
no independence of the outside constraints is needed. Averaging over T
therefore yields the exact population-input amplitude statement.
The auxiliary failure probability is exponentially small in the stated
spectral regime, and the remaining error is O(1/d).

5. Adaptive Fourier decomposition.
On the uniform squared-coercivity event, every posterior draw satisfies
training (fp)_- <=|G_{>r}|, so
  E_post||p||² <=8tau_r+16 D B_r tau_r/n.
The middle and high bands have expected norm² bounded by
  2tau_r+4ell_r D,  and  2tau_u+4ell_u D,
respectively. They require no posterior independence and no additional
empirical event at u. Orthogonality of the two low bands is pathwise.
The extra 2ceil(10log(d+1)) degrees make n ell_u/tau_r<=d^-10 uniformly;
the prior trace term is harmless after division by the growing output
amplitude squared.

6. Exact clipping estimate and exponents.
The Jensen argument with the one-coordinate-flip Markov operator proves
  |E parity clip(P/A)| <=||Delta P||_1/(dA)
                         <=u||P||_2/(dA)
for degree <=u, without any unsupported L1 Bernstein inequality.
Combining with A_d²~d^(1-beta) gives squared correlation at most
  C rho^(2-2beta)[1+sqrt(log(e/rho)/rho)+log(e/rho)]+o(1).
The worst exponent is 3/2-2beta>=1/2. Thus it tends to zero uniformly as
the upper endpoint alpha for rho tends to zero, including depth L=2.
The ambient log M in the Gram bound causes no remaining obstruction.

7. Small n and exact confidence.
For n<=exp(log(d+1)^3), choose odd s~d^(1/3). The rough denominator and
spectral decay give D ell_s=o(1), while tau_s=o(1). The fixed global
margin a already suffices to compare signs with the degree-s truncation;
no large-output result is needed here. The parity PTF bound gives draw
risk 1/2-o(1). At n=0 sign symmetry gives correct probability 1/2.

For every finite-width posterior, R_N<=1/3+(2/3)F_N, where F_N counts
inputs whose correct-label probability is <=2/3. Exact finite-dimensional
Gaussian posterior convergence implies R_N->R. Therefore R>=3/8 yields
liminf_N F_N>=1/16, with the required strict >2/3 success convention.
No continuity claim about the discontinuous confidence functional is
needed. The statement is for each specified n, not one simultaneous
event over all sample prefixes.
