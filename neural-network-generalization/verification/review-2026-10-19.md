# Mathematical review, 19 October 2026

This record describes a review of the collection by Claude, requested by the owner. It had two aims. The first was to check that the Lean statements of the five main theorems formalize what the papers claim. The second was to look for mathematical errors elsewhere, with priority on material the Lean development does not cover. It is an AI review, not external peer review.

## Method

Each Lean main statement was compared with its paper's theorem by direct reading, and independently by one adversarial checker per theorem. A further checker examined the publication gates and every README claim about Lean coverage. The checklist covered the model and its parametrization, quantifier order and constant dependence, conclusions, junk-value and vacuity loopholes, and name resolution.

For the mathematics, one reviewer per paper, or per part of a large paper, read the source and checked constants numerically where possible. Every reported mathematical error then went to two further checkers. One re-derived the step from the source. The other tried to defend the text, using the paper's conventions, later lemmas, and audit records. An edit was made only when the error survived both checks, or when a split verdict was resolved by direct inspection.

## Lean statements

All five propositions in `lean/NeuralGeneralization/Statements.lean` and `RobustStatements.lean` formalize their papers' main theorems. These points were checked explicitly:

- Model. The layer variances match each paper's parametrization: $N^{1-\gamma}/d$, $N^{-\gamma}$, $N^{-\gamma-1}$, the strong-prior scales, and the robust $t^2/d,t^2/N,t^2/N^2$ with $t^2=N\sigma^2\le1$. So do depth indexing, the bias coordinate, the all-input strict margin, the posterior over sign functions, and the strict $>2/3$ rule.
- Quantifiers. Wherever Lean differs from a paper, Lean is stronger. The polynomials are chosen before the activation. In the strong-prior statement $\sigma_0$ is chosen before $\delta$. In the subcritical statement $c$ and $d_0$ are chosen before $\gamma$. The robust polynomial is chosen before the circuit.
- Loopholes. Every upper-bound statement asserts positivity of the posterior normalizer, which excludes the zero-posterior loophole. For the lower bound, a junk liminf value would make the claim false, not vacuous, and positivity holds eventually in width for all samples. Satisfiability witnesses exist: `tanh_admissible`, `ParityNetwork.exists_parity_complexity`, and constructible layered circuits.
- Gates. The gates require theorem declarations of exactly the stated types and reject every axiom outside `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The source contains no `sorry`, `native_decide`, custom axioms, syntax extensions, or shadowed Mathlib names.

One narrow difference was found. Lean's `LayeredBooleanCircuit` reserves a constant-true wire at every stage, while the robust paper counts a constant wire only where a gate needs one. For $C\ge2$ the two conventions compute the same functions: once a stage has no constant-valued wire, no later stage can contain a NAND, so the output is $\pm$ one wire of that stage. The only case Lean leaves uncovered is learner width $N=1$ for width-one targets (a literal, a constant, or a single first-stage gate). The robust paper and the Lean README now state this.

The strong-prior and $\gamma>1$ propositions bound posterior average error. Their papers' $>2/3$ confidence statements follow from the Markov step $F_{2/3}\le3R$, applied at accuracy $\varepsilon/3$. This review added both confidence versions as closed corollaries in `lean/CheckConfidence.lean`.

The whole Lean project, including every imported mathlib module, was rebuilt from source with the pinned toolchain. All existing gates pass: 7,202 audited declarations, 5 / 5 main results, and the parity supplement. So does the new confidence check. The outputs are in `lean/review/verification/review-2026-10-19/gates.txt`.

## Mathematical findings

No reviewed theorem was found to be false. The following local errors were confirmed and repaired.

| Paper | Error | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Robust polynomial depth | A general-margin remark said the same proof works for any training margin $m$, but the target cylinder only guarantees outputs above $1$. | Use the rescaled cylinder of the general-margin corollary; the bound is then polynomial in $m$ too. |
| Robust polynomial depth | Model 005's minimum training margin was stated as exceeding $.692$; the record gives $0.69186$. | Corrected to $.6918$ in the robust paper; the frozen depth-experiments original is left unchanged. |
| Unrestricted depth | A corollary and the introduction claimed independence from later teacher computation at every learner depth. | The depth-six realization gives this for $L\ge6$; condition added. |
| Non-Gaussian priors | "Gaussian densities times positive bounded factors" was given as an example satisfying the envelope condition. The factor $e^{-u^4}$ violates the lower envelope. | Factors bounded above and below by positive constants. |
| Growing-depth parity | The calibration proof used exceptional-set mass $C\rho^g$, but the huge-amplitude set has mass $C\rho^{b_0}$ with $b_0\le pt_0<g/2$, so the stated choice $a_1\le g/8$ fails. | Correct exponent; choose $a_1\le b_0/8$ after $t_0$. The overall proof was unaffected. |
| Growing sparse positive, intermediate parity, subcritical (condition section), zero-gamma note | The shared localized-interpolation lemma was stated for every $d$; its proof needs large $d$. | Added a large-$d$ hypothesis; every application already has it. |
| Scaling tuples | The box action bound omitted the cross terms with the centre. | Bound by twice the central action plus the stated terms. |
| Unrestricted depth | A remark gave retained depth $dP^2\log(2P)$, contradicting its own $d$-independent theorem. | $P^4\log(2P)$ after restriction to relevant coordinates. |
| Strong-prior appendix audit | The audit said the slides' Appendix H reader/output budget had a constant-factor mismatch; its own identity was off by a factor of four, and the slides' display is correct. | Corrected the audit's remark. |
| Gamma above one | The Gaussian interval bound was stated under the hypothesis $\lvert Z-a\rvert\le\rho t$, which is the event being measured. | Every real $a$ and $\rho>0$. |

The checks also confirmed the main-paper arguments where the Lean proofs take a different route. These were the $\gamma>1$ singular-value tail lemma and truncation bound, the mean-field paper's own constants, the subcritical paper's constants, and the robust paper's statistical proof. They also confirmed several numerical claims in side results. Examples are the $\gamma=1/2$ fluctuation counterexample, with prefactor ratio $189/125$, and the sparse-parity failure fraction $2\pi(1/e-7/20)^2/256\approx7.85\times10^{-6}$.

## Statement and scope revisions

Theorem statements were tightened to say what is proved where the displayed wording was weaker or ambiguous:

- the strong-prior theorem now chooses $\sigma_0$ before $\delta$;
- the robust theorem now chooses the universal polynomial before the circuit;
- the subcritical abstract now says $d\ge d_0$.

Abstracts, introductions, and READMEs were corrected where they overstated scope. Examples:

- the sequential-class bounds of the critical-depth paper are polynomial in length, not independent of it;
- the sparse-parity summaries now state the small universal failure fraction;
- the continuous-depth summaries now state their large width thresholds;
- the label-noise width threshold grows with the number of corruptions;
- "exponential" for an $\exp(c\sqrt k)$ amplitude bound is now qualified.

Older side papers now carry dated notes pointing to main result 4. The remaining edits are notation clashes, missing definitions, and cross-references. Each commit message lists its changes.

## Not changed

These items were left for the authors:

- **Authorship.** The strong-prior paper lists ChatGPT, while the collection byline names OpenAI Codex.
- **Lean docstrings.** A few cite older theorem numbers, for example mean-field Theorem 1.1, now Theorem 2.1. Editing them would change the Lean source hashes recorded in the verification record.
- **Frozen archive.** `works-in-progress/depth-experiments/experiment/README.md` lists four bundled checkpoints; five are included (model 301 is missing from the list). The file is recorded as an unchanged original in the archive manifests, so it was not edited.
- **Presentation.** The intermediate-parity theorem's cutoff constants could be stated explicitly, and its angular fine-tail bound proved in full. The growing-depth paper could restate its lemmas with depth-uniform constants. Several papers could rename overloaded symbols.

Reviewed:
