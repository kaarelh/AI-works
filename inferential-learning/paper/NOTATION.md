# Unified notation for the paper (all section writers must follow this)

Language and judgments
- `\Fm`: formulas. Atoms p, q, r, p_0, p_1, .... Connectives ¬, ∧, ∨, →, ⊥, ⊤.
- A *judgment* is `\Gamma \der \varphi` (single conclusion) or `\Gamma \der \Delta` (multiple conclusion). A context Γ is a finite set of formulas.
- A *position* `\pos{\Gamma}{\Delta}` asserts all of Γ and denies all of Δ (Restall). "Coherent" means "in bounds".
- A *step* is `P \step j`: from finitely many premise judgments P infer judgment j. For single-formula steps write `\varphi_1,\dots,\varphi_k \step \psi`.
- A *rule set*, or step set, is R. `\Cl_R(B)` is the derivability closure. `\Sound(R) = \{P \step j : j \in \Cl_R(P)\}`.
- The target human calculus is `\Rstar`. Practice (what humans actually do) is `R^P = \Rstar \cup R_F`, where `R_F` is generated by systematic fallacies F.
- Schemas are terms with metavariables x, y, A, B. `\inst(\sigma)` is the set of instances. `\lgg` is Plotkin's least general generalization. μ(t) = size(t) − #distinct variables.
- Consequence operators are C or ⊢, with `\Cn_2`, or `\Cn_{\CPC}`, for classical consequence. "Structural" means closed under substitutions σ.

Learning
- `\Hc` is the hypothesis class. `\VS(D)` is the version space of hypotheses consistent with the data D. w(h) is a prior weight and `\wstar = w(\Rstar)`.
- A verifier answers ACCEPT, REJECT or ESCALATE to steps chosen by an adaptive *prover*. "δ-sound" means P(some invalid step is ever accepted) ≤ δ, uniformly over provers.
- Escalation dimension `\Esc`; positive elasticity `\el`.
- The three channels are named (P) practice/imitation, (C) coherence, and (W) world. W covers truth values, counterexample objects, computation and simulation.
- *Designated contexts* `\Ac`: contexts certified coherent. Coherence is enforced only on them.
- *Negative bag*: the step set of a derivation of ⊥ from a designated context. It contains at least one invalid step.
- `\Alt(h^*)`: coherent uniform alternatives to the target, the "Kripkensteinian residue".

Contexts (Section 11)
- A *frame* is a top-model family M(λ) with named parameters λ. A context c denotes a filter of parameter points or models.
- The root context is @, or c_P for the problem's intended model.
- `\SUP(A)` is a supposition: exact discharge.
- `\DEF` is an idealization, given by a declared deformation of named parameters.
- `\ist(c,\varphi)` means "φ holds in context c".
- An export (bridge) carries a certificate, with tolerance ε.

Status tags
- Each theorem's source is marked with `\src{T1 Thm 3.1}` or similar, pointing to research/theory/*.md.
- Use `\leanok{Name}` if the result is formalized in lean/.
- Mark with `\status{proof sketch}`, `\status{conjecture}` or `\status{computed}` where applicable. Default is a full proof given in the paper or its appendix.
