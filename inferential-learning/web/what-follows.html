The question

Can a learner come to know which inferences are valid?

On an inferentialist picture, learning which inferences are valid is roughly learning meanings. The report studies a learner that acquires inference rules in three stages.

  • It imitates human inferences: formal proofs, informal proofs, physics olympiad solutions. A valid argument is a sequence of valid inferences.
  • It is conditioned on coherence: there should be no good arguments for both P and ¬P.
  • It receives occasional truth values from the world.

Arguments live in contexts, including idealizations that contradict background knowledge, such as “assume air pressure is 0”. The question is whether this can be made to work, provably, for formal mathematics, for mathematics as it was before formalization, and for physics olympiad problems. Behind it is a larger question: what does principled justification look like beyond mathematical proof?

All guarantees are relative to stated assumptions, chiefly that the target is realizable in a structured hypothesis class and that data and designations are truthful. Computational cost is mostly not addressed.

The three success criteria

What the report establishes

A setup that provably works for formal mathematics

Yes for classical propositional logic; conditional beyond

A two-tier learner (audit, then assert, with bold conjectures in a sandbox) is sound against every adaptive prover under its stated assumptions, and converges exactly for classical propositional logic. For complete decidable theories, exactness holds only conditionally on a realizability hypothesis that is not established for standard axiomatizations; without it the learner stays sound but loses some rules. For arithmetic it is Popperian, with sharp impossibility results beyond Σ₁.

A setup that would have worked before formalization

Conditionally

If the practice’s latent formalization lies in the learner’s class, a bounded-gap verifier over latent formalizations is sound against adaptive provers. For a finite class under complete presentation it identifies the practice’s step relation and brackets its meaning, which is identified exactly up to informal-validity equivalence when long inferences decompose into short steps. History violated the realizability condition at its decisive steps (ε–δ, uniform convergence, ideals). Affordable soundness when the learner must invent language remains open.

A principled system for checking physics olympiad solutions

A principled design with a soundness theorem, not yet a system

Solutions written in a Structured Physics Solution format are checked soundly against adversarial solvers, relative to a trusted base, the reading of the problem and the adequacy of the top model. That reading and top-model layer is provably ineliminable. The design is illustrated on one EuPhO problem by a problem-specific prototype. No general checker has been built. We deliberately built a checker, not a solver.

The main result, informally

Each signal fixes something and leaves a residue

The theorems combine into a division of labour. Every learning signal can refute errors in one direction only, so principled justification is a set of signals that jointly cover every direction of error, together with a declared residue.

Cautious acceptance is the canonical sound verifier

Report §4
  • Accepting exactly what every surviving hypothesis accepts is sound against every adaptive prover. It is the largest deterministic sound policy; a randomized δ-sound verifier gets anything outside it accepted with joint probability at most δ.
  • Its worst-case cost in human escalations equals a positive-elasticity dimension of the class: linear in step size for named schemas, at least ⌊(N−1)/k⌋k without citations, exponential for unstructured classes.
  • A Bayesian version is time-uniformly sound by Ville’s inequality, via the prior–posterior-ratio martingale of Waudby-Smith and Ramdas.
StepSoundness.thm_3_1_a · StepSoundness.thm_3_1_b

Imitation identifies rules, not norms

Report §5
  • For rules cited by name, Plotkin’s least general generalization identifies a schematic calculus exactly at a coupon-collector rate: N ≳ maxi (πiρi)−1 ln(kci/δ), and this order is necessary.
  • Exact identification generalizes to derivations of any size. Finite usage fixes inferential role for unbounded use, relative to the hypothesis class.
  • Systematic human errors are indistinguishable from rules at every rate.

Coherence is eliminative negative data

Report §6
  • A derivation of ⊥ from a context certified consistent is a negative bag. Only bold learners receive it; a halving learner suffers at most log₂(1/w*) detections.
  • Post-completeness. Every structural extension of classical consequence is classical or trivial, so one coherence datum pins classical logic with no simplicity bias. Substituting ⊤/⊥ turns any invalid rule into a derivation of ⊢⊥. For affirming the consequent:
⊥ ⊢ ⊥ (ax),  ⊥ ⊢ ¬⊥ (¬I),  ⊢ ⊥ → ¬⊥ (→I),  ⊢ ¬⊥ (¬I),  ⊢ ⊥ (AC)
  • Glivenko: coherence is blind among intermediate logics. In arithmetic it detects Π₁ truth but not Σ₁ truth, and runs out at Σ₂.
  • Carnap’s categoricity problem is, in part, Gold’s problem in the dual space of valuations. Single-conclusion data cannot fix the classical meanings; finitely many bilateral data plus one coherence datum can.

An end-to-end theorem for formal mathematics

Report §7
  • The naive design, cautious assertion plus a bold red team, is provably unsound because practice contains fallacies.
  • The corrected learner audits, then asserts, assigning blame through the union of minimal conflicts (Reiter’s duality). Under realizability, a frequency floor, truthful designation and an exact depth-d refutation oracle, with probability at least 1 − δ it is sound against every adaptive prover and red team.
  • Separate results show that positive data, caution, negative evidence, structurality, burn-in, depth relativization and truthful designation are each needed.
  • Arithmetic is Popperian. False Π₁ axioms get singleton blame; ¬Con(PA) survives under presumption of validity; no computable policy sorts false Σ₂ axioms in the limit.

Informal mathematics before formalization

Report §10
  • Informal practice is modelled as the shadow of a latent formalization. A bounded-gap verifier must check links between contexts as well as steps: Cauchy’s sum theorem is an equivocation.
  • Under complete closure-level data, validity is identified up to informal-validity equivalence and no finer; bounded-gap data only bracket meaning. Ontology is never identifiable: standard and nonstandard reals agree on the practice.
  • Counterexample objects need at most log₂|H| corrections; paradoxes can need |H| − 1, with a sorites chain as the tight case.
  • Frege → Russell → Zermelo comes out as constrained repair selected by coverage of practice. A robust-core theorem holds conditional on the notes’ robust-core hypothesis, which is untested here.

Contexts and the principled checking of physics

Report §11
  • Contexts denote filters of models from a declared deformation family. Suppositions are exact discharge; idealizations may contradict the background without explosion.
  • No truth-functional eternalist reading of in-context claims tracks in-context truth. Designating “idealization plus full background” as coherent forces a structural learner to drop some classical schema in every context: a formal version of the air-pressure worry.
  • No free export. Leaving an idealization needs information of finite radius about the de-idealized family (the adversary argument of information-based complexity). Context choice is an attack surface.
  • The Structured Physics Solution checker is sound against adversarial solvers relative to a judgment layer (the reading of the problem and the adequacy of the top model), and that layer is ineliminable. “True in the context at hand” becomes supertruth over admissible completions.
Two questions from Hänni’s notes

Simplicity, and a philosophical completeness theorem

Does a steeper simplicity penalty lead from imitation to norms?

Report §8
  • For function induction with private randomness the hoped-for kink at the simple model exists, proved for parity teachers. Kinks beyond parities are open.
  • Selection is a rate threshold: a component costing k bits that lowers risk by r is kept iff r/k > c.
  • Hence rate blindness (cheap frequent fallacies are kept, rare valid rules lost), rate inversion, guard erosion, and the steepness c is not identifiable from imitation data.
  • Simplicity is useful as one filter in the division of labour, not as the source of norms.
RateThreshold.rate_threshold · RateThreshold.prod_isMin_iff

“A mode of talking makes sense iff there is something it could be talking about”?

Report §9
  • In the generalized sense asked for, yes: for positions (bilateral Lindenbaum), credences (de Finetti with counting sequents) and context systems.
  • Coherence cannot certify the intended model. That needs categoricity, anchoring of the vocabulary to the world, or an infinitary condition such as the probabilistic ω-rule. No computable coherence notion certifies intended existence beyond Π₁.
  • Bounded-arity constraints of the kind used in consistency-based probing do not characterize coherence (a Specker-style counterexample). The limits of logical inductors are non-standard.

A bold learner over classical natural deduction accepted all 59 valid structural candidate schemas and refuted all 118 invalid ones, each by an explicit derivation of ⊢⊥ (median 6 steps for random schemas). A physics checker on EuPhO 2025 Theory 1(a)

The polished chair leg: sunlight reflected by a vertical specular cylinder of radius a onto the floor. The setup was reconstructed from Hänni’s notes and not checked against the official text.

In the top model the reflected illuminance is exactly E = I₀ac / (2s + ac), and at a fixed floor point it does not depend on the sun’s zenith angle, which the photograph leaves open. That independence is the completion-invariance certificate. A rigorous thin-leg bridge exports the answer E₀ = (aI₀/2)|sin(φ/2)|/r to an annulus a ≪ r < L tan α.

The prototype accepted the honest solution on r ∈ [20a, 34a] with certified error at most 8.88%, and rejected all twelve variants: overclaimed domains, side conditions evaluated inside the idealization, a narrowed reading, undeclared imports and disguised wrong answers. One rejection (a 5% tolerance) is a completeness gap, since the truth is within 5%. Plausible syntactic versions of the checks accept four of the attacks; only semantic checks reject them.

How it was checked

Refereed, repaired, machine-checked

Adversarial refereeing

Seven theory documents were split among two or three referees each, so every main result had one referee told to refute it, with brute-force computation on small cases where possible.

Round 1 found 4 fatal and 20 major issues; the fatal ones were false statements, and at least 8 of the major ones were counterexamples. Each was repaired, weakened or retracted. Round 2 found 2 more major problems in the two-tier theory, and 2 in glosses in one literature memo. A third referee re-checked the two-tier repairs and found no fatal or major problem.

Lean 4 with Mathlib

About fifty paper results in 17 modules, roughly 14,600 lines, with no sorry. About a third are formalized as special cases, weaker statements or variants, and the appendix map says which.

The axiom audit covers 483 declarations. Every one uses only the standard axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound) or a subset of them.

Code and paper review

The Python package cil has 85 tests and a 55-step quick reproduction script. An independent code review re-ran everything and fixed two reporting errors.

The draft went through a fidelity check of each section against its verified source, an eight-lens review of the whole paper and a final consistency pass, which corrected several overclaims in earlier drafts.

Philosophy

What principled justification beyond proof looks like

The setup is Goodman’s mutual adjustment of rules and inferences, made into a learning problem. Without world feedback it is narrow reflective equilibrium; with it, wide. The theorems give conditional reliabilist vindications of that process: under explicit assumptions, mutual adjustment is sound against every adaptive arguer, at every time and not only in the limit. They are not a non-circular foundation for logic.

Every signal is one-sided. Positive data never refute an over-generalization. Coherence never refutes over-caution, nor a coherent over-generalization. Coherence among tolerances never refutes one that is too wide. Demanding a well-posed problem refutes readings that are too wide and rewards readings that are too narrow. Each one-sided signal is an attack surface and calls for a complementary signal.

⊢

Proof inside contexts. Each step is checked against a calculus, formal where possible. Where the calculus is learned, acceptance is cautious, so the check holds against an arguer who searches for weaknesses.

⇝

Certified bridges between contexts. Leaving an idealization needs a certificate with information of finite radius about the de-idealized family. Side conditions are evaluated where they are true, in the parent context.

◇

An explicit judgment layer. That the problem was read correctly, that the top model is adequate, and that the trusted base is trustworthy. The first two are provably ineliminable. A principled justification minimizes this layer and declares it.

∀

Correctness as supertruth. For underspecified questions, “true here” means true in every admissible completion, to the demanded precision. Answer-invariance, which experienced solvers notice and rely on, is a certificate of supertruth.

⊥

Learning signals with known limits. Every learned component states the signal that trained it and the residue that signal leaves.

Mathematical proof is the limiting case: the judgment layer shrinks to reading the formal statement and trusting the axioms, every bridge is exact, and the calculus is fixed. Informal mathematics sits in between. Mathematical and physical justification differ in degree, measured by the size of the declared residue.

On meaning. Inferential roles are learnable exactly from finite use, through schemas plus a small set of telling instances; the skeptic’s “quus” survives only as a non-uniform patch or a named coherent alternative. More than role is not identifiable, and ontology is not. Rules can be identifiable when truths are not: the classical connectives are pinned by finitely many bilateral data, while no computable learner decides Σ₂ arithmetic truth in the limit. This bears on Dummett’s acquisition argument without refuting it.

Where philosophies part. Outside the residue of coherent, uniform, world-adequate alternatives, realist, Peircean, Dummettian, Carnapian and Quinean positions all count the learner correct. The residue is empty for classical propositional logic, contains every intermediate logic for an intuitionistic practice, contains PA + ¬Con(PA) for arithmetic, and contains the meadow in the algebra experiment. A theorem can say where the philosophies part ways. It cannot settle who is right inside the residue.

Limits

Open problems

Affordable soundness when the learner must invent language

Every guarantee assumes realizability, and history did not respect any fixed class: it invented uniform convergence, ideals, ε–δ and the fundamental group. A universal class restores realizability but costs on the order of 2K(h*) escalations. Needed is a class closed under the definitional extensions mathematics actually made, with small escalation dimension. This is the hardest open problem.

Computational cost

Bounds count samples, escalations and oracle calls, not computation. Version-space membership for untagged unions is likely NP-hard; refutation search is only semi-decidable.

Learning the reading

Which positive data identify the admissible worlds a problem text intends? Conjecture: official solutions with marking schemes form a tell-tale. Probably the central open problem for checking beyond mathematics.

Sharp combinatorics

A binomial conjecture for unions of intersection-closed classes; the exact exchange rate between paradoxes and counterexample objects; the query complexity of sound blame.

Coherence beyond Post-completeness

Which first-order and modal targets are coherence-pinned? Is there a principled learner of the right amount of reflection in arithmetic?

Simplicity and normativity

Kinks beyond parities; a data-free choice of the simplicity coefficient; assumptions under which description length identifies competence.

Scale

Whether cautious learned calculi stay affordable on real corpora is untested. We deliberately built no strong prover, and make no recommendations for prover development.
