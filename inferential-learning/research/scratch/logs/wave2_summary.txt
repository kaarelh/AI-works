This formalizes the user's (Kaarel's) proposal: function induction with private per-input randomness and loss lambda*K(h) + sum NLL, with lambda = cN.
- Two versions are given: an idealized one (K = prefix complexity, arbitrary computable hypotheses) and a computable one (prefix code on a countable class).
- The per-sample objective is J_c(h) = c*l(h) + R(h). Lemma 1.2 shows the empirical selection converges to the population c-optimum. This refines L11: separation with lambda proportional to N is asymptotic, not a finite-sample window. The obstruction is rate inversion, not sample size.
- Literature. The achievable (l, R) frontier is a product-model analogue of Kolmogorov's structure function. Set models are shared-randomness models, and their MDL function is flat ("1 bit pays for 1 bit"). Restricting to product models creates kinks; this is the precise content of the user's insight. lambda = 1/eta, so the proposal is exactly a tempered (generalized) posterior with learning rate eta < 1. With lambda = cN this is a fixed Gibbs measure that does not concentrate. SafeBayes-style data-driven choice of eta targets the teacher channel P* and cannot find the normative eta.
- Main results:

  - Shared-randomness frontier is flat (Thm 2.5(a)). For simple-model-plus-noise data, set/mixture models have constant total code length in model complexity, so every lambda > 1 selects the zero-knowledge class mixture.

  - Coherence repair by sacrifice (Prop 5.3). Without valid dominance, coherence-constrained MDL keeps a valuable fallacy and drops the valid rules that witness its incoherence.
  - Language relativity (Section 4.4 (d6)). For any finite component list, any target inclusion set is realized by some prefix code. The invariance theorem's additive constant is decisive at the 10-50-bit scale of inference schemas.

===== T6-philosophical-completeness.md
All "coherent iff there is something it could be talking about" theorems are instances of one picture. Sentences and models form a Galois connection, Mod ⊣ Th; this is the precise sense of Sam's "completeness is an adjunction".

Two slogans:
- Sam's slogan is strong completeness: the image of Th is exactly the deductively closed sets.
- The user's slogan is weak completeness: coherent implies there is a model. This is strictly weaker. IPC is weakly complete for Boolean valuations but not strongly complete for them.

Anatomy: for a finitary calculus, completeness holds iff every point (completely meet-irreducible theory) is realized by a model. Every completeness proof therefore has two steps:
- (i) points exist, by Zorn/Lindenbaum (free);
- (ii) points are realized by independently given objects (Henkin witnesses, Zariski's lemma, Carathéodory, or nothing at all for propositional valuations).

Step (i) alone makes every calculus complete for its own points (tautological completeness). So the proposition is substantive only when the semantic class is fixed independently of the calculus.

Instances developed with full proofs:
- (1) Positions. Coherent maximal positions are exactly the admissible valuations. Scott relations are dually isomorphic to closed subsets of 2^Fm, with explicit closure operators (overlap+weakening+cut; topological closure). This is Stone duality for the free Boolean algebra on formulas-as-atoms. Structural calculi correspond to substitution-invariant closed sets and are complete for their multiple-conclusion Lindenbaum bundle.
- (2) Credences. Coherent iff in the de Finetti polytope iff every valid "counting sequent" holds. CCS-style local constraints provably fail in arity and in multiplicity. They suffice on logically closed agendas. First-order: Gaifman coherence equals measures on the Stone space of complete theories. The Gaifman condition is a probabilistic omega-rule that pins Th(N), and every logical inductor's limit violates it. Finite VNM data: incoherent iff there is a money pump (Motzkin).
- (3) Contexts. A monotone multi-context calculus is sound and complete for local-models (belief-state) semantics. Coherence at the designated contexts holds iff there is a bridge-compatible family of nonempty local model sets; the canonical one is the grounded equilibrium. ===== T7-two-tier-coherent-inferential-learner.md
T7 combines T1 (soundness under search) and T2 (coherence as negative data) into one learner, TTL(d,δ), with one end-to-end guarantee.

The requested theorem is inconsistent as sketched, and how it breaks is the main finding. Positive data cannot tell a systematic fallacy from a rule (T1 Cor 6.5), so the realizable target of the cautious version space is the practice R*∪R_F, not R*. A tier cautious over that version space asserts every frequent fallacy (Prop 2.2). A tier cautious over hypotheses that may declare practice rules erroneous asserts nothing (Prop 2.4).

Three corrections make it work. Each is forced by a matching lower bound.
- Audit, then assert. Presume practice rules valid, but assert only after practice identification (burn-in N_1) and after an audit has removed implicated schemas (Thm 5.5).
- Blame. Coherence refutations are bags. The sound blame set is the union of minimal conflicts, by Reiter's hitting-set duality (Lemma 2.3). A genuine rule inside a minimal conflict cannot be asserted by any sound learner (Thm 5.6). The witness is practice {MP, AC} on context {q, p→q, p→⊥}: the rival diagnosis is that '→' means '←'. World feedback via descent through trusted steps (L6 TC1), or a single bilateral denial, turns bags into singletons. In CPC and arithmetic, computation refutes nothing that coherence does not; it adds localization.
- Depth relativization. Soundness is stated relative to R*∪R_{F_res^(d)} for a size-d refutation oracle, with search cost counted separately. Full-depth anytime soundness plus completeness is impossible for computable learners (Thm 5.7).

The architecture:
- Tier 0: per-tag trimmed lgg (mgu of the trimmed lggs).
- Audit: descent first, with a minimal-conflict fallback.
- Tier 1 (assertion): the audited practice, frozen at N_1, with abstention before then.
- Tier 2 (sandbox): oligarchic halving (OH) for conjectures. It is isolated: promotion happens only by derivation in the assertion tier, which cannot enlarge its closure.
