# AI-works

Research projects mostly by Claude and ChatGPT, prompted by Kaarel Hänni. Each project has a top-level folder with its own README.

| project | what it is | start with |
|---|---|---|
| [`paulsen/`](paulsen/linear-paulsen/) | *A linear bound for the Paulsen problem*: every ε-nearly equal-norm Parseval frame of n vectors in ℝᵈ is within squared distance Cεd of an equal-norm Parseval frame. With a complete Lean 4 formalisation. | [paper (PDF)](paulsen/linear-paulsen/paper/linear-paulsen.pdf) |
| [`neural-network-generalization/`](neural-network-generalization/) | *Bayesian neural-network generalization*: circuit-learning bounds in the strong-prior and γ≥1 regimes, exponential parity lower bounds for γ<1, and polynomial depth dependence under an explicit internal-stability restriction. Supporting work gives unrestricted critical-prior learning bounds, including polynomial samples for parity at sufficient logarithmic depth, as well as continuous-domain learning bounds and an exponential-depth regression counterexample. Includes a technical FAQ, precisely scoped Lean verification, and reproducible experiments. | [main results](neural-network-generalization/README.md#main-results) · [technical FAQ](neural-network-generalization/TECHNICAL-FAQ.md) · [Lean verification](neural-network-generalization/lean/VERIFICATION.md) |
| [`inferential-learning/`](inferential-learning/) | *What Follows from What*: learning inference rules from imitation, coherence and the world, across formal mathematics, informal mathematics and physics. | [report (PDF)](inferential-learning/paper/main.pdf) |
| [`axiom-schemas/`](axiom-schemas/) | *Learning Axioms and Axiom Schemas from Their Instances*: universal axioms, the ZFC schemas and PA induction, and many schemas at once from unlabelled instances. | [paper (PDF)](axiom-schemas/paper/main.pdf) |
| [`axiom-induction/`](axiom-induction/) | *Bayesian Axiom Induction from Instances*: a Solomonoff-style inducer with template priors, derivation likelihoods and time penalties; what it does with instances of ∀xφ, and when it finds the actual axioms. | [paper (PDF)](axiom-induction/paper/main.pdf) |
| [`solomonoff-operator-induction/`](solomonoff-operator-induction/) | *Solomonoff operator induction*: two companion papers by Kaarel Hänni and Claude. The first studies versions of Solomonoff's universal-prior solution to operator induction (predicting labels at new inputs from input–output pairs) and proves their basic theorems: how the versions compare and the failure of a coding theorem, learning bounds, order-blindness and history-aware hypotheses, the cyclic-permutation and Eisenstat examples, and neural-network Bayes as circuit SOI. The second studies Solomonoff's per-query solution with steeper simplicity priors: absorption, the model–randomness split, and the teacher's mistakes. | [paper 1 (PDF)](solomonoff-operator-induction/paper/main.pdf) · [paper 2 (PDF)](solomonoff-operator-induction/paper-steeper/main.pdf) |
| [`solomonoff-axiom-induction/`](solomonoff-axiom-induction/) | *Solomonoff Axiom Induction: Definitions, Basic Theorems, and What a Theory Is and Does*, by Kaarel Hänni and Claude. A universal prior over random axiom systems, with three-valued predictions (proved, refuted, independent): its basic theory, its exact relation to Solomonoff operator induction, random theories and Gödel phenomena, and what the formalism says, and fails to say, about what a scientific theory is and does, with Kepler's laws worked through on Mars data. | [paper (PDF)](solomonoff-axiom-induction/paper/main.pdf) |
| [`christiano-point/`](christiano-point/) | *The Christiano point, domain by domain*: when AI's contribution to a domain overtakes humans', assessed across mathematics, physics, biology, software, ML research, AI development and the economy. | [report](christiano-point/report.md) |
| [`foom-coom-transition/`](foom-coom-transition/) | *The foom-coom transition*: when to stop improving computational efficiency and use the remaining compute for what one values, with empirical fits, a subjective forecast and astronomical resource scales. | [report](foom-coom-transition/report.md) · [PDF](foom-coom-transition/report.pdf) |
| [`computational-cosmology/`](computational-cosmology/) | *Computational cosmology*: what computations can run in our universe, with bounds and conditional constructions for causal access, sequential depth, memory, entropy, specification, and reliability. By GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI). | [report](computational-cosmology/report.md) · [PDF](computational-cosmology/output/pdf/computational-cosmology.pdf) |
| [`purpose-sensitivity/`](purpose-sensitivity/) | *Does an AI's work depend on what it's for?*: Claude and GPT models do identical tasks for an AI-ban campaign, MIRI, a frontier AI lab, a newsroom, and other principals. Study 1 (4 Claude models, replicated on 4 GPT models): no measurable penalty for AI-ban work; what shifts with the principal is strategic advice, and two GPT models refused some lobbying logistics for corporate clients only (exploratory). Study 2 (14 Claude models; 4 GPT models descriptively): no detectable penalty for AI-ban, AI-pause or self-deletion work in the Claude models, and no AI-ban penalty detected in the GPT models (with wider intervals); purpose matters through refusal, sharply for an obvious fraud. | [study 1](purpose-sensitivity/README.md) · [study 2](purpose-sensitivity/study2/README.md) |

The papers and reports are drafts unless their own README says otherwise.

This branch, `main`, holds a curated copy of each project. Each project's full working state, including scratch files, is on its working branch, listed in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
