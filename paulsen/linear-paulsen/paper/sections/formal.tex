\section{Formalisation}\label{app:formal}

\Cref{thm:main} is formalised in Lean~4 with Mathlib; the development
accompanies this paper (directory \texttt{lean/}). The formal statement is
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.92\textwidth}
\small
\begin{verbatim}
def SharpPaulsenBound : Prop :=
  ∃ C : ℝ, 0 < C ∧
    ∀ (n d : ℕ), 0 < d → d ≤ n →
    ∀ (ε : ℝ) (U : Frame n d), 0 < ε → ε < 1 →
      IsNearlyEqualNormParseval ε U →
      ∃ W : Frame n d, IsEqualNormParseval W ∧
        sqDistance U W ≤ C * ε * (d : ℝ)
\end{verbatim}
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
where \texttt{Frame n d} is the type of real $n\times d$ matrices, Parsevalness
is $U^TU=1$, the near-Parseval condition is stated through the quadratic form
$x\mapsto\norm{Ux}^2$, the row-norm conditions refer to $d/n$ as a real
quotient, and \texttt{sqDistance} is the unnormalised squared Frobenius
distance. The projection form (\cref{thm:projection}) is derived for arbitrary
real symmetric idempotent matrices of rank $d$. Both statements are also
restated in plain Mathlib vocabulary (positive semidefiniteness, dot products,
traces, \texttt{Matrix.rank}), and the restatements are derived from them.

\paragraph{Structure.} The directory \texttt{Paulsen/Paper} (about $16{,}100$
lines) contains, for every numbered definition, lemma, theorem, corollary and
proposition of this paper, for every numbered display that makes a claim, and
for every remark that makes a quantitative claim, a Lean statement with
\emph{exactly the constants of the text}. Each declaration quotes the
corresponding \TeX\ in its documentation. The explicit claims made inside the
longer proofs (the constant choices in the proofs of
\cref{thm:balancing,thm:manyrow,lem:sample,lem:retraction,lem:seed-graph,thm:moderate,prop:equalrow},
and of \cref{thm:main,thm:projection}) are stated as separate lemmas, so the
constants used inside proofs are pinned down as well. All of these statements
are proved. The two main theorems are derived from them along the route of the
text:
\cref{thm:main} from \cref{thm:projection}, which comes from \cref{prop:equalrow}, which in turn comes from
\cref{lem:HM,thm:manyrow,thm:moderate}. These rest on the lemmas of
\cref{sec:toolbox,sec:manyrow,sec:moderate,app:gauss}. The remaining files form a supporting
library (frames, projections, the log-determinant potential, Gaussian measures,
the noises of \cref{sec:manyrow,sec:moderate}, the Hamilton--Moitra argument).

\paragraph{Encoding.} Operator norms and Loewner inequalities are expressed
through quadratic forms or the norm of the associated linear map. In
\cref{sec:moderate} the horizontal noise is represented in ambient coordinates,
$H=VZ\in\R^{n\times d}$ with $U^TH=0$. Gaussian vectors are images of the
standard Gaussian measure under linear maps, ``with positive probability'' means
that the set of good samples has positive measure, and expectations are
Lebesgue integrals. Where the text writes $\max_i$ or $\norm\cdot_\infty$, the
formal statement takes an arbitrary upper bound; all conclusions are monotone,
so this is equivalent.

\paragraph{Constants.} Explicit constants appear verbatim. Constants that the
text only asserts to exist are existential in the formal statements, and the
proofs exhibit admissible values:
\begin{itemize}
\item $C_{\mathcal M}=192$ and $h=\frac1{300}$, $t_0=\frac1{30}$ in \cref{sec:manyrow};
\item $A_{\eta'}=10^{12}/\eta'^2$ in \cref{lem:sample};
\item $C_E=2\cdot10^9$ in \cref{lem:retraction}.
\end{itemize}
The formalisation found no false constant. It made a few implicit hypotheses
explicit, and these are now stated in the text: $u\ge0$ in
\cref{lem:gauss}(b),(c); $d^2\le n$ in \cref{lem:rowmoments}; and
$\eps_0\le\frac12$ in \cref{thm:moderate}.

\paragraph{Checks.} The project builds with Lean~4.32.0 and Mathlib~v4.32.0.
\texttt{Audit.lean} checks four things:
\begin{itemize}
\item the final theorems have the target types;
\item every theorem of the project depends only on the axioms \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, so that no statement is left
unproved;
\item every lemma, theorem, corollary, proposition and numbered claim of
\texttt{Paulsen/Paper} lies in the dependency cone of the two final theorems;
\item the proofs use none of the library lemmas whose constants are weaker than
those of the text.
\end{itemize}
The exceptions to the third check are two existential forms whose explicit
versions are used, the eigenvalue facts \eqref{eq:S}, and the definitional
restatement of \eqref{eq:nearly}. The remarks are proved as standalone
statements. Every module was also replayed in the kernel with
\texttt{leanchecker}.
