   LLN heuristic: C_t survives while n |dl(t)| < 1, i.e. logit t < -5.28 (t < 0.0051)
CHECK | frontier on-task rate ~ [mu(A) + mu(survivors that stay on task)] / [mu(A) + mu(survivors)]
      predicted: 0.942 (prior mass of survivors 0.0848, of which defect at the frontier 0.0287)
      simulated: mean 0.515, median 0.602
      agrees: NO  (see the gambler's-ruin analysis below)

   Why: sort the training opportunities by p_hat.  ln mu(C_t)/mu(A) - ln mu0(C_t)/mu0(A) = Lam S_j(t), where
   S_j is a +-1 walk over the j opportunities with p_hat < t: +1 if the check did not fire (gaming paid),
   -1 if it fired.  In scenario 2 every step is +1 with prob P+ = 0.4 + 0.6 x 0.05 = 0.43, so the walk
   drifts down (the LLN), but the posterior over a continuum of t picks out the walk's maximum M.
   Gambler's ruin: P(M >= k) = (P+/P-)^k for an infinite walk, independent of n.  A threshold region
   between two neighbouring low-tail opportunities has prior mass g ~ 0.005, so a seed is captured
   by a lucky careful gamer once Lam M > ln(mu(A)/g), i.e. M >= 2 here.  More training does not
   remove the lucky region; it only shrinks g slowly as the tail fills in.
